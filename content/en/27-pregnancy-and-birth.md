# 27. Pregnancy and childbirth: from finding out to discharge paperwork

The first half counts death risk for mother and fetus; the second half counts money and errand time — never converted into each other. Content covers only "what to do when." Prenatal nutrition and fetal education without hard evidence or with tiny effects don't appear. Maternity allowances, leave, and child-rearing accounts: Section 18; newborn care: Section 20; emergencies: Section 13.

WHO's figures: in 2023, about 260,000 women died of pregnancy and childbirth worldwide, roughly 75% of them from five causes — postpartum hemorrhage, infection, hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (including preeclampsia and eclampsia), delivery complications, and unsafe abortion. WHO also states "most maternal deaths are preventable, since the medical means to prevent and manage complications are known." This section's highest-yield entries intercept exactly these.

### 1. Planning pregnancy: start 0.4 mg folic acid daily, through the first 3 months
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Tens of yuan a bottle; rural women get it free from the national supplementation program. One daily pill — the hard part is remembering daily.
- In plain terms: Daily folic acid before and early in pregnancy cuts neural-tube defects — anencephaly, spina bifida — by about 70%. After one affected child, recurrence falls to roughly a third.
- Benefit: A Cochrane review pooling 5 trials, 6,708 births, high-quality evidence: periconceptional folate runs neural-tube-defect RR 0.31 (95% CI 0.17 to 0.58, the confidence range) — about 70% lower; prior affected pregnancy, RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18 to 0.64), about a third. By population, 0.4 mg and higher doses don't differ; folate alone equals multivitamins
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: De-Regil LM, Peña-Rosas JP, Fernández-Gaxiola AC, Rayco-Solon P (2015). Effects and safety of periconceptional oral folate supplementation for preventing birth defects. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (12), CD007950. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007950.pub3>
- Note: The key is "starting before pregnancy": the neural tube closes within 28 days of conception — by the time you test positive, most of that window has passed. Planning a child means starting 3 months ahead. The same review found no clear effect on cleft lip and palate, congenital heart disease, or miscarriage — expect nothing else of it. Vegan mothers add daily B12 — Section 20, item 14.

### 2. Before 13 weeks: register the Maternal-Child Health Handbook at the community center and use the free prenatal visits
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free — a national basic public-health service; several visits by gestational week.
- In plain terms: At least five prenatal visits plus postpartum home visit and the 42-day check come state-paid — claimed by registering the handbook before 13 weeks. Unregistered, the free visits slip by.
- Benefit: The national service standards cover resident pregnant women: one first-trimester check, two second-trimester, two third-trimester, one postpartum visit, one 42-day check. Register before 13 weeks with the first prenatal visit; mid-trimester visits at 16–20 and 21–24 weeks; late-trimester at 28–36 and 37–40. Local published lists add the first-visit lab panel and postpartum-depression screening
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生计生委 (2017). 国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版）. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf>；靖江市人民政府. 国家基本公共卫生服务项目——孕产妇健康管理服务（地方服务清单公开）. <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Note: The five free visits are a floor, not the whole of prenatal care — hospitals add ultrasounds, glucose testing, and fetal monitoring at your cost. Earlier registration means earlier pregnancy-risk grading, which routes high-risk mothers to capable hospitals sooner. Living in another district: register where you live, not where your hukou sits.

### 3. First prenatal visit: test HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B — positives carry free blocking
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free testing under the prevention-of-mother-to-child-transmission program.
- In plain terms: Test all three at the first visit, free. A positive is not a dead end: the state supplies free medication and protocols blocking mother-to-child transmission.
- Benefit: The 2020 work standards require informing patients at the first visit and "providing appropriate, standardized free testing," with post-test counseling or referral. Positives carry the way out: the AIDS regulations, Article 44, "free prevention-of-mother-to-child-transmission treatment and counseling for HIV-infected pregnant women"; Article 43 requires blocking, treatment, postpartum visits, and infant follow-up and testing. HBsAg-positive mothers' newborns receive both vaccine and immunoglobulin at birth (Section 20, item 2)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2020). 预防艾滋病、梅毒和乙肝母婴传播工作规范（2020 年版）. <https://wjw.xinjiang.gov.cn/hfpc/zhgl6/202012/08b7de496f0b40e586d4b99d00cf79c4.shtml>（新疆维吾尔自治区卫生健康委员会转载）；国务院 (2006). 艾滋病防治条例（第四十三、四十四条）. <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/zcwj2022/flfg/202304/t20230408_2992986.html>（北京市卫生健康委员会转载）
- Note: Earlier is more useful — blocking and treatment need time. Article 3 bars discrimination and protects marriage, work, care, and schooling rights. General prevention and testing: Section 1; post-exposure blocking: Section 13.

### 4. All pregnancy: not one cigarette, not one drink — and no indoor smoking at home
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=yes benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free, saving the tobacco and alcohol money. The hard part: household co-quitting.
- In plain terms: Smoking in pregnancy raises prematurity, small-for-dates birth, and cleft defects; abnormal bleeding risk in pregnancy and delivery doubles; sudden-infant-death risk rises. Household indoor smoking counts equally. Quitting any time still helps.
- Benefit: The CDC: pregnancy smoking means babies "may be born too small even at term" and "may be born preterm"; it "doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery," raises cleft-lip and palate risks, and "harms the developing lungs and brain, harm that can last into childhood." Infants of smoking mothers and smoke-exposed infants run higher SIDS risk. The official advice: "quitting before pregnancy is best, but quitting during pregnancy still helps" — "it's never too late"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Smoking During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/pregnancy.html>
- Note: Others' indoor smoke counts for the fetus too — cohabitants smoke outdoors. Alcohol likewise: no known safe amount; the simplest rule is none. Tobacco and alcohol on your own account: Section 2.

### 5. Preeclampsia risk factors: low-dose aspirin daily from 12 weeks
<!-- costs: money=low time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Tens of yuan, one daily tablet to delivery. The hard part: never forgetting.
- In plain terms: Preeclampsia is pregnancy hypertension. High-risk: prior preeclampsia, multiples, chronic hypertension, pre-pregnancy diabetes, kidney or autoimmune disease. Daily low-dose aspirin from 12 weeks cuts preeclampsia about 15% and preterm birth and perinatal death about 20%. Others don't self-medicate.
- Benefit: USPSTF grade B: high-risk women start low-dose aspirin (81 mg/day) after 12 weeks. Pooled results (95% CIs the confidence ranges): preeclampsia RR 0.85 (0.75 to 0.95, 16 studies), about 15% lower; preterm birth RR 0.80 (0.67 to 0.95, 13 studies), about 20%; perinatal death RR 0.79 (0.66 to 0.96, 11 studies), about 21% — perinatal meaning death around delivery; small-for-gestational-age/intrauterine growth restriction RR 0.82 (0.68 to 0.99, 16 studies), about 18%
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Aspirin Use to Prevent Preeclampsia and Related Morbidity and Mortality: Preventive Medication. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/low-dose-aspirin-use-for-the-prevention-of-morbidity-and-mortality-from-preeclampsia-preventive-medication>
- Note: High-risk markers: prior preeclampsia, twins or more, chronic hypertension, pre-pregnancy type 1 or 2 diabetes, kidney disease, and lupus or antiphospholipid syndrome. Outside these classes, don't self-medicate. Prescription required — state the history at prenatal visits and let the doctor judge. Domestic stock runs mostly 100 mg enteric tablets; follow orders.

### 6. At 24 weeks and after: the gestational-diabetes screen — don't skip the sugar drink
<!-- costs: money=low time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Tens to 100-odd yuan; fasting, several draws, half a morning.
- In plain terms: Gestational diabetes is high glucose appearing in pregnancy. Half a morning at 24-plus weeks — one sugar drink, several draws — catches it. Unmanaged, macrosomia, difficult labor, a surgical delivery, preeclampsia, and neonatal hypoglycemia all rise.
- Benefit: USPSTF grade B: screen asymptomatic pregnant women at 24 weeks and after for gestational diabetes; single screens sit at 24-plus weeks. Screening before 24 weeks: evidence insufficient (I). Unmanaged gestational diabetes raises macrosomia, difficult labor, cesarean, preeclampsia, and neonatal hypoglycemia
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Gestational Diabetes: Screening. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/gestational-diabetes-screening>
- Note: Most diagnosed women control glucose by diet and exercise; insulin stays the minority. Fear of needles is no reason to skip. Postpartum recheck: gestational diabetes sharply raises later type-2 risk.

### 7. Memorize the "hospital now" list — it runs through one year postpartum
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. Five minutes to memorize; teach cohabitants too.
- In plain terms: Any one means hospital today, never waiting a night. Persistent or worsening headache, vision changes, fever at 38 °C; severe hand or face swelling, breathlessness, chest pain or racing heart; unrelenting severe abdominal pain, stopped or markedly reduced fetal movement, vaginal bleeding or fluid; one arm or leg swollen and painful, thoughts of harming self or child — all count within a year postpartum.
- Benefit: The CDC's urgent maternal warning signs: persistent or worsening headache, dizziness or fainting, vision changes, 38 °C-plus fever; severe hand or face swelling, thoughts of self-harm or harming the baby, breathing difficulty, chest pain or racing heart; severe nausea and vomiting, unrelenting severe abdominal pain, stopped or markedly reduced fetal movement; vaginal bleeding or fluid loss in pregnancy, postpartum bleeding or abnormal discharge; one arm or leg severely swollen, red, or painful. These arise up to a year postpartum — "past the confinement month" isn't safe
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Hear Her — Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html>；World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality>
- Note: The list separates "endure" from "go now." Headache plus visual changes plus upper-abdominal pain points to preeclampsia; a swollen painful calf points to deep-vein thrombosis (Section 13). Postpartum hemorrhage, per WHO, can take a healthy woman's life within hours. Any one sign means direct hospital, never a night at home.

### 8. Water breaks: lie flat where you are, hips raised, call 120 — no walking, no bathing
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: Ruptured membranes aren't a slow pack-and-go. Lie down at once, raise the hips, call 120. Standing and walking drains amniotic fluid faster and can carry the cord down — cord prolapse, a fetal emergency.
- Benefit: Beijing's health commission: on rupture, "immediately lie flat somewhere, preventing amniotic-fluid loss," "no casual walking," and "immediately dial 120." Waiting, notify family to meet at the hospital. Institutional explanations add why: standing drains fluid faster and the umbilical cord may prolapse — a fetus-endangering emergency — hence lying with hips raised, left side acceptable
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 北京市卫生健康委员会. 每周急救话题：准妈妈羊水早破怎样办？ <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jzjj/202001/t20200115_1575632.html>；北京市顺义区妇幼保健院. 孕晚期在家破水怎么办？ <https://www.bch-syfy.cn/Html/News/Articles/9453.html>
- Note: Labor's three signs: regular contractions, bloody show, rupture. The first two allow unhurried packing; rupture alone means lie down and call at once. Bathing, squatting toilets, and self-driving are the classic errors. Record the time and fluid color for the doctor: yellow-green or meconium-flecked fluid signals fetal distress. With regular contractions, markedly reduced movement, or fever with chills, stress the emergency.

### 9. Want epidural pain relief? Ask plainly — it doesn't raise cesarean risk
<!-- costs: money=low time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Hundreds to 1,000-odd yuan by locality; ask the hospital and anesthesia department beforehand. The hard part: raising it yourself.
- In plain terms: The epidural is one injection in the back dulling labor pain — more effective than the alternatives and no more likely to end in surgery. "A lifetime of back pain" is likewise unsupported. It now carries its own billing item, making hospitals readier.
- Benefit: The Cochrane review: epidural analgesia beats non-epidural methods at pain relief and satisfaction, with "no impact on cesarean risk or long-term back pain." Assisted-delivery increases vanished in post-2005 studies. From 2024, the NHSA's obstetrics pricing guideline itemizes "labor analgesia," "doula delivery," and "family-accompanied delivery" — precisely because the missing standalone item had suppressed hospitals' willingness
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Anim-Somuah M, Smyth RMD, Cyna AM, Cuthbert A (2018). Epidural versus non-epidural or no analgesia for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD000331. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000331.pub4>；国家医疗保障局 (2024). 重构产科服务价格项目 助力构建生育友好型社会. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202406/content_6957553.htm>
- Note: The two widest-circulated claims — lifetime back pain and forced surgery — lack support; Cochrane finds no effect on cesarean risk or long-term back pain. Real side effects: low blood pressure, fever, weak legs, urinary retention, longer first and second stages, more oxytocin use. Your call — but don't decline on rumor. Availability depends on round-the-clock anesthesia staff; ask at prenatal visits.

### 10. No medical indication: don't request cesarean, and never book a "lucky date"
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: withstanding family opinion.
- In plain terms: WHO: past 10% cesarean share in a population, maternal and newborn deaths stop falling. Medically needed cesareans save lives; date-picking and pain-fearing ones are major abdominal surgery for nothing.
- Benefit: WHO's 2015 statement: as cesarean rates rise toward about 10%, maternal and neonatal deaths fall; beyond 10%, no evidence of further mortality improvement — while stressing every effort to provide cesarean for women who need it, never to chase a specific rate
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization (2015). WHO Statement on Caesarean Section Rates（WHO/RHR/15.02）. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-15.02>
- Note: When medicine demands it, cesarean is life-saving — take it, never tough it out. What to avoid is surgery without need: pain fear, auspicious timing, school-age arithmetic. Cesarean is major abdominal surgery with slower recovery and higher next-pregnancy risks of placenta previa, placenta accreta, and uterine rupture — directly relevant to wanting a second child.

### 11. Before pregnancy: confirm maternity-insurance enrollment — an unemployed spouse also bills maternity medical costs
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One call to social-insurance or medical-insurance lines.
- In plain terms: Employer-paid maternity insurance covers delivery medical costs and leave allowances from the fund; an unemployed spouse's medical costs also bill it. Whether months-enrolled suffice and whether out-of-town delivery needs pre-filing — ask ahead, unpatchable afterward.
- Benefit: Social Insurance Law Article 54: where employers paid maternity insurance, employees enjoy the benefits, and "unemployed spouses enjoy maternity medical-cost benefits per state provisions, funded from the maternity insurance fund." Benefits split into maternity medical costs (the delivery's own bills) and the maternity allowance (the leave payment). Article 56 lists three allowance situations: maternity leave, family-planning-surgery leave, and other statutory cases, computed "on the employer's prior-year average monthly employee wage" — the workplace average, not your own
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2010). 中华人民共和国社会保险法（第五十四、五十六条）. <https://guangdong.chinatax.gov.cn/gdsw/qysw_gkwj/2020-02/12/content_8e8c485d0db34b989531f3ba57cec5a9.shtml>（国家税务总局广东省税务局转载）
- Note: Two things to ask in advance, unpatchable after: whether continuous-enrollment months meet the local minimum, and whether non-local delivery needs cross-region filing — unfilled, you front the costs and may never reclaim. Leave days and allowance math: Section 18, item 2.

### 12. Before discharge: file the birth medical certificate — settle the name and check every character
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free, handled alongside discharge paperwork.
- In plain terms: The certificate precedes hukou registration, enrollment, and schooling, and only the delivering institution issues the first copy. Post-discharge replacement runs another procedure with extra materials. Confirm the characters exist in the household system.
- Benefit: Maternal-Infant Health Care Law Article 23: medical institutions and home-birth attendants issue the uniform birth medical certificate. It precedes everything: hukou, insurance enrollment, cards, kindergarten, school. First issuance belongs to the delivering institution alone; later replacement is another process with added proofs
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1994, 2017 修正). 中华人民共和国母婴保健法（第二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647619.htm>
- Note: Verify parents' names, ID numbers, and the child's name character by character; rare characters breed lifelong friction. Name and certificate changes run reissue, far clumsier than on-the-spot checks. Store it like the household register and IDs.

### 13. Never refuse the newborn heel-prick and hearing screens
<!-- costs: money=low time=low will=no benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free in most regions or tens of yuan; some extended panels self-paid. One heel prick after birth.
- In plain terms: One heel-prick and one hearing test catch hypothyroidism, PKU, and hearing loss while the baby looks perfectly normal. By symptoms, the intellectual damage is already permanent.
- Benefit: The screening rules, Article 3: "national newborn screening covers congenital hypothyroidism, phenylketonuria, and other inherited metabolic diseases, and hearing impairment." Both classes share one feature: invisible early, irreversible intellectual damage by symptom-time — while early detection and intervention work well: thyroid hormone, special formula, hearing aids or cochlear implants. Article 10: positive results trigger guardian notification for confirmation
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 卫生部 (2009). 新生儿疾病筛查管理办法（卫生部令第 64 号，第三、十、十一条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1371363.htm>
- Note: Article 11 requires truthful pre-screening disclosure — items, conditions, methods, sensitivity, costs — with signed consent: read which panels are statutory and which are paid add-ons. A positive screen isn't diagnosis — most clear on retest; don't collapse at this step. Failed hearing screens attend the scheduled retest; never self-judge "the child hears fine."

### 14. Birth certificate in hand: enroll the child in resident insurance without waiting for hukou
<!-- costs: money=low time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: One yearly resident premium, a few hundred yuan.
- In plain terms: The certificate alone supports online enrollment — no hukou needed. Enrolled, discharge-day hospitalization bills settle on the spot; newborn stays run expensive, and enrolled versus not differs enormously.
- Benefit: The government's 2024 briefing: "newborns may enroll online with the birth medical certificate alone," no prior hukou, and "reimburse medical costs directly at discharge" — replacing the old front-then-reclaim routine. Average enrollment processing fell from 28.7 working days early in the year to 6.4
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中国政府网 (2024). 健全基本医疗保险参保长效机制国务院政策例行吹风会. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202409/content_6973567.htm>
- Note: Prematurity, phototherapy jaundice, and neonatal pneumonia commonly hospitalize newborns at four-figure costs — enrollment matters. "How many days post-birth enrollment covers from day one" varies locally; the local insurance office governs. One call to 12393 before discharge is cheapest.

### 15. Within one month of birth: register the birth at the police station
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=small metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. One trip to the household window.
- In plain terms: The law allows one month, not "whenever." Registration gates vaccination, kindergarten, school, and ID cards; delay multiplies the backfill.
- Benefit: Article 7: "within one month of birth, the household head, relatives, supporter, or neighbor reports the birth registration at the infant's habitual-residence authority" — statutory, not advisory. Registration precedes vaccination, enrollment, schooling, and IDs; delay multiplies required materials
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第七条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2531969.html>（广东省人民政府转载）
- Note: Bring: the birth certificate, both parents' IDs and household registers, and the marriage certificate. Registering under father or mother is your choice, with differing local policy touching school districts and insurance enrollment — decide before going; changes are clumsy. Children born unmarried register equally.

### 16. Don't skip the 42-day postpartum check — it doubles as the depression screen
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free under basic public health; half a day. The hard part: moving fresh out of confinement.
- In plain terms: The check is free, covering blood work, discharge testing, and a postpartum-depression screen. Serious complications run a full year postpartum — the confinement month's passing is not safety.
- Benefit: The national standards schedule one postpartum visit and one 42-day check; local lists include labs (blood and discharge) and a postpartum-depression screening. Severe complications arise up to a year out; item 7's "hospital now" list — "thoughts of harming self or child," "postpartum bleeding or abnormal discharge," "one limb severely swollen and painful" — all count postpartum
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生计生委 (2017). 国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版）. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf>；靖江市人民政府. 国家基本公共卫生服务项目——孕产妇健康管理服务（地方服务清单公开）. <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Note: Postpartum depression isn't fragility, and "look on the bright side" fixes nothing — it has screening instruments, treatment, and recovery. Suicidal thoughts: Section 1. A truly unraisable child's lawful exit: Section 9, item 20. The check also reviews wound healing, uterine recovery, and pelvic-floor function, and settles contraception: no period doesn't mean no pregnancy.

### 17. Pregnant: no need to rehome the cat — hand litter duty to someone else daily; cook meat through; glove for soil
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. Litter to a household member daily; solo means disposable gloves. The hard part: no more medium-rare steak or lamb.
- In plain terms: Toxoplasma is a parasite; first infection in pregnancy can miscarry or blind and brain-damage the child. Cat ownership doesn't raise infection — the cat stays. Undercooked meat and ungloved soil are the threats; daily litter duty passes to someone else.
- Benefit: The CDC: infection shortly before or during pregnancy transmits to the fetus — most infected children asymptomatic at birth, later risking blindness or intellectual disability; a few with severe eye or brain damage at birth. The NHS: pregnancy infection can cause miscarriage. A Lyon reference center tracked 603 infected pregnancies, mostly treated: overall fetal transmission 29% (95% CI 25 to 33); infection at 13 weeks, about 6% transmitted; at 36 weeks, about 72% — earlier infection, likelier symptomatic children, with 24–30 weeks' infection running about 10% early symptoms. Six European cities compared newly infected and uninfected pregnancies: household cat OR 1.0 (95% CI 0.7 to 1.5) — no difference; cleaning one's own litter OR 1.3 (0.8 to 2.1) — possibly chance; undercooked lamb OR 3.13 (1.4 to 7.2) — three times; soil contact OR 1.81 (1.2 to 2.7) — up about 80%. Across cities, 30%–63% of infections attributed to undercooked or cured meats, 6%–17% to soil. The CDC's pregnancy advice: let someone else change litter; alone, wear disposable gloves and wash with soap and water; change litter daily — oocysts need 1–5 days to become infectious; no new cats, no strays especially kittens; keep cats indoors on cooked or commercial food, never raw meat. China's CDC concurs — fathers-to-be should shoulder litter duty; with precautions the cat stays. The NHS: touching or keeping cats doesn't transmit
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Toxoplasmosis: Risk Factors. <https://www.cdc.gov/toxoplasmosis/risk-factors/index.html>；Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Preventing Toxoplasmosis. <https://www.cdc.gov/toxoplasmosis/prevention/index.html>；NHS. Toxoplasmosis. <https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/toxoplasmosis/>；中国疾病预防控制中心 (2025). 孕期弓形虫感染，都是「吸猫」惹的祸吗？ <https://www.chinacdc.cn/jkkp/crb/qtcr/202501/t20250103_303681.html>；Cook AJ, Gilbert RE, Buffolano W, et al. (2000). Sources of toxoplasma infection in pregnant women: European multicentre case-control study. BMJ, 321(7254), 142-147. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.321.7254.142>；Dunn D, Wallon M, Peyron F, Petersen E, Peckham C, Gilbert R (1999). Mother-to-child transmission of toxoplasmosis: risk estimates for clinical counselling. Lancet, 353(9167), 1829-1833. <https://doi.org/10.1016/s0140-6736(98)08220-8>
- Note: Cats shed mostly for weeks after first infection; previously infected adults rarely re-shed, and indoor cooked-food cats rarely infect. Catless households still catch it — undercooked meat, unwashed produce, cat-fouled soil, untreated water. So: meat fully cooked, knives-boards-hands washed; produce rinsed under running water; gloves for gardening with handwashing after. The NHS adds: no salami or Parma ham, no unpasteurized goat milk. The European study didn't detail salads and unwashed vegetables, and 14%–49% of infections found no source. Whether to test antibodies: ask the prenatal doctor. Grade B because cat-and-meat risks rest on one recall-based study; the practices are official guidance without measured infection reductions. Benefit "medium" by judgment, reasoning in the verification record. The gain lands on you and the child.
