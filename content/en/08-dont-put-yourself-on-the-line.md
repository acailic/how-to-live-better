# 8. Don't put yourself on the line: law and property safety

This section counts money and personal freedom, not lifespan. Three things: what the law makes you liable for, how following it cuts what you pay, and what procedural rights you hold when things go wrong. Article numbers are verified against official texts. Ordering: biggest one-time loss with least effort first; advance arrangements middle; experience-only, no official backing, last.

Entries in this section are grouped by theme, with item numbers in parentheses.

**Anti-fraud**: the moment you spot fraud, demand payment stop (item 2), hard anti-fraud rules (item 3), a face on video isn't verification (item 4), never lend bank or phone cards (item 8), free credit reports (item 9), scammed online or secondhand (item 22), ask the price before door-to-door repairs (item 27), never carry things for strangers (item 29), "just scan your face for me" is AB-loan fraud (item 45).

**Signing, lending, debt**: read the whole page before signing (item 17), write a proper IOU (item 18), rights enforcement has deadlines (item 19), report assets truthfully under enforcement (item 20), the deadbeat list (item 21), never be a nominee legal representative (item 28), a family member's gambling debts (item 44).

**Conflict and grievance**: call police at the first sign of conflict (item 10), you may fight back against unavoidable attack (item 11), don't settle scores over a grudge (item 12), never strike uninvolved people (item 13), when "take them with me" thoughts appear (item 14), when someone near you says "nobody gets to be okay" (item 15), the insure-first-then-attack road is closed (item 38).

**Love and marriage**: think before delivering large gifts (item 23), betrothal gifts by transfer (item 24), write premarital property down (item 25), verify marital status before marrying (item 26), domestic violence: call police first (item 43).

**Police, accusation, evidence**: lawyer first when summoned (item 5), volunteer to explain after an incident (item 6), when falsely reported (item 33), insufficient evidence means acquittal (item 34), state compensation (item 35), demand a case-filing receipt when reporting (item 39), never bribe case handlers (item 40), record conversations that may turn hostile (item 41), photograph the wide scene first (item 42).

**Online speech and mobbing**: no insults, no rumors online (item 16), turn on protection when mobbed (item 37).

**Sex and extortion**: no sex with someone whose consciousness is impaired (item 31), sextortion: go straight to police (item 32), victims claiming compensation: never meet the other side alone (item 36).

**Driving, dogs, burglary**: traffic accident: stop, save, report — never flee (item 1), drinking means no steering wheel (item 7), leash your dog (item 30), fit good locks (item 46).

### 1. In a traffic accident: stop, help the injured, call police — never drive off
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Stop, hazard lights, help, call police — waiting for traffic police costs at most an hour or two. Minor accidents without injuries or factual disputes: photograph first, note the other party's name, phone, plate, license number, insurance certificate number, and impact points; both sign, then clear the scene and negotiate.
- In plain terms: Flee, and the accident defaults to your full fault — license revoked for life, never testable again. The practical layer: own-damage and third-party coverage never pay for hit-and-run; hundreds of thousands come out of your pocket. Staying, helping, reporting costs an hour or two.
- Benefit: The fleeing party bears full liability; only proof of the other side's fault reduces it. Fleeing also revokes the license permanently. If the accident constitutes the crime of traffic offense, fleeing draws 3–7 years; if fleeing causes death, 7+. Insurance pays nothing: commercial own-damage and third-party clauses exclude "traffic-offense flight" and "deliberately destroying, fabricating the scene, or destroying evidence" — the hundreds of thousands are yours alone (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 道路交通安全法（2021 年修订，第七十、一百零一条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html>（北京市公安交通管理局转载）；国务院 (2017). 道路交通安全法实施条例（国务院令第 405 号，2017 年修订，第八十六、九十二条）. <http://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=75>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第一百三十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；中国保险行业协会 (2020). 机动车商业保险示范条款（2020 版）（第九、二十二条）. <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Note: With injuries: save first, then report. Moving the scene to save people: mark the original positions of vehicles and people first. "Bumped and felt nothing, drove on" can later count as flight — when unsure, call police. The insurance clause cites the model terms — an industry template; your actual policy governs.

### 2. The moment you realize you've been scammed, call 110 or 96110 and demand a payment stop — don't investigate first
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One call stating the transfer time, amount, and counterparty account. Keep transfer screenshots and chats.
- In plain terms: The brief window right after money leaves is the only one. Police can emergency-stop and fast-freeze; banks must comply. Once the money hops and scatters, recovery is basically gone. So call 110 or 96110 first — not your own inquiry. 96110 calling in is police warning you: answer.
- Benefit: Police may conduct instant queries, emergency stops, and fast freezes on involved funds; banks and payment institutions must comply. Frozen funds return to victims by procedure. Once transferred and dispersed, recovery odds collapse — the earlier the stop, the better. 96110 is the national unified anti-fraud warning line; incoming calls are police dissuasion — answer them (national, since December 2022)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第二十、三十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；福建省公安厅 (2023). 96110 来电，请务必接听. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm>（省级公安机关页面）
- Note: Anyone claiming to be police, prosecutors, or courts and telling you to move money to a "safe account" is a scam. The law establishes the stop-and-freeze system; how much returns depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report. The "National Anti-Fraud Center" app and 96110 descriptions come from the Fujian provincial page; the MPS original was unreachable.

### 3. Memorize the hard anti-fraud rules: distrust callers, disclose nothing, click no links, verify transfers — the seven commonest scams share this shape
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Memorize the "three no's, one more"; read the seven types below; install the "National Anti-Fraud Center" app; always answer 96110. Anything asking you to transfer, share your screen, install unknown apps, click links, or read verification codes: hang up first, then verify via official numbers. The hard part: they rush you — resist and hang up anyway.
- In plain terms: Anything asking for transfers, screen shares, unknown app installs, link clicks, or verification codes: hang up, then call back on official numbers to verify. The seven commonest — task-brushing rebates, fake investment schemes, online loans, fake customer service, fake police/prosecutors/courts, pig-butchering, sexual-bait task scams — all want your money first. Recognizing them beforehand is far cheaper than chasing money afterward.
- Benefit: The law requires government to publicize telecom-fraud methods. Police, with finance, telecom, and cyberspace authorities, run warning-and-dissuasion systems; 96110 is the national line — answer it. MPS's June 2026 briefing: the top 10 scam types account for 85% of cases; task-brushing rebates lead incidence at 25%; fake investment schemes lead losses at about 40%. Fujian police's type-by-type markers: task-brushing — "anyone inviting you to make money together mostly wants to make money off you," principal never refunded until endless tasks; fake investments — shills post gains in groups, small rebates first, then bigger asks, withdrawal "requires taxes"; online loans — "real loans never require deposits or flow-making; if asked, it's fake"; fake customer service — claims you enrolled in auto-charging memberships affecting credit, demanding transfer to cancel; fake police/courts — "no 'safe account' exists; any 'police' handling cases by phone, QQ, WeChat, or internet is fraud"; pig-butchering — "never trust a 'lover' you've never met except in photos or video," never video-chatting or meeting yet always with money emergencies; sexual-bait tasks — dates preceded by "tasks," payments mounting. Recognition beforehand beats post-hoc stops; recovery depends on money still sitting in accounts at report time — item 2 (national, since December 2022)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第八、二十、三十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；公安部 (2026). 公安部发布会：通报当前电信网络诈骗犯罪的最新形势、特点提示防范电信网络诈骗要点（2026 年 6 月 12 日）：「以上这10类案件就占了全部电诈案件的85%。其中，刷单返利诈骗发案最高，占所有电诈案件的25%；虚假网络投资理财诈骗损失最高，约占全部电诈案件损失的40%」. <https://www.mps.gov.cn/n2254536/n2254544/n2254552/n10491781/index.html>；福建省公安厅 (2023). 96110 来电，请务必接听. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm>（省级公安机关页面，含「来电不轻信，信息不透露，链接不点击，转账多核实」口诀及「警惕 192 开头的诈骗电话」提示）；福建省公安厅 (2023 到 2024). 识骗技巧专栏：刷单 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202307/t20230703_6196881.htm>；投资理财 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202312/t20231220_6362145.htm>；贷款 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202312/t20231207_6326741.htm>；冒充客服 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202310/t20231020_6279505.htm>；冒充公检法 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202309/t20230913_6255863.htm>；杀猪盘 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202308/t20230818_6232030.htm>；色诱刷单 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202403/t20240307_6410612.htm>
- Note: Anyone claiming to be police, customer service, a boss, or platform risk control asking you to "cooperate with investigation," "cancel membership," "brush tasks for rebates," or share "investment tips" runs one script. Real loans need no deposits; police have no safe accounts — those two sentences block half of everything. Grade B because the mnemonic is official propaganda without measured prevention rates. The mnemonic and seven-type breakdown come from a provincial page. The MPS's other top-ten types — fake e-commerce logistics, loan-credit, game trading, online dating, fake bosses and acquaintances, ticket rebooking — all end in "transfer money." An acquaintance asking you to "face-scan for a loan" — no transfer, but debt on you — is item 45. Agency-operation scams in business: Section 12, item 24.

### 4. A face on video or a voice on the phone isn't verification — for anything involving transfers, hang up and call back on the old number in your own contacts
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One extra call, a few extra minutes. Post fewer full-face videos and long voice clips on public accounts. The hard part: they rush you — don't transfer on the spot.
- In plain terms: Seeing a face, hearing a voice — none of it verifies. Anything involving transfers: hang up, call back on the old number stored in your contacts, or ask a mutual acquaintance. "Bad signal," "in a meeting," refusing live video — treat as fraud.
- Benefit: MPS cybersecurity gave three methods. Motion verification: ask for a continuous head turn or a full palm-cover of the face quickly removed — "current AI easily stutters, blurs, or distorts simulating these complex occlusions and lighting shifts." Voice details: "AI-generated speech sometimes lacks natural breathing pauses and emotional contour, or carries slight mechanicality, abnormal background noise." Multi-channel verification — "the core move": "hang up and dial the number you know to be theirs (never redial the suspicious incoming number), or verify sideways through a mutual third party." The mnemonic: "video and voice deserve no trust; motion-verify and cross-check." Legally, face- and voice-swapping is banned for fraud; face generation, face replacement, synthesized speech, and voice imitation must carry prominent labels on platforms, and no one may maliciously delete, alter, forge, or hide the label. But compliant parties are platforms — scammers don't label, so "no AI mark seen" can't prove a real person. MPS criminal investigation published a case: a 14-person ring caught with 50,000-odd synthetic dynamic face videos and 500,000-odd personal data records; 60 cloud servers seized (national; labeling duty since 2025-09-01)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 公安部网安局 (2025). 眼见≠为实，警惕 AI 新型诈骗！<http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202509/t20250922_7013008.htm>（福建省公安厅转载）；国家互联网信息办公室、工业和信息化部、公安部 (2022). 互联网信息服务深度合成管理规定（第六、十六、十七条，2023 年 1 月 10 日施行）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-12/12/content_5731431.htm>；国家互联网信息办公室、工业和信息化部、公安部、国家广播电视总局 (2025). 人工智能生成合成内容标识办法（第十条，2025 年 9 月 1 日施行）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm>；公安部刑侦局、央视新闻 (2026). 警方查获 5 万多条合成动态人脸视频 谁在「复制」你的脸？<http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202603/t20260330_7117146.htm>（福建省公安厅转载）
- Note: Call back on the old number in your contacts — never redial the incoming display, never use a fresh number sent in chat. "Bad signal" or "in a meeting" refusals of live video or the motion test: treat as fraud. Motion verification is auxiliary — AI keeps improving; only the third method, cross-checking, is stable. Already transferred: item 2 (call 110 for a stop). Preventing your face and voice from being harvested: Section 14. Grade B because officials give methods and cases, not measured fraud-rate drops. The MPS site returned 521s again; originals taken from Fujian reprints.

### 5. Accused or summoned: lawyer first — no private settlements, no deleting records
<!-- costs: money=high time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Attorney fees from a few thousand yuan; the destitute get legal-aid lawyers free. In questioning, answer only case-related questions; leave the rest to counsel. The hard part: staying calm rather than explaining yourself in panic.
- In plain terms: From the first questioning, you may retain a lawyer. When your lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center arranges it within 48 hours, unmonitored. Questions unrelated to the case: you may decline.
- Benefit: From the first interrogation or compulsory measure (loss of liberty), you may retain counsel. During police investigation, only lawyers may serve as defenders. Requested meetings: within 48 hours, unmonitored. No one may be forced to prove their own guilt. Unrelated questions may be refused. Summons or compulsory appearance: 12 hours max, 24 for major complex cases; no back-to-back summons amounting to disguised detention. Fabricating facts to falsely accuse another of criminal liability: up to 3 years; serious consequences, 3–10. Detained or arrested, then case withdrawn, not prosecuted, or acquitted: state compensation applies, paid per day at the prior year's national average daily wage (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十五、三十九、五十二、一百一十九、一百二十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百四十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2012). 国家赔偿法（2012 年修正，第十七、三十三条）. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html>（国家统计局转载）
- Note: Don't privately seek the other party or their family — any "settlement" can read as admission. Chats, calls, and travel records are your proof of innocence; deleting them destroys your own evidence. Misremembering or reporting something later unproven isn't false accusation. This entry applies to the accused of any sex. The three expansions: items 33 (pursuing the fabricator), 34 (insufficient evidence means acquittal, plus retrial), and 35 (per-day state compensation after detention).

### 6. After an incident, go explain voluntarily: surrender cuts up to 40% of the sentence, minor crimes cut more or escape punishment entirely — "outlast the limitation period" is a dead end
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Turn yourself in at the police, or stay put and call, then tell it truthfully. The hard part: resisting "wait and see; maybe nobody investigates."
- In plain terms: Surrender cuts up to 40% of the sentence; minor crimes can escape punishment outright. Truthful confession after being found cuts up to 20%. Fleeing and denial buy nothing. Outlasting the limitation period fails too: once a case is filed, your years in hiding don't count toward the clock.
- Benefit: Criminal Law Article 67: voluntary surrender plus truthful confession is surrender — punishable more leniently, minor crimes exempt. Truthful confession without surrender (confession) earns leniency; confession preventing especially serious consequences earns mitigation. The two supremes' sentencing guidance sets the discounts, all below the baseline sentence computed from the case. Surrender: up to 40% off; minor crimes: 40%+ off or exemption. Confession: up to 20% off; confessing the same-category heavier crime unknown to authorities: 10%–30%; preventing especially serious consequences: 30%–50%. Guilty plea with acceptance of punishment: up to 30% off; combined with surrender, major confession, restitution, or compensation-and-forgiveness: up to 60%; minor crimes: 60%+ or exemption. Limitation periods run in four tiers by statutory maximum: 5, 10, 15, 20 years (Article 87). But Article 88: once procuratorate, police, or state security files, or the court accepts the case, evading investigation or trial escapes no limitation (national; sentencing ranges since 2021-07-01)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大. 中华人民共和国刑法（经刑法修正案十一修正的整合文本）第六十七、八十七、八十八条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181796a636a0179822a19640c92>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2021). 关于常见犯罪的量刑指导意见（试行）（法发〔2021〕21 号，2021 年 7 月 1 日施行）三（六）（七）（十四）. 甘肃省张掖市人民检察院转载全文. <http://www.zhangye.jcy.gov.cn/info/1039/2086.htm>；最高人民法院 (2010). 关于处理自首和立功若干具体问题的意见（法发〔2010〕60 号）一. 最高人民法院公报. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/f3089058d37e11cd16d801530934ae.html>
- Note: The beneficiary is yourself. Surrender is only mitigation — the crime still stands, the court sets the discount, and violent and drug crimes require strict application. Traffic accidents have their own arithmetic: staying, saving, and reporting count as voluntary surrender, but as these are statutory duties the leniency is strict; surrendering after fleeing still counts, only from the heavier statutory band as baseline — see item 1. Lawyer first and truthful confession don't conflict (item 5): the lawyer checks the transcript's accuracy, not your story.

### 7. Alcohol means no steering wheel — including e-bikes and "just moving the car"
<!-- costs: money=low time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: A designated driver or taxi runs a few dozen yuan. Agree before the gathering who stays dry. The hard part: pushing back against toasts.
- In plain terms: Drunk driving: 6-month license suspension and a 1,000–2,000 yuan fine; repeat offense, detention plus revocation. Driving while intoxicated revokes outright, no retest for 5 years, criminal liability for dangerous driving with a record that affects vetting and some careers. Insurance pays nothing for drink-driving. "Just moving the car" counts as driving.
- Benefit: Drunk driving: 6-month suspension plus 1,000–2,000 fine; repeat: up to 10 days' detention and revocation. Intoxicated driving: outright revocation, 5-year retest ban, criminal liability under dangerous driving — short custody plus fine (detention meaning short-term confinement). Commercial drivers face more: drunk, 15 days, 5,000 fine, 5-year ban; intoxicated, 10-year ban and lifetime commercial ban. Drink-caused major accidents constituting crimes: lifetime ban. Commercial auto insurance never pays for drink or drug driving (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 道路交通安全法（2021 年修订，第九十一条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html>（北京市公安交通管理局转载）；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（第八条，即刑法第一百三十三条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>；中国保险行业协会 (2020). 机动车商业保险示范条款（2020 版）（第九、二十二条）. <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Note: Intoxicated driving is a criminal record affecting vetting and some qualifications. "Drinking" versus "intoxication" lines follow the blood-alcohol national standard — this section hasn't verified the numbers; don't guess your band by feel.

### 8. Never lend bank cards, phone cards, or payment accounts to anyone — "money muling" isn't a side job
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. "Borrow your card to run some money, a few hundred for you" — refuse.
- In plain terms: Renting, lending, or selling bank cards, phone cards, or payment accounts is itself illegal: fines of 1–10× illegal gains, up to 200,000 without gains, up to 15 days' detention when serious, plus credit-record entries and account restrictions. Knowingly helping collect and transfer criminal money is the aiding-cybercrime crime — up to 3 years.
- Benefit: Renting, lending, buying, or selling phone cards, bank accounts, payment accounts, or internet accounts is itself illegal: confiscation of gains plus 1–10× fines; without gains, up to 200,000 yuan; serious cases, up to 15 days' detention; credit-record entries, account restrictions, suspension of non-counter services (counter-only banking). Knowingly providing payment-settlement help for cybercrime, when serious, constitutes the crime of assisting information-network criminal activity (aiding-cybercrime), punishable up to 3 years plus fines (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第三十一、四十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（第二十九条，即刑法第二百八十七条之二）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>
- Note: "I didn't know what they'd do with the card" doesn't shield you: investigators infer knowledge from circumstances — benefit fees received, abnormal flow. Cards used to receive criminal funds freeze even without charges, with personal penalties. Face-scanning or reading codes to pass identity verification for others carries the same penalties — item 45.

### 9. Check your credit report free twice a year for loans and cards you never opened
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Online, ten minutes.
- In plain terms: Twice a year, free, ten minutes — via the PBOC Credit Reference Center site or mobile banking, never third-party "credit check" apps (they harvest you). Adverse records last 5 years from the event's end, then must be deleted. Disputes must receive written answers within 20 days.
- Benefit: The regulation states "information subjects may obtain their own credit reports free of charge twice a year." Adverse information is kept "5 years from the termination of the adverse act or event; beyond 5 years, it shall be deleted." Disputes get written replies within 20 days of receipt
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2013). 征信业管理条例（国令第 631 号，第十六条、第十七条、第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-01/29/content_2303.htm>
- Note: Identity theft into online loans surfaces years later at mortgage rejection. Query via the PBOC center or mobile banking — never third-party apps. After "face-scanning as a favor" for an acquaintance, check especially: item 45.

### 10. At the first sign of conflict, call police — don't strike; whoever strikes first almost always loses
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Retreat to a crowded, camera-covered spot and call. The hard part: not talking back, not shoving.
- In plain terms: Hitting someone: 5–10 days' detention plus a 500–1,000 yuan fine. Group beatings, hitting elders, pregnant women, children, or the disabled, or repeat offenses: 10–15 days, 1,000–2,000. Light injury is criminal — up to 3 years; serious injury, 3–10. Mutual fighting isn't self-defense; the first striker almost always loses. Retreating and calling police beats swinging back.
- Benefit: Assault or intentional injury: 5–10 days plus 500–1,000 yuan. Grouped assaults, assaults on elders/pregnant women/children/the disabled, repeat assaults or multiple victims: 10–15 days plus 1,000–2,000. Group brawling is handled as picking quarrels — up to 15 days plus 2,000. Intentional injury constituting crime: up to 3 years; serious injury: 3–10. Mutual combat isn't lawful defense — but if the other side struck first with clearly excessive means, or you tried to escape and they kept attacking, counterstrikes are generally recognized as defense. Excess becomes excessive defense only with both: clearly exceeding necessary limits AND causing major harm. Even genuine excess should be mitigated or exempted (national; Public Security Administration Punishments Law since 2026-01-01)
- Evidence grade: A

- Cost: Free. Phone call-recording or a recorder app. Record start to finish, keep originals uncut and unoverwritten.
- In plain terms: Loans, debts, compensation, refunds, labor disputes — said on a call and left unrecorded, months later the two of you remember differently. You may record conversations you participate in; not announcing it beforehand doesn't by itself bar the recording as evidence. What you can't do: wiretap, peep into others' privacy, break into homes to record, or coerce speech.
- Benefit: Civil Procedure Law Article 66 lists audiovisual material and electronic data as evidence. The Supreme Court's interpretation, Article 106, excludes only three acquisition methods: seriously infringing others' lawful rights, violating prohibitive law, or seriously offending public order and morals. "Didn't tell them beforehand" isn't among them. The evidence rules, Article 15: audio and video evidence requires the original carrier — the phone itself. Article 90: questionable audiovisual and electronic data can't alone establish facts. A judge's article carried on the Supreme Court's site concurs: legally obtained, genuine content, uncut, case-relevant (national)
- Evidence grade: A

Get people, events, amounts, and times aligned on tape — not just "when will you pay me," but naturally, "the 50,000 yuan I lent you last year — when do you plan to return it?" ### 42. When something happens on scene: wide shot first, then spatial relations, then damage and injuries — keep originals, never delete
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Tens of seconds to minutes of phone footage. Safety first — never delay rescue or response.
- In plain terms: Close-ups of one scratch or one object on the ground tell nobody where it was days later. Shoot a wide pan first — intersection, house number, vehicles, people, surroundings — then relative positions, then details. Before moving anything, photograph. Keep originals on the phone, not just app-compressed copies.
- Benefit: Civil Procedure Law Article 66 lists physical evidence, audiovisual material, and electronic data; the evidence rules classify images, audio, and video as electronic data, requiring originals and original carriers. Photos and video freeze the soon-to-change scene, cross-checkable against police reports, repair records, medical records, and witnesses. The "wide, position, detail" order is practice, not statute (national)
- Evidence grade: C

### 43. Domestic violence: police first for the response record, then the court for a personal-safety protection order — no divorce needed, no fee
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=yes benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Protection-order applications carry no court fee and no security deposit. The costs are time: reporting, injury documentation, drafting. The hardest: opening your mouth against family.
- In plain terms: Of women killed worldwide, over a third die at partners' hands — usually the end of long abuse. Among battered women, those once strangled by the partner run 7-times-plus the homicide risk of those never strangled. So report, then apply for the order. Courts usually answer within 72 hours.
- Benefit: The risk first: pooling 66 countries (systematic review), 38.6% of female homicides are intimate-partner, versus 6.3% of male — the authors call these the final step of prolonged abuse. Another study compared 506 homicides and attempted homicides with 427 battered-but-surviving women: prior non-fatal strangulation raised attempted-homicide odds 6.70-fold (OR 6.70, 95% CI 3.91–11.49) and homicide 7.48-fold (OR 7.48, 95% CI 4.53–12.35). The law: victims and close relatives may report; police must respond promptly, stop the violence, investigate and collect evidence, and assist medical care and injury assessment; minor cases draw written admonishments. Facing violence or its realistic danger, apply to court for a personal-safety protection order — ruled within 72 hours of acceptance, 24 in emergencies. Orders may ban violence, ban harassment, stalking, or contact with you and close relatives, and order the abuser out of the home. Orders run up to 6 months, extendable before expiry. No divorce prerequisite; issuance requires only "relatively large possibility" of violence. Violations: fines up to 1,000 yuan, detention up to 15 days; constituting crime, refusal-to-execute treatment. Regular insult, threat, stalking, harassment, and deprivation of warmth and food also count as domestic violence
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2015). 反家庭暴力法（第二、十三、十五、十六、十九、二十三、二十八、二十九、三十、三十四条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7f9d60889>；最高人民法院 (2022). 关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定（法释〔2022〕17 号，第一、三、六、十、十二条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/366021.html>；最高人民法院 (2016). 关于人身安全保护令案件相关程序问题的批复（法释〔2016〕15 号，第一、二项）. 平原县人民法院转载全文 <http://qdzy.sdcourt.gov.cn/dzpyfy/393141/393283/1461545/index.html>；Stöckl H et al. (2013). The global prevalence of intimate partner homicide: a systematic review. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(13)61030-2>；Glass N et al. (2008). Non-fatal strangulation is an important risk factor for homicide of women. Journal of Emergency Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jemermed.2007.02.065>

Evidence accumulates: response records, admonishments, injury assessments, medical records, written promises, chats, recordings (item 41). Orders need no lawyer and usually resolve within days. Divorce and compensation are separate suits — 6+ months first instance, attorney fees your own. Domestic-violence victims get legal aid, and courts may defer, reduce, or waive filing fees. An order isn't a bodyguard: with immediate danger, call 110. Victims coerced or frozen may be applied for by close relatives, police, the women's federation, or residents' and villagers' committees. ### 44. A family member's gambling debts: don't rush to repay — gambling debts enjoy no legal protection, and loans taken for gambling aren't joint marital debt
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: not paying when creditors knock and family weeps. If they actually sue, you must answer.
- In plain terms: Table debts get no court protection. Lenders who knew the money was for gambling get none either. One spouse's gambling loans aren't joint debt. Without trial, courts can't make the non-borrowing spouse liable.
- Benefit: The Supreme Court's 2017 notice states four points: debts from gambling, drugs, and other illegal activities get no legal protection; lenders knowing or should-have-knowing likewise; loans in one party's name funding crime can't be treated as joint marital debt; and without trial procedure, the non-borrowing party bears no civil liability (national, since 2017)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 最高人民法院 (2017). 关于依法妥善审理涉及夫妻债务案件有关问题的通知（法〔2017〕48 号，第二、四条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/36982.html>
- Note: Grade B: an internal court notice without numbers, and lender knowledge is evidentiary. It predates the Civil Code — current joint-debt doctrine: Section 10, item 12. Loans the lender didn't know were for gambling remain owed — by the borrower alone. Proving gambling use takes transfer records, platform records, or police materials: keep them. If sued: 6+ months ordinary, 3 simplified; attorney fees each side's own. Creditors blocking or detaining people: Section 9, item 15 — call police. If the gambler has reached wanting to die: Section 1, item 37. Beneficiaries: spouse and immediate family.

### 45. An acquaintance asking you to "face-scan as a witness" for a loan, or to read out a verification code: refuse — this is the AB-loan; the borrower is you, not a guarantor
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: refusing family and friends to their faces. "Just witnessing, no liability" — refuse anyway. Don't hand phone, ID, or bank card to brokers; don't face-scan or sign on their arrangement; codes are yours alone, acquaintances included.
- In plain terms: "Help me face-scan, just witness" means borrowing from the bank in your name. The borrower is you — not a guarantor; once the money passes to your acquaintance, the bank still collects from you. The broker defrauded you, but the loan contract can't be undone against an unknowing bank. The acquaintance's "I'll repay" note binds the bank not at all.
- Benefit: The Fujian financial regulator's 2022 alert describes the scheme: client A fails qualification; the broker demands a "guarantor," and A recruits well-qualified B; in processing, the broker applies in B's identity, the money goes to A — "the borrower is in substance B," who "learns only upon collection that he is the borrower, not the guarantor." Shandong's 2024 alert adds brokers promising B "no responsibility whatsoever" and coaching B through the bank's pre-loan interview. Shanghai Putuo court's 2024 case: a broker's staffer downloaded the bank app on B's phone and applied in B's name; funds hit B's account, B forwarded to A, A paid 80,000-odd yuan in fees; the 8-person ring drew 10 months to 4 years for contract fraud — but the conviction doesn't transfer the loan. Civil Code Article 149: third-party fraud is rescindable only when the counterparty "knew or should have known" — the counterparty here is the bank, unknowing, so no rescission; what's rescindable must be sought within 1 year of discovering the fraud (Article 152). Borrowers repay per terms (Article 675); contracts bind only their signatories (Article 465); debt assignment needs the bank's consent (Article 551) — hence A's note binds the bank not at all, and recovering from A means your own suit. Face-scanned without reading the contract still binds: Neijiang's Shizhong court, 2025 — Jiang couldn't qualify alone; Zhang's son supplied her identity behind her back as co-borrower; the process required her own face scan, she scanned without reading; the court held she "should have foreseen the importance and legal consequences of facial recognition," jointly liable for the remaining 60,000-odd principal. The Electronic Signature Law: "reliable electronic signatures have equal legal force with handwritten signatures or seals." The Anti-Telecom-Fraud Law: no one "may provide real-name verification help" — violations draw fines, serious cases detention. Ordinary first instance concludes within 6 months, extendable; attorney fees are your own (national)

### 46. Fit good locks on doors and windows, a motion light outside, and actually lock up: these prevent burglary — a lone alarm shows no effect
<!-- costs: money=low time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Key-deadbolt door locks, window locks, and an outside motion light run tens to hundreds of yuan; a full security door, over a thousand. The hard part: actually locking up every departure, open-window summers included.
- In plain terms: In UK data, homes with door and window locks ran one-twelfth the burglary risk of unprotected ones; adding an outside light cut it to one-thirtieth-plus. An alarm alone showed no protective effect. This covers burglary — not home-invasion robbery with people present.
- Benefit: The England and Wales crime survey asked 37,416 households (2008–2012) about security devices and compared burgled homes. Unprotected homes were just 4.9% of all households but 36.6% of burgled ones. The authors computed "protection multiples" — burglary risk as a fraction of unprotected. Door plus window locks: 12.54×, about one-twelfth the risk (door locks meaning double-locked or deadbolted — bolts needing keys). Locks plus outside motion or timer light: 34.41×. Adding indoor timer lights: 49×. Single measures: outside light about 3×, door locks about 2.8×; window locks alone showed nothing. Five devices beat a well-chosen three not at all. Alarm-only homes burgled more than unprotected ones; dummy alarm boxes correlated with more attempted break-ins. And locks must be locked: 1993–2006/07, forced-entry burglaries (lock-picking, window-breaking, door removal) fell from 31 to 7 per thousand households, −77%; unlocked walk-in burglaries held at 7 throughout (England and Wales)
