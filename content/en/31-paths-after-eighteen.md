# 31. The paths after eighteen

This section counts money and time; the military-service refusal entry adds personal freedom. It's a route map, not one road's manual: the legal thresholds side by side, then the things you can only use by knowing — what service pays, the non-civil-service entries into the system, the two majors that lock staffing slots at college-application time, whether credentials can be backfilled, what to look for in a company for work abroad, social insurance without an employer, and the state-subsidized interest loans for starting without capital.

The paths after eighteen far exceed these; only those with national public documents — thresholds and benefits verifiable line by line — appear. State enterprises, factories, seafaring, freelancing, continuing to university: all walkable, none with unified documents, none written from impression.

Which road is yours. This section offers two things only: where thresholds are written, and which deadlines and qualifications expire. Study-versus-work and skill choice: Section 23. Overtime, injuries, severance: Section 19. Starting a shop: Section 12. Study abroad: Section 32. Unemployment protections: Section 7. None repeated.

### 1. First match the thresholds: twelve paths with age and education requirements written in black and white
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Ten minutes against the list.
- In plain terms: Working requires 16. Military service: males at 18, relaxed to 24 for bachelor's graduates. Civil service: 18–35 with junior-college-plus. Self-taught exams and sole proprietorship check neither education nor age.
- Benefit: Service per the Military Service Law: "male citizens who have reached eighteen shall be enlisted for active service; those not enlisted that year remain subject until twenty-two; university graduates relax to twenty-four, postgraduates twenty-six." Civil service per Article 18 of the recruitment provisions: "aged eighteen to thirty-five" with "junior-college education or above," adjustable with provincial-plus approval; Article 19 lists five exclusions — criminal punishment, dismissal from public office, joint-dishonesty listing. Firefighters: 18–22 with senior-high-plus; college graduates and veterans relax to 24. Military civilian personnel: 18-plus, entry-tier first-hire ceiling 35. Public institutions: society-wide open recruitment. Sole proprietorship: "citizens with business capacity... registered as individual industrial and commercial households." Self-taught exams: "citizens of the People's Republic, without restriction of sex, age, ethnicity, race, or educational attainment" — full-time enrolled students excluded. Government-funded normal students: six ministry universities only, integrated bachelor-master design, tuition and housing on central finance with living subsidies, early-batch admission. Targeted medical students: tripartite agreements before enrollment letters. Overseas labor: companies need commerce-department licensing — unlicensed organizers are illegal, and "no unit or individual may organize laborers to work abroad under the names of business, tourism, or study." Platform gig work carries no statutory education bar — insurance grounds in items 11–12. Employment: the compulsory-education law bars hiring school-age children, and under-16 hiring is banned outright (penalties in Section 23, item 1)

### 2. The year you turn 18: military-service registration by October 31; conscript service runs exactly two years
<!-- costs: money=none time=high will=yes benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free — minutes online. Actual service costs two full-time years.
- In plain terms: Registration is a statutory duty, not enlistment — registering doesn't mean serving. Males turning 18 register online by October 31; refusal draws correction orders, then compelled performance and fines. Conscripts serve two years.
- Benefit: The registration provisions: county-level military organs "shall, before October 31 each year, conduct initial registration of male citizens who reach 18 by December 31," primarily online self-registration. Refusal per Service Law Article 57(1): county-set correction deadlines, then compelled service plus fines. The law adds: "conscripts serve two years"; 17-year-olds may volunteer; sergeants "with twelve-plus years or meeting other state conditions" receive government-arranged work; thirty years or 55 brings retirement placement. The conscription regulations prioritize university graduates and allow enrolled students and fresh graduates to enlist from either home or school locality (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅、中央军委办公厅 (2025). 兵役登记工作规定（国办发〔2025〕41 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202512/content_7051363.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2021). 兵役法（2021 年修订，第二十、二十五、二十六、五十四、五十七条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm>（国防部刊登）；国务院、中央军委 (2023). 征兵工作条例（国务院、中央军委令第 759 号，第五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2023/content_5753313.htm>
- Note: Registration and enlistment are two different acts at very different prices. The two-year bans in item 3 punish only post-enlistment refusal. Females register voluntarily. Soldier-to-officer paths live in internal military regulations; the law says only that officers come partly from "outstanding active-duty soldiers" and academy admissions escape enlistment ages — no cutoffs or quotas here.

### 3. Refusing service after enlistment: two years barred from exit or re-enrollment, plus no civil service or state enterprises
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free — the price is deciding before registering.
- In plain terms: Registering then refusing costs a chain: no civil-service hiring, no state enterprises or public institutions, two years barred from exit, further study, or school return, and listing as seriously dishonest on national-defense obligations. "Sign up first, back out later" is an expensive idea.
- Benefit: Article 57(1)(2) — citizens refusing or evading enlistment — draws the same correction-then-compulsion-plus-fine. Paragraph 2: "those refusing correction may not be hired as civil servants or staff managed under the Civil Servant Law, may not be recruited or engaged by state-owned enterprises and public institutions, may not exit the country or resume schooling within two years, and are entered into the serious-dishonesty list for national-defense obligations for joint punishment." Joint punishment: multiple agencies enforcing the same list. Article 58 extends to the enlisted — service evasion, duty refusal, or desertion ending in discharge, expulsion, or criminal liability draws paragraph 2, discharge adding fines (national; since 2021-10-01)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 兵役法（2021 年修订，第五十七、五十八条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm>（国防部刊登）
- Note: This counts freedom and employability, not money. The chain punishes two classes — enlistment refusers and the discharged-or-prosecuted — never mere non-registration (item 2). Punishment presupposes "refusal to correct," county-determined; fine amounts are local and unwritten here. Decide before registering.

### 4. Student soldiers' two solid blocks: tuition covered up to 20,000 yuan yearly, enrollment preserved two years, dedicated master's quotas
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free — post-discharge paperwork, a trip or two.
- In plain terms: Enrolled students, fresh graduates, and new admits get their schooling years' tuition paid — actual amounts, junior-college and bachelor's capped at 20,000 yearly, postgraduates 25,000; cheaper tuition tops out at actual. Enrollment holds two years; master's admissions run a dedicated quota for discharged student soldiers.
- Benefit: The 2024 four-department notice: tuition compensation, student-loan buyout, and tuition reduction "adjusted to no more than 20,000 yuan per year for junior-college and bachelor's students and 25,000 for postgraduates" from fall 2024, executed at "the higher of actual tuition paid or the national student loan obtained (principal plus interest before full repayment)," amounts above the cap uncompensated — so yearly payment equals the smaller of the higher-of-tuition-or-loan and the cap; 5,000-yuan tuition pays 5,000. Years follow schooling, not service: "years completed before enlistment count as compensation or buyout years; remaining years completed after return count as reduction years," with graduates compensated across the full program. Enrollment: the Veterans Support Law — "admitted or enrolled students retain admission status or enrollment during service, may enter or re-enroll within two years of discharge, and may transfer majors per state rules," with universities admitting veterans through separate plans. Master's: the dedicated "discharged student-soldier plan" — quotas carved out nationally, not convertible, institutions setting their own score floors "generally not above the ordinary plan's," longer service preferred at parity, 532 institutions in 2026 (national)

### 5. Only six situations bring government-arranged post-service work; the self-employed must report within 30 days
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free — one trip to the placement locality's veterans affairs office with discharge papers.
- In plain terms: "Service guarantees assignment" stopped being general. Six situations trigger arranged work; two-year conscripts mostly sit outside. The rest is self-employment — with a hard deadline: report within 30 days of discharge approval, or forfeit placement benefits.
- Benefit: The Placement Regulations, Article 25, lists six: sergeants with 12-plus years; decorations and honorary titles; third-class combat merit or second-class merit or above; first-grade commendations; war disability grades 5–8; and martyrs' children. Local governments complete placement within 6 months of receiving sergeants and conscripts; employers sign "no-less-than-3-year employment contracts, enterprises receiving sergeants with 10-plus years signing open-ended contracts" — open-ended meaning no end date. Awaiting placement pays local minimum wage monthly; failure to place for reasons beyond the person pays no less than 80% of comparable workers' average wage until placement. Placement barred for those expelled from the Party or criminally punished. Self-employment deadline, Article 51: report within 30 days of approved discharge; "absence beyond 30 days without justification deems waiver of placement benefits" (national; since 2024-09-01)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院、中央军委 (2024). 退役军人安置条例（国务院、中央军委令第 787 号，2024 年 7 月 29 日公布、9 月 1 日施行；第二十五、三十一、三十二、三十五、五十一、七十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11526/202408/content_6969192.html>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 退役军人保障法（第十三、十四、十五条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/fgwx/flfg/16048597.html>（国防部刊登）
- Note: The same day repealed the 2011 placement regulations — most online guides still cite the old wording ("second-class merit in peacetime or third-class in wartime"). The 30 days bind only self-employment; other placement modes follow their own schedules. Reporting issues the veterans preference card, from which payments and support flow.

### 6. Three post-service accounts: one-time discharge payment, service years as seniority, and 20,000 yuan yearly tax deduction for the self-employed
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free — the military transfers your insurance and funds; you complete continuation at the local window.
- In plain terms: First, the self-employed one-time discharge payment by service years, augmented for merit. Second, service years count as seniority and social-insurance years, merging with pre- and post-service contributions. Third, self-employed veterans deduct up to 20,000 yuan of taxes yearly for three years.
- Benefit: First account: the regulations grant one-time payments "by active-service years," funded centrally, standards set and adjusted by the veterans and military authorities; augmentation in five bands — decorations and honorary titles +25%, first-class combat merit or first-grade commendation +20%, second-class combat merit, first-class merit, or second-grade commendation +15%, third-class combat merit or second-class merit +10%, fourth-class combat merit or third-class merit +5%. Unemployed veterans may claim unemployment benefits with service years "deemed contribution years" — paid-without-paying — except retirees and supported-placement veterans. Second: the Support Law — service years count as seniority, merging with post-service unit years; pension, medical, and unemployment years "merge by law"; conscripts' and sergeants' service "deemed grassroots work experience." The Military Insurance Law: service years deemed employee-medical contribution years, merging with pre- and post-service. Third: the 2023 tax notice — self-employed veterans deduct "in sequence from VAT, urban-maintenance tax, education surcharges, local education surcharges, and individual income tax, capped at 20,000 yuan per household yearly over 3 years (36 months), caps floatable to 20% higher" — deduction, not subsidy; enterprises hiring deduct 6,000 per person yearly, floatable 50%; effective 2023-01-01 to 2027-12-31 (national)

### 7. Entering the system isn't only the civil-service exam: two years' grassroots service unlocks ~10% dedicated recruitment
<!-- costs: money=none time=high will=yes benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free — the price is two years at township level on fiscal stipend, not market wages.
- In plain terms: Programs like "Three Supports and One Assistance" carry a written two-year term; completion with passing review unlocks dedicated civil-service quotas — provinces carve out about 10% yearly — plus +10 master's points within three years and exam-free adult-degree upgrades. The cost: two years on stipend.
- Benefit: The fourth-round program notice sets two-year service and directs: "provinces annually allocate about 10% of civil-service recruitment plans for dedicated recruitment of grassroots-program participants; county and township public institutions set aside posts for special hiring of qualified finishers... with no probation period." Finishers gain +10 master's initial-exam points within three years with priority at parity, junior-college graduates exam-free adult bachelor's entry, and — for those without prior work history — fresh-graduate treatment in recruitment, entrepreneurship, hukou, and study for two years, with service years counting as seniority from arrival day. Central stipends: 30,000 yuan yearly (west), 24,000 (central), 12,000 (east). The central opinion adds: qualified no-work-history finishers enjoy fresh-graduate policy for two years, and "provincial-plus civil-service recruitment, save special posts, draws exclusively from those with two-plus years of grassroots experience." The Western Plan runs 1–3 years through the Communist Youth League and three ministries (national)

### 9. Firefighter and military civilian posts: two more open channels with lower thresholds than civil service
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free — months through physicals, political review, fitness, psychology, and interviews, each stage filtering.
- In plain terms: Neither channel gates on junior-college-plus. Firefighters: 18–22, senior-high-plus, relaxed to 24 for college graduates and veterans. Military civilians: 18-plus, entry-tier first-hire ceiling 35. The shared price: political review and physicals filter hard.
- Benefit: Firefighters: Articles 5 and 6 — 18–22 with senior-high-plus; college graduates, veterans, and 2-plus-year professional firefighters flex to 24, urgently needed specialists to 28 with ministry approval. Article 7: public recruitment, mainly from the province's residents. Article 9's sequence: mobilization, registration, qualification, physical, political review, fitness and adaptability, psychology and interview, publicity, hiring.
