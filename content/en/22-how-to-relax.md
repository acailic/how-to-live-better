# 22. How to relax: entertainment venues and stress relief

The first half covers which entertainment money is wasted and where the exits are — money and personal freedom. The second half covers stress relief — energy and overall mortality. The two halves never convert into each other. Section 3 covered the first steps when mood sinks; here are the concrete practices.

### 1. Before entering KTV, bars, or escape rooms: locate the fire exits — locked or blocked means another venue
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. Ten seconds looking up at the door.
- In plain terms: By regulation, venues must keep evacuation routes and exits clear during business — no blocking, no locking, no barriers, no obscured signage. Internet cafés likewise. Failure is unlawful and reportable. Basement rooms, single exits, cluttered corridors: change venues.
- Benefit: This is the operator's statutory duty. The regulation: "during business hours, entertainment venues shall keep evacuation routes and safety exits clear, not blocking or locking evacuation routes and safety exits, and not setting fences or other evacuation-impeding obstacles on them," with "conspicuous directional signs on evacuation routes and safety exits, not obscured or covered." Internet cafés: "during business hours, blocking or locking doors, windows, evacuation routes, and safety exits is prohibited," as are open-flame lighting and smoking
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2006). 娱乐场所管理条例（国令第 458 号，第二十、二十一条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>；国务院 (2002). 互联网上网服务营业场所管理条例（国令第 363 号，第二十一条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2002/content_61788.htm>
- Note: Any one of these means change venues: basement rooms, a single exit, cluttered corridors, dead exit signs. These venues burn with thick smoke, narrow passages, crowds, and alcohol — escape windows measured in seconds. The reporting number must be posted on in-venue warning signs.

### 2. Demand the price list before ordering — venues must post prices; "play first, settle later" gets refused
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One ask. The hard part: asking prices to a face.
- In plain terms: Services and goods must be posted and the list shown on request. "Sky-high drink" disputes nearly all involve unseen price lists and verbal post-hoc bills. Request the list on entering, photograph it; request a confirmed order list; disputes mean 12315 on the spot.
- Benefit: The regulation: "entertainment venues providing entertainment services or selling goods shall post prices clearly and present the price list to consumers, and may not force or deceive consumers into accepting services or buying goods." Violations draw statutory penalties. "Sky-high drink" disputes overwhelmingly cluster where no list was shown and settlement followed verbal quotes
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2006). 娱乐场所管理条例（国令第 458 号，第二十九、五十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>
- Note: Practice: request the list entering the room — paper or electronic — and photograph it; request the confirmed order after ordering; ask prices on every recommended "special blend" or "fruit platter" first; on disputes, 12315 immediately. Never pay first and litigate later — payment multiplies your proof burden.

### 3. Someone passing "things" around the room: leave at once — harboring and providing aren't "helping a friend"
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Standing up and walking out. The hard part: leaving a full table.
- In plain terms: Venues may not sell, provide, or tolerate drugs — violations mean 3–6 months' closure, graver, license revocation. Personal use draws administrative punishment. Letting people "use" in your booked room or residence is harboring drug use — criminal. Unknown powders, tablets, or pods offered: leave on the spot.
- Benefit: The regulation bans venues from "selling or providing drugs, or organizing, forcing, instigating, inducing, deceiving, or harboring others' drug use or injection." Staff may neither use nor facilitate. Violations: confiscation, 3–6 months' suspension; serious, license revocation. Venues must post anti-drug, anti-gambling, and anti-prostitution warning signs with reporting numbers
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2006). 娱乐场所管理条例（国令第 458 号，第十四、三十、四十二条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>
- Note: Unknown powders, tablets, or e-pods offered for a "try" — leave, don't spectate. Your personal exposure outweighs the venue's. Personal use is itself punishable; letting friends "use here" in your room or residence constitutes the crime of harboring drug use.

### 4. No candy or snacks from strangers, no drinks that left your sight, no pods handed over
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Refuse what's handed over; a drink that left sight gets replaced.
- In plain terms: Today's drugs don't look like drugs. Synthetic-cannabinoid "high" vapes look near-identical to ordinary ones and get passed around bars, KTVs, and hotels. Tricking someone into use draws 10–15 days plus 1,000–5,000 yuan — and the tricked user draws 10–15 days too. Drinks off-sight get replaced.
- Benefit: Today's drugs don't look like drugs. Synthetic cannabinoids have been scheduled as a class since 2021-07-01; SPP-reported cases describe oils that "can be colored and flavored with food coloring," "essentially identical in appearance to ordinary e-cigarettes," hawked in bars, KTVs, and hotels with minors running nearly four in ten of that year's use cases locally. Etomidate entered the class-2 psychotropics list on 2023-10-01. Inducing, instigating, deceiving, or forcing drug use draws 10–15 days plus 1,000–5,000 yuan; criminal grades run to 3 years, serious 3–7, forced 3–10, minors aggravated. The deceived carry consequences too: use draws 10–15 days plus up to 3,000 yuan; lighter cases up to 5 days or 1,000 yuan; possible 6–12-month entertainment-venue bans; addiction means 3 years' community rehab; refusal or relapse means 2 years' compulsory isolation, extendable 1 (national; punishments law since January 2026)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 最高人民检察院 (2021). 江苏苏州吴江区：办案检察官揭秘披着潮流外衣的新型毒品. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202110/t20211011_531831.shtml>；国家药监局、公安部、国家卫生健康委 (2023). 关于调整麻醉药品和精神药品目录的公告（2023 年第 120 号）. <https://yjj.sh.gov.cn/qtgzwj/20230911/3b612c94e35b4b63abba314c972a2593.html>（上海市药监局转载，原文页 nmpa.gov.cn 在本机返回 412）；全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第八十四、八十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第三百五十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2007). 禁毒法（第三十三、三十八、四十七条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2520706.html>（广东省人民政府转载）
- Note: Not only venues — school gates, gatherings, long rides alike; candy, drinks, or pods all the same. Drugging often precedes robbery, assault, and extortion — no official statistics, experience judgment. Replacing an off-sight drink beats pursuing after the fact. Unknown substances in venues: item 3. Carrying for others: Section 8, item 29.

### 5. Script-kill and escape rooms: skip those in residential buildings or below basement level one
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. One question about the floor before booking.
- In plain terms: The 2022 five-department notice bars script-entertainment venues from residential buildings and below the first basement level. Dark, partitioned, flammable props, doors that open only by puzzle — fires there run far deadlier than in ordinary shops. Check the listing's address and floor; non-compliant means another venue.
- Benefit: The 2022 notice is concrete: "script-entertainment business venues may not be located in residential buildings or below the first basement floor (the first basement floor excluded)," with venues implementing the safety and fire laws, "routinely knowing, checking, and correcting fire risks, and improving emergency evacuation and incipient-fire response." The same notice requires age advisories on scripts, bars minors from unsuitable scenarios, and restricts weekday provision to minors
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 文化和旅游部、公安部、住房和城乡建设部、应急管理部、市场监管总局 (2022). 关于加强剧本娱乐经营场所管理的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-06/27/content_5698021.htm>
- Note: These venues run dark, partitioned, flammable, with puzzle-locked doors — fires there escape harder than ordinary shops. Listing pages show floor and building type; non-compliant, change venues.

### 6. Mood low: walk or run — exercise's antidepressant effect scales with intensity
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=yes benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. Half-hour sessions. The hard part: moving when you least want to.
- In plain terms: Brisk walking or jogging improves most; yoga, strength training, and tai chi/qigong follow closely. Higher intensity, better effect; strength and yoga stick best. Evidence quality runs low — what's certain is movement over stillness, in whatever form you'll keep.
- Benefit: Pooling 218 randomized trials — 495 arms, 14,170 participants meeting major-depression criteria — in a Bayesian network meta-analysis against active controls (usual care, placebo pills), effect sizes in Hedges' g (more negative, more improvement): walking/jogging −0.62 (95% CrI −0.80 to −0.45, the credible interval), yoga −0.55 (−0.73 to −0.36), strength training −0.49 (−0.69 to −0.29), mixed aerobic −0.43 (−0.61 to −0.24), tai chi/qigong −0.42 (−0.65 to −0.21). "The effects of exercise were proportional to prescribed intensity," with strength training and yoga most tolerable
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Noetel M, Sanders T, Gallardo-Gómez D, et al. (2024). Effect of exercise for depression: systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials. BMJ, 384, e075847. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-075847>
- Note: Contested: the authors flag that only 1 of 218 trials ran clean; walking/jogging grades "low" certainty, the rest "very low." Exercise can't blind participants — knowing you exercise colors the report, an expectation that can't be removed. The direction holds: move over stay, intensity not too low, the form you'll keep. This expands Section 3's "move first" and replaces no prescribed treatment.

### 7. When anxiety disrupts life, mindfulness-based stress reduction matches a common anxiolytic
<!-- costs: money=low time=high will=yes benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Courses cost a little; 8 weekly sessions plus daily home practice. The hard part: 8 weeks of daily practice.
- In plain terms: Diagnosed anxiety-disorder patients completing 8 weeks of MBSR improved almost identically to escitalopram. Nearly 80% of the drug arm had adverse reactions, some quitting; the mindfulness arm ran 15%, zero quits. The course must be standardized 8-week MBSR — a meditation app isn't.
- Benefit: A non-inferiority randomized trial at 3 US academic centers: 276 adults with diagnosed anxiety disorders, 1:1 randomized to 8-week MBSR or flexible-dose escitalopram (10–20 mg); 208 completed. End-point anxiety severity (CGI-S): mindfulness −1.35, drug −1.43; difference −0.07 (95% CI −0.38 to 0.23; P=0.65 — indistinguishable from zero), the lower bound inside the pre-set non-inferiority margin of −0.495. Side effects split widely: drug 78.6% with at least one related adverse event, 10 quitting; mindfulness 15.4%, zero quits
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Hoge EA, Bui E, Mete M, Dutton MA, Baker AW, Simon NM (2023). Mindfulness-based stress reduction vs escitalopram for the treatment of adults with anxiety disorders: a randomized clinical trial. JAMA Psychiatry, 80(1), 13-21. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2022.3679>
- Note: The trial used standardized 8-week MBSR — no meditation app equivalent. It applies to those meeting anxiety-disorder criteria; the doctor still comes first. If side-effect fears have kept you untreated, here's an equally evidenced option.

### 8. For on-the-spot recovery, 5 minutes of "cyclic sighing": two-stage inhale, long exhale
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. Five minutes daily.
- In plain terms: Five minutes of breathing daily for a month improved mood and slowed breathing better than equal-time mindfulness. Among breathing patterns, exhale-lengthening cyclic sighing won. Method: nasal inhale, a second small sip to fill the lungs, then a slow mouth exhale to empty. One trial, self-reported — but free.
- Benefit: A home-based randomized trial compared three 5-minute daily breathing practices against equal-time mindfulness over 1 month: cyclic sighing (exhale-emphasized), box breathing (equal inhale-hold-exhale), and cyclic hyperventilation with retention (long inhale, short exhale). Mixed-effects modeling favored breathing over mindfulness — cyclic sighing above all — on mood improvement (P<0.05) and respiratory-rate reduction (P<0.05)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Balban MY, Neri E, Kogon MM, et al. (2023). Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal. Cell Reports Medicine, 4(1), 100895. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895>
- Note: Method: nasal inhale, brief second sip to fill, then a long slow mouth exhale, 5 minutes. One home trial, self-reported, small sample — better than meditation but no problem-solver. Free, 5 minutes, anywhere: high value.

### 9. Treat "regularly seeing people" as a health expense — not just when mood dips
<!-- costs: money=low time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: A little money and meeting time. The hard part: initiating while feeling fine.
- In plain terms: The socially isolated run about 30% higher mortality; the lonely about a quarter; those living alone about 30% — stronger under 65. All observational: the already-ill may live alone more.
- Benefit: Pooling 70 prospective studies — 3.4 million participants, average 7 years — social isolation, loneliness, and living alone each tracked higher mortality. Adjusted weighted odds ratios: isolation 1.29, loneliness 1.26, living alone 1.32 — roughly 30%, a quarter, and 30% higher — effects similar in size and larger under 65
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Holt-Lunstad J, Smith TB, Baker M, Harris T, Stephenson D (2015). Loneliness and social isolation as risk factors for mortality: a meta-analytic review. Perspectives on Psychological Science, 10(2), 227-237. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691614568352>
- Note: Contested: observational associations, no assignment, causation unsortable — the already-ill isolate more. The authors rank social connection alongside smoking and obesity, but no randomized trial shows added socializing lowering mortality. No conflict with Section 3's "cut draining relationships": that trims what drains you, this preserves what restores you.

### 10. Count neighborhood greenery when choosing housing — but don't expect park visits to extend life
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=small metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. Glance at the greenery while viewing.
- In plain terms: Better greenery within 500 meters of home tracks lower mortality — about 4% per 0.1 NDVI. It measures home surroundings, not park visits. Green neighborhoods house wealthier, cleaner-air residents — so read it as a housing criterion, not a longevity prescription.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis of 9 cohorts across 7 countries, 8.32 million people, measuring greenness within 500 meters by normalized difference vegetation index (NDVI). Each 0.1 increase ran pooled mortality HR 0.96 (95% CI 0.94–0.97) — about 4% lower, the true value between 3% and 6%. Seven of nine studies found greener-lower mortality beyond chance; two found nothing
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Rojas-Rueda D, Nieuwenhuijsen MJ, Gascon M, Perez-Leon D, Mudu P (2019). Green spaces and mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of cohort studies. The Lancet Planetary Health, 3(11), e469-e477. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2542-5196(19)30215-3>
- Note: Contested: heterogeneity runs extreme (I²=95%); the measure is residential greenness, not park visits; greener-dwelling people run richer, cleaner air, and more exercise — inseparable contributors. Don't read "parks extend life"; the usable part is greenery as a free housing criterion. NDVI: bare ground and concrete near 0, dense canopy near 1 — 0.1 approximates the jump from a bare block to tree-lined streets.
