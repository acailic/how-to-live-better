# 21. Travel abroad and safety overseas

This section counts money and personal freedom, with a few entries touching personal safety. Abroad, the destination's law governs you, not China's — and what Chinese embassies and consulates can do has clear limits.

### 1. Before departure, check the foreign ministry's safety notices once — "do not travel" places stay unvisited
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. Minutes on the consular-service site.
- In plain terms: The ministry grades foreign risk in four tiers, topping out at "do not travel." From 2026-09-15, avoiding high-risk countries is written into administrative regulation, with document and border staff reminding — and where necessary dissuading — travelers. Read the page before departure.
- Benefit: The ministry publishes graded safety notices, roughly "take precautions," "heighten precautions," "travel with caution," and "do not travel." The new regulation of 2026-09-15 writes the personal duty in: "Chinese citizens shall follow foreign safety reminders and destination risk notices, avoiding travel to and stay in high-risk countries or regions," while requiring the immigration authorities to remind — and "where necessary dissuade" — those heading to top-tier or incident-surging destinations
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第二条）；国务院 (2023). 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第十九、二十一条）；外交部领事司. 安全提醒. <https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zggmzhw/lsbh/aqtx/>
- Note: Notices shift with events; this book copies no list — check the page yourself. As of 2026-09-07, the column carried "do not travel"-class notices for Eswatini (2026-08-25), Palestine (2023-10-10), and Sudan (2023-04-17), plus Afghanistan (2023-02-17), Syria (2023-01-04), Peru (2022-12-29), and Somalia (2022-11-24), with separate notices for northern Myanmar and parts of the DRC. Tour operators carry disclosure duties too — demand written risk statements before joining a tour.

### 2. Save 12308 and the local consulate's protection line in your phone, plus a paper copy in your wallet — not after trouble starts
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Two numbers saved, one copy written, minutes.
- In plain terms: One rescue line exists. Domestic: 12308. Overseas: +86-10-12308. The hotline has answered 3-million-plus calls, with 500,000-plus consular-protection cases handled in the same period.
- Benefit: The global consular-protection emergency hotline runs 24 hours: domestically 12308; overseas +86-10-12308 or +86-10-65612308. Since 2014 it has fielded over 3 million calls, with the ministry and missions handling 500,000-plus cases
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 外交部、中国驻缅甸使领馆 (2023). 关于当前缅北地区安全局势的领事提醒. <https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zggmzhw/lsbh/aqtx/202311/t20231109_11176682.shtml>；国务院新闻办 (2023). 国务院政策例行吹风会介绍领事保护与协助有关情况. <https://www.gov.cn/xinwen/2023zccfh/11/index.htm>
- Note: The local mission keeps its own protection line, usually faster than the switchboard — look it up by country on the consular site and store both. A lost or dead phone defeats stored numbers: write a copy for the wallet or a family member.

### 3. Know what consular protection can and cannot do: visits yes, springing you no, and the bills stay yours
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Minutes reading the boundary.
- In plain terms: Arrested abroad, the mission can verify your situation, demand lawful and fair treatment, visit you, and observe trial. It cannot overturn verdicts or walk you out. Food, lodging, transport, and medical advanced by third parties revert to your bill. So prepare money and insurance beforehand — "the state will catch me" is not a plan.
- Benefit: On your arrest, the mission verifies with local authorities and demands lawful, fair handling — the regulation's words: "visit or contact per the host country's law and applicable treaties, learn the citizen's needs, and require the host authorities to give the citizen humanitarian and fair treatment." Missions may observe trials and demand protection of your procedural rights, urge emergency rescue when you're injured or disaster strikes, and assist families with aftermath. On money, the regulation is equally clear: "Chinese citizens, legal persons, and non-legal-person organizations that receive food, lodging, transport, medical goods or services from third parties during consular protection shall pay the costs properly borne by themselves" — advanced sums revert to you
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2023). 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第九、十、十四、二十六条）
- Note: Missions pay no fines, medical bills, or tickets, and overturn no verdicts. They provide local lawyer, translator, medical, and funeral referrals, contact relatives, and locate relief channels. Prepare money and insurance yourself.

### 4. Buy insurance covering overseas medical and medical evacuation — not just flight-delay cover
<!-- costs: money=low time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Short-term travel insurance, tens to hundreds of yuan.
- In plain terms: Overseas care is out-of-pocket: domestic insurance barely reimburses, consulates don't advance. What truly sinks a household isn't the clinic bill but hospitalization after serious injury and getting the person home — charter or medical escort. Two limits matter: overseas medical costs, and emergency medical evacuation and repatriation.
- Benefit: Overseas treatment bills are yours: domestic insurance barely reimburses, and consular protection doesn't advance them (item 3, per Article 26). What sinks households is hospitalization and evacuation: repatriation from an overseas hospital takes charter aircraft or medical escort — the priciest class of self-paid items. The US CDC: emergency transport from a remote area to a quality hospital "could otherwise cost more than $100,000." Two limits govern purchase: the overseas medical limit and the emergency-evacuation-and-repatriation limit
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 国务院 (2023). 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第二十六条）；美国疾病控制与预防中心. Travelers' Health: Travel Insurance："Medical evacuation insurance covers emergency transportation from a remote area to a high-quality hospital, which could otherwise cost more than $100,000.". <https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/page/insurance>；其余为作者经验，无直接文献
- Note: $100,000 is a floor statement, not a range — no official or industry range statistics were found, so none quoted. Confirm three things before buying: destination coverage; exclusions on diving, skiing, and motorcycling; and direct-billing versus reimbursement. Visa-free destinations deserve cover equally.

### 5. "Overseas high-pay recruitment" is fraud, full stop — scammed into telecom-fraud compounds, you return exit-barred
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: declining an attractive job with your own hands.
- In plain terms: Absurd salaries, no credentials asked, no proper contract, assembly in a border city, passport "kept for you" — go, and neither you nor your passport stays in your hands. Forged exit documents get stopped at the border; overseas crime involvement can bar exit 6 months to 3 years after return.
- Benefit: The official characterization is blunt: people "deceived abroad or leaving illegally on fabricated grounds to engage in cross-border gambling, telecom fraud, and other illegal activity, gravely endangering people's lives and property." The new regulation tightens both ends. Exit: "grounds for exit, entry, stay, or residence shall be true and lawful" — false materials or statements mean no documents and no departure. Return: "Chinese citizens who engaged in illegal or criminal activity abroad harming national security or interests may... be barred from exit for 6 months to 3 years from the date of return"; administrative detention for fraudulently obtained documents or illegal exit-entry carries the same 6-month-to-3-year bar
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第三、四条）；司法部、公安部、国家移民局 (2026). 就《国务院关于出境入境管理的规定》答记者问. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202608/content_7077270.htm>
- Note: The markers are fixed: salaries far above the post, credentials waived, no proper contract, border-city assembly, visa handled and passport held. Northern Myanmar carries dedicated consular notices. Once there, liberty and passport leave your hands together.

### 6. Overseas cash withdrawal caps at 100,000 yuan yearly — across all cards under your name combined
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Estimate cash needs beforehand and lean on card payments. The hard part: breaking the cash habit.
- In plain terms: The cap isn't per card but across all your bank cards (supplementary cards included), per calendar year, 100,000 yuan. Once exhausted, no overseas withdrawals for the rest of the year and the next. Card spending runs untouched, and the yearly $50,000 FX quota stays unspent.
- Benefit: Under SAFE's 2017 notice (effective 2018-01-01): "individuals holding domestic bank cards withdrawing cash abroad — all cards under the person's name (supplementary cards included) may not exceed the equivalent of 100,000 yuan per calendar year; exceeding the annual quota suspends overseas cash withdrawal on domestic cards for the remainder of the year and the following year." Borrowing or lending cards to evade the rules is barred. SAFE daily sends suspension lists to issuers, which must block listed holders' overseas withdrawals from 17:00 Beijing time that day. Listed individuals may query their withdrawal details at SAFE branches with ID. Foreign-currency cards' per-card daily cap moved from $1,000-equivalent to 10,000-yuan-equivalent; RMB cards hold at 10,000-yuan-equivalent. Violations punish under the FX regulations. The Q&A adds: the notice doesn't affect card spending abroad — food, lodging, transport, and shopping pay by card without touching the $50,000 convenience quota. Quotas compute from delayed batch data, "SAFE uses delayed control" rather than real-time interception. In 2016, 81% of overseas cash withdrawals ran under 30,000 yuan (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家外汇管理局 (2017). 关于规范银行卡境外大额提取现金交易的通知（汇发〔2017〕29 号，第一、二、三、四、六、七条，2018 年 1 月 1 日起实施）. <https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2017/1230/21873.html>；国家外汇管理局 (2017). 外汇局有关负责人就规范银行卡境外大额提取现金交易有关问题答记者问. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2017-12/31/content_5251958.htm>；中国银联国际. 境外ATM取款. <https://m.unionpayintl.com/wap/cn/serviceCenter/cardUsingInstructions/805.shtml>
- Note: Quotas reset January 1. Only withdrawal suspends; spending runs on. Genuinely needing large cash abroad: purchase FX under the individual FX measures and carry banknotes. Two withdrawal traps per UnionPay's page: dual-logo cards unable to select the UnionPay rail on some foreign ATMs, and local surcharges collected by acquirers, "unrelated to UnionPay." The same page advises debit over credit cards for withdrawal (overdraft interest). Lost, swallowed, or defrauded cards: Section 14, item 5.

### 7. Photograph passport, visa, and ID into the cloud; lose the passport — police report first, then a travel document
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. A few photos before departure.
- In plain terms: Lose the passport, and the mission issues a return document — provided you can prove who you are. Photos of the data and visa pages plus entry-exit records speed everything. You'll also need the local police report: exit checks and insurance claims honor it. Phones vanish with passports — don't store photos only on the phone.
- Benefit: Missions issue return travel documents on proof of identity; clean photos of data and visa pages and travel records change the speed materially. Report locally first for the receipt — exit checks and insurers want it. The mission's entry point: the two numbers from item 2
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 国务院 (2023). 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第十六条）；作者经验，无直接文献
- Note: Store twice — cloud plus one family member — plus a paper copy apart from originals; photograph bank cards and prescriptions too. Phones die with passports, and cloud logins demanding SMS codes die with the phone: test login from another device before departure. Document photos are sensitive — encrypted storage is safer, but lost passwords lock everyone out, so the family copy is non-negotiable.

### 8. Overseas self-drive: confirm the destination accepts Chinese licenses — most "international driving permits" sold online are waste paper
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. One regulation check before departure.
- In plain terms: The "IDP" doesn't hold in mainland China: it's a translation instrument under the 1949 Geneva convention among 103 parties — mainland China absent. Nothing anyone issues domestically carries legal force. Whether a Chinese license drives depends on the destination's own rules — unconfirmed, don't drive.
- Benefit: The "international driving permit" is a translation document mutually recognized among parties to the 1949 Road Traffic Convention (Geneva, 1949-09-19, effective 1952-03-26), currently 103 parties. Mainland China isn't among them — China issues no IDP, and any domestic agency "processing one" produces nothing lawful. Driving on a Chinese license turns on the destination's rules: some accept it with notarized translation, some require local exchange, some reject it outright
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: United Nations Treaty Collection. Convention on Road Traffic, Geneva, 19 September 1949 — status of treaties. <https://treaties.un.org/pages/ViewDetailsV.aspx?src=TREATY&mtdsg_no=XI-B-1&chapter=11&clang=_en>
- Note: In most countries unlicensed driving is criminal or heavily fined — and post-accident insurance mostly refuses to pay, the true cost. Check two ways: the destination's "notice for Chinese citizens" on the consular site, or written confirmation from the rental company. Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, and select states run separate arrangements.

### 9. Visa, study-abroad, and immigration agents: ask whether they're filed with the immigration authorities first
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. One filing question, and keep the papers.
- In plain terms: From 2026-09-15, such agents file with the immigration authorities. "Guaranteed pass," "guaranteed visa," "we'll do your documents" sit among the express prohibitions. Your own form errors cost a refusal; fabricated materials leave a stain on your future entry-exit record.
- Benefit: The 2018 abolition of pre-approval for private entry-exit intermediaries bred rapid growth and problems — "unclear agency numbers, some agencies handling business illegally, harming exit-entry parties' lawful rights." From 2026-09-15, filing management replaces it: agencies report within 15 days of establishment, existing ones within 90 days of effect. Prohibited: publishing false information or exaggerated and misleading solicitation; providing or assisting false materials and irregular visa, stay-residence, or passport processing; leaking, selling, or unlawfully providing privacy or personal information; and organizing or assisting cross-border crime. Offshore entities may not provide such services in China
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第七、八、十、十二、十三条）
- Note: Agents mouthing "guaranteed pass" are doing the expressly prohibited; discovered, the stain lands on your visa record. Your own errors cost a refusal; fabricated materials cost far more. Unpaid policy consulting and information lookup don't count as intermediary services.

### 10. Never issue invitation letters for strangers, nor lend your name to entry-exit procedures
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: refusing a favor to a face.
- In plain terms: Issuing someone's invitation letter or application materials with false contents bills the issuer: individuals 5,000–10,000 yuan plus confiscation of gains; units 10,000–50,000, handlers another 5,000–10,000. One favor, one bill.
- Benefit: Issuers answer for truth: "individuals issuing false invitation letters or other application materials for others' exit-entry, stay, or residence applications face immigration-authority fines of 5,000 to 10,000 yuan, with illegal gains confiscated." Units issuing false materials face 10,000–50,000 yuan, with directly responsible managers and other liable persons fined another 5,000–10,000
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第三、十一条）
- Note: Same class as Section 8's "never carry for strangers": materials under your name land consequences on you. Public officials and military personnel seeking foreign nationality or residence through agents — agents must refuse and report to the supervision organ.

### 11. Before departure: send itinerary, lodging, and companions to one family member, and agree on a contact cadence
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. One message before departure.
- In plain terms: If you go missing abroad, family can seek the mission, which shares local police procedures and presses cases after filing — provided someone at home knows where you should be and when you should call. Flight numbers, address, companions' phones, and the agreed cadence, sent to one person, suffice.
- Benefit: When Chinese citizens go missing abroad, relatives may seek the missions — "the diplomatic mission shall provide local reporting methods and other rescue information," pressing local police after filing. All of it starts only when someone at home knows your expected whereabouts and contact schedule
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 国务院 (2023). 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第十三条）；作者经验，无直接文献
- Note: Four items suffice: flight number, lodging address, companions' names and phones, agreed contact frequency. For signal-poor or insecure regions, add the "no news for X days means report to police" line. Every rescue procedure above requires family knowing your itinerary first.
