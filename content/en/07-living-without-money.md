# 7. Living without money

This section counts money and coverage: how much you can draw, how much waived, what services, what conditions — none converted into lifespan. Policies change yearly and vary by region, so amounts and thresholds follow your locality's current publications. Ordering: free, low-paperwork, fast-acting first; long-qualification and regionally divergent later; experience-only, no official documents, last.

Entries in this section are grouped by theme, with item numbers in parentheses.

**Lost your job**: claim unemployment insurance online (item 1), employment-difficulty certification (item 12), vocational-training subsidy (item 13).

**Owed wages**: complain to labor inspection first (item 2), apply for legal aid (item 3), figure out who hired you (item 22).

**Emergency backstops**: relief stations (item 4), temporary assistance (item 6), subsistence allowance/dibao (item 7), the two disability subsidies (item 8), replace a lost ID immediately (item 11), truly nowhere to sleep (item 21).

**Medical insurance and social insurance**: don't lapse resident medical insurance (item 9), serious illness goes through insurance and medical assistance first (item 10), don't panic over lapsed contributions (item 18), add medical or critical-illness cover (item 20).

**Finding work and starting over**: public employment services and gig markets (item 5), systematic job hunting (item 14), no deposits, no surrendered IDs (item 15), paths back after prison or bankruptcy (item 19).

**Housing and food**: queue for public rental housing (item 16), cut housing and food to the minimum (item 17).

### 1. When unemployed, claim unemployment insurance online first
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Apply from your phone — three entries work: the "12333" app, the electronic social-security-card mini-program in WeChat or Alipay, or the national social insurance public service platform. A social-security card or ID card suffices; most regions need no in-person visit. Prerequisite: file unemployment registration first.
- In plain terms: Employer-plus-you contributions totaling 1 year, and not a resignation you initiated — register as unemployed and you can draw. 1–5 years: up to 12 months. 5–10: up to 18. Over 10: up to 24. While drawing, unemployment insurance pays your employee medical insurance.
- Benefit: Three conditions together: unemployment insurance paid 1+ cumulative years; the job didn't end at your own initiative (legally "employment interruption not due to the person's own will"); and you've registered as unemployed with job-seeking intent. Duration follows contribution years: 1–5 years, up to 12 months; 5–10, up to 18; 10+, up to 24. Monthly amounts are set provincially and differ. The benefit sits between the local minimum wage and urban dibao, with the national goal of gradually reaching 90% of minimum wage. While drawing, you remain in employee basic medical insurance with full benefits — the premium comes from the unemployment fund, not you (national; regulation since 1999)

Non-compete obligations and unpaid compensation: Section 11, item 12. Employee medical insurance during the benefit period is covered by unemployment insurance. Provincial amounts differ widely — your enrollment region's publication governs. ### 2. For owed wages, complain to labor inspection first, then arbitrate — both free, most cases resolved within months
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free — both complaint and arbitration. Assemble the labor contract, pay slips, attendance records, transfer records, and chats; visit the human-resources bureau or arbitration committee 1–3 times. Arbitration carries a 1-year limitation period (statute of limitations); current employees still being underpaid face no such limit — the departed must file within 1 year of leaving. The hard part is repeated trips, document hunting, and flagging morale.
- In plain terms: Both routes cost nothing. First call 12333 or complain to the bureau's labor inspection: 60 working days to investigate once filed, with orders to pay on deadline plus 50%–100% extra for noncompliance. Then arbitration, concluded within 45 days. But "concluded" isn't "paid": assetless companies and absconded bosses can leave you winning nothing.
- Benefit: Step one, the complaint. Call 12333 or visit the local labor inspection office. The law allows 60 working days from filing, extendable 30 more for complex cases. Verified, they can order payment on deadline; missing it adds compensation of 50%–100% of the owed amount. In 2024, inspection bodies nationwide handled 137,000 cases, recovering 8.65 billion yuan of wages for 654,000 people. With an IOU or payroll and clear facts, you can also apply directly to the basic-level court for a payment order — a court command to pay, issued within 15 days of acceptance. Step two, arbitration: 45 days from acceptance, extendable 15. Wage claims may seek advance execution — partial enforcement before full adjudication. Claims under 12× the local monthly minimum wage are final at award: the company cannot sue again. In 2024, 4.257 million arbitration cases were filed, 4.156 million concluded, 93.47 billion yuan at stake resolved; mediation success 79.6%, case-closure rate 98.2%, finality rate 73.7%. The "half a year to a year" talk describes post-arbitration court cases: 6 months first instance (3 under simplified procedure) plus 3 on appeal. In 2024, courts concluded 88,000 wage-claim cases and recovered 28.54 billion yuan; 998 people were convicted of the crime of refusing to pay wages (national)

- Note: Complaints and arbitration work, but distinguish "concluded" from "paid": the 98.2% closure rate only means procedure completed. Assetless companies and absconded bosses leave judgments unenforced. ### 3. Can't afford a lawsuit? Apply for legal aid — wage claims, support, and work injuries are already covered
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Legal aid charges the aided nothing.
- In plain terms: Wage claims, support payments, social-insurance or dibao benefits, and losses from acting heroically — all qualify for free legal aid. Call 12348 or visit the local legal aid center. No more trekking home for a poverty certificate — a truthful statement now suffices. Capital-punishment defendants, plus blind, deaf, mute, and minor defendants, get lawyers assigned unconditionally.
- Benefit: The State Council regulation lists six civil matters: "state compensation claims; social insurance or minimum-living-guarantee benefits; pensions and relief payments; support payments; labor remuneration; civil rights arising from acting bravely for a just cause." In plain words: state compensation, social-insurance or dibao benefits, pensions and relief, support, wages, and heroism losses. On the criminal side, defendants facing possible death sentences, and blind, deaf, mute, or minor defendants, get assigned counsel regardless of means
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2003). 法律援助条例（国令第 385 号，第十条、第十一条、第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6944.htm>；司法部有关负责人 (2021). 就贯彻实施法律援助法答记者问. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-12/29/content_5665114.htm>
- Note: The Legal Aid Law, effective 2022-01-01, changed one thing: instead of "submitting a poverty certificate," a truthful self-declaration now suffices — no home-town trek. Entry points: local legal aid centers and public legal service centers, or 12348.

### 4. At the end of your rope, go to a relief station — food, shelter, and a ticket home
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Present yourself at the local relief administration station, or tell police or civil affairs to take you. Registration requires truthful name and belongings. The hard part is saying out loud that you have no money and nowhere to go.
- In plain terms: Relief stations feed and house you, hospitalize sudden illness, and contact family. No money to get home means a ticket. Stays usually run under 10 days. Voluntary in, voluntary out — not custody, no cash.
- Benefit: Stations provide hygienic food and shelter, hospital treatment for sudden illness, and contact with relatives and employers. Those unable to buy tickets to their registered or habitual residence get travel vouchers. "Registered residence" means your hometown; habitual residence, where you actually live. Stays generally under 10 days, extendable while awaiting family pickup, treatment, or placement. Police and other officials encountering vagrants in duty must inform them of the option; the disabled, minors, elderly, and mobility-impaired are guided and escorted (national, since 2003)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2003). 城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法（国务院令第 381 号，第五、六、七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62246.htm>；民政部 (2003). 城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法实施细则（民政部令第 24 号，第十一、十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62510.htm>
- Note: Voluntary entry and exit — not custody. Basic food and lodging, no cash. Travel vouchers are issued to registered or habitual residence — decide where you're going before asking.

### 5. For work, start with free public employment services and gig markets, not fee-charging agencies
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Register at county-level-or-above public employment services — employment service centers, human-resources markets. Local gig markets accept registration too. Unknown address: call 12333.
- In plain terms: Public institutions — job referrals, career guidance, policy consulting, employment and unemployment registration — all free, and the law bars them from profiting off these services. Gig-market registration and listings are free too. While there, file the unemployment registration — drawing benefits and employment-difficulty certification both need it.
- Benefit: The law mandates free: employment policy consulting; job vacancy information; career guidance and referrals; employment and unemployment registration; assistance for the employment-disadvantaged. These institutions may not run commercial operations or profit from these services. Gig job listings — registration and publication free to the public — are inside public employment information services. Long-waiting gig workers and low-income households get reinforced support (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007, 2015 修正). 就业促进法（第三十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647636.htm>；人力资源社会保障部等五部门 (2022). 关于加强零工市场建设 完善求职招聘服务的意见（人社部发〔2022〕38 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-07/09/content_5700177.htm>
- Note: Unemployment registration is the prerequisite for benefits and difficulty certification — do it in passing. Day-rate gig work: settle pay on the spot, keep contacts and work logs.

### 6. For sudden upheavals, apply for temporary assistance first
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Apply to the township government or subdistrict office, explaining what happened — fire, traffic accident, sudden major illness, or unavoidable expenses suddenly surging. After review and publication, the county civil affairs bureau approves. Materials follow the local checklist; usually 1–2 trips.
- In plain terms: A household suddenly overwhelmed — fire, crash, grave illness — can apply for temporary assistance without already being a dibao household. Usually a one-time payment; amounts vary widely by locality.
- Benefit: Two household classes qualify: those whose basic needs — food, shelter, medical care — are temporarily unmeetable due to accidents or sudden major illness; and dibao households whose unavoidable expenses spiked beyond capacity. Scope and amounts are set and published by county-level-or-above governments (national system since 2014; amounts local)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 国务院 (2014, 2019 修订). 社会救助暂行办法（国务院令第 649 号，第四十七、四十八、四十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm>
- Note: No dibao requirement. Usually one-time, amounts vary widely — your locality's current publication governs.

### 7. Income below the local dibao line: apply for dibao
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. A household member applies in writing at the township or subdistrict of your household registration; village or residents' committees can act for you. Expect income and asset verification. Usually 2–3 trips with IDs, household registers, and income/asset proof. The hard part is baring your finances to scrutiny and waiting through publication.
- In plain terms: Household income per head below the local dibao line, assets within limits: you can apply. At end-2024 the national averages were 798.1 yuan per person monthly urban, 593.9 rural. The payment is the gap: line minus per-head monthly income. Property, cars, savings, or children able to support you can block approval.
- Benefit: Household per-head income below the local standard with assets in range qualifies. End-2024 national averages: 798.1 yuan/person/month urban, 593.9 rural; urban dibao covered 6.25 million people, rural 33.615 million. Payment is the monthly gap: standard minus per-head income — e.g., standard 798.1 and household income 500 per head means 298.1 monthly per head, not 798.1. The standard is set from the prior year's local per-capita consumption times a ratio, adjusted periodically — last year's average spending per person, discounted (national average; local figures differ)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2014, 2019 修订). 社会救助暂行办法（国务院令第 649 号，第九、十、十一条；第十二条：「县级人民政府民政部门按照共同生活的家庭成员人均收入低于当地最低生活保障标准的差额，按月发给最低生活保障金」）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm>；国家统计局 (2025). 社会事业向好发展 民生福祉不断增进——「十四五」以来社会民生统计报告. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202509/content_7042627.htm>；民政部等四部门 (2024). 关于进一步做好最低生活保障标准确定调整工作的指导意见（民发〔2024〕16 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202403/content_7007237.htm>
- Note: Dibao is the last safety net, applied for at the registration locality. Household property, vehicles, or savings above local standards, or children with support capacity, can block approval. Approval brings more: subsidized resident medical insurance (item 9), medical assistance for care (item 10), and means-test-free legal aid (item 3).

### 9. Don't let the ~400-yuan resident medical insurance lapse; hardship cases get reductions
<!-- costs: money=low time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: The individual share runs at least 400 yuan yearly — the 2024/2025 national standard, locally exceeded — payable in the annual centralized enrollment window. The destitute get full subsidies; dibao recipients, fixed subsidies.
- In plain terms: You pay from 400 a year; the treasury adds about 700 per head. The destitute get full funding, dibao households fixed funding. Lapsing costs: enrolling outside the window or non-continuously triggers a 3-month no-coverage wait, plus 1 more month per added lapse year.
- Benefit: Beyond your own payment, the treasury adds per-head: at least 670 yuan in 2024, raised 30 in 2025 to at least 700, with the individual share still at least 400 (医保发〔2025〕22 号, per provincial documents); the 2026 government work report promised another 24. For dibao recipients and the destitute, eligible expenses after insurance reimbursement get another pass of at least 70% through medical assistance. Lapsing means waiting periods: enrolling outside the window or non-continuously brings a fixed 3-month benefit wait; each additional lapse year adds 1 month in principle (from 2025, national)

### 10. For serious illness, go through insurance, major-illness cover, medical assistance, and cross-region filing — not online loans
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The order is fixed. Step 1: before a cross-province hospitalization, file via the "National Cross-Region Treatment Filing" mini-program inside the "National Healthcare Security Administration" WeChat account. Step 2: settle directly at discharge. Step 3: if the remainder is still crushing, apply for medical assistance at the township or subdistrict. Step 4: if employed, take sick leave under the medical-treatment period — the sick yet un-dismissable window; work injuries go to work-injury insurance. Step 5: credit cards unpayable — proactively negotiate installments with the bank. Step 6: only then, fundraise on civil-affairs-designated personal-help platforms. Materials: diagnosis certificate, bills, settlement statements; 2–4 trips. The hard part is running errands while ill.
- In plain terms: The order is fixed. File before cross-province hospitalization in the NHSA account; settle at discharge without fronting the full bill. Still unaffordable after reimbursement: apply for medical assistance at the township or subdistrict. Employed: 3–24 months of medical-treatment period by seniority, during which the company cannot dismiss you. Avoid online loans and high-interest private borrowing.
- Benefit: The state stacks basic insurance, major-illness insurance, and medical assistance into the "triple system," catching layer by layer. Dibao recipients and the destitute get at least 70% of eligible costs assisted; patients impoverished by treatment may apply for further assistance. Cross-province filing enables direct settlement at discharge — no fronting and reimbursing back home. Sick leave carries a 3–24-month medical-treatment period by seniority, during which the employer may not terminate, and sick pay may not fall below 80% of local minimum wage. Injured workers get the wage-and-benefit-preserving recuperation period, paid monthly by the employer, generally up to 12 months. Credit-card debt beyond capacity with willingness to repay can be restructured into personalized installments up to 5 years — signed, the bank must stop collections.
