# 26. Building a website or platform: licenses, filing, and servers

You want to build a site or app. This section answers four things: which business it is legally, which license it needs, where the servers sit, and who answers for user content and money. Metrics: personal freedom and money. Ordering: prison-risk first, license-first second, daily platform duties next, and last the server-choice judgment, which offers judgment but no numbers. Employed as a technical worker: Section 11's red lines. Company registration and taxes: Section 12. The license comparison table and server decision table: [docs/做平台要办哪些证.md](../docs/做平台要办哪些证.md).

### 1. Never pool buyer money through the platform — route settlements through licensed payment institutions directly
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Time to integrate a licensed payment institution — a company holding a PBOC payment license — using its split-settlement or escrow features so buyer money settles straight to sellers, never entering platform accounts.
- In plain terms: Buyer money entering your account then forwarding to sellers is payment business — requiring central-bank approval and a genuinely injected 100 million yuan of capital. Unapproved, expect shutdown, confiscation, and fines, with the boss and handlers fined separately; serious cases bring prison. One safe design exists: the money never touches your account.
- Benefit: Payment business means "transferring monetary funds per electronic payment instructions submitted by payers or payees" — moving money on others' instructions. The business "requires People's Bank of China approval and a payment-business license," with minimum registered capital of 100 million yuan in paid-in currency. "Unapproved establishment of non-bank payment institutions or engagement, direct or disguised, in payment business is banned by the PBOC, with illegal gains confiscated." Unapproved operation: shutdown and confiscation; gains of 500,000-plus add 1–5× fines; none or under 500,000, 500,000–2 million; legal representatives and responsible persons fined another 100,000–500,000. Criminal law counts "illegally conducting funds payment and settlement" as illegal business — serious, up to 5 years; especially serious, 5-plus. The two-supreme threshold: 5-million-plus business volume or 100,000-plus gains (national; since May 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2023). 非银行支付机构监督管理条例（国务院令第 768 号，第二、六、八、二十七、四十七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11086/202401/content_6924970.html> ; 中国人民银行 (2024). 非银行支付机构监督管理条例实施细则（中国人民银行令〔2024〕第 4 号，第六十八条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11546/202408/content_6970979.html> ; 全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百二十五条第三项）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml> ; 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2019). 关于办理非法从事资金支付结算业务、非法买卖外汇刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（第一、三、四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/201901/t20190131_407161.shtml>
- Note: Designing money never to enter your account is the only safe route. The market calls this collection-and-disbursement "second settlement" (二清), a term absent from official documents; the implementation rules say "disguised engagement in payment business." The two-supreme interpretation lists only three scenarios — fabricated cash-outs, corporate-account cash-outs, check cash-outs — without naming platform collection, so platform collection "necessarily constitutes crime" cannot be claimed.

### 2. Charging websites mostly need a license; self-run sales without third-party merchants don't; matchmaking needs another class; free sites only file
<!-- costs: money=high time=high will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Filing is free; the value-added telecom license costs — it governs "whether a user-charging site may open." A company is prerequisite: 1 million yuan registered capital in-province, 10 million cross-province; 60-day review, 5-year validity.
- In plain terms: User-charging sites mostly need the value-added telecom license, unavailable to individuals. Selling your own goods on your own site without third-party merchants needs none; mini-programs and marketplace shops need none. Operating unlicensed: confiscation plus fines, serious cases closure.
- Benefit: The regulation: "the state implements a licensing system for commercial internet information services and a filing system for non-commercial services; without license or filing, no internet information service." Charging means licensing, free means filing. "Commercial" means "providing information or webpage-production services to users for payment through the internet." The matchmaking class is online data processing and transaction processing — EDI in the trade — defined as "providing users online data processing and transaction/affair processing via data and transaction platforms connected to public networks," including "transaction processing, electronic data interchange, and network/electronic-device data processing." Unlicensed or out-of-scope operation: confiscation plus 3–5× gains; under 50,000 or none, 100,000–1 million; serious, closure (national). Charging doesn't always mean licensing: Guangdong's communications authority (October 2025, per MIIT) lists three exemptions — enterprises "directly selling their own or other enterprises' goods or services in self-operated fashion" on their own sites without third-party storefronts; enterprises publishing only their own information; and enterprises operating solely on WeChat/Alipay mini-programs, official accounts, video channels, or marketplace shops "with no other independently operated platform." Admitting merchants or user publishing exits all three
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 广东省通信管理局 (2025). 关于办理增值电信业务经营许可证的常见问题解答（2025 年 10 月 17 日，第一项）. <https://gdca.miit.gov.cn/bsfw/bszn/jyxk/tzgg/art/2025/art_6bcbfa4f041247868c0de08e765b33e9.html> ; 国务院 (2000). 互联网信息服务管理办法（国务院令第 292 号，2024 年 12 月 6 日第二次修订，第三、四、七、十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1860864.htm> ; 国务院 (2024). 关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定（国务院令第 797 号，第十八项）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2025/issue_11786/202501/content_6997034.html> ; 国务院 (2000). 电信条例（2016 年第二次修订，第七、九、十三、六十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2016/content_5139478.htm> ; 工业和信息化部 (2017). 电信业务经营许可管理办法（工信部令第 42 号，第六、九、十一、十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2017/content_5240090.htm>
- Note: Whether yours is EDI — match the Telecommunications Business Classification Catalog's definitions yourself; no official sentence says "marketplaces with matchmaking and funds processing must hold EDI." The MIIT guide says only "apply for the corresponding telecom license per the business definition." Individuals can't hold the license — only companies. The three self-run exemptions come from Guangdong's Q&A citing MIIT requirements; no other province publishes the same text. Unsure: call your local communications authority. The same Q&A notes no agent needed — file on MIIT's telecom market management system, free of charge.

### 3. Livestreaming needs the network-culture license; the audiovisual-program license is effectively beyond private companies
<!-- costs: money=high time=high will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: The network-culture license governs "whether you may monetize livestreaming and game commentary" — provincial culture authority approval, decided within 20 days of acceptance, 3-year validity, renewal 30 days before expiry. The audiovisual-program license governs "whether you may publish programs online" and requires state-sole or state-controlled applicants.
- In plain terms: Livestreaming and game commentary need the provincial network-culture license with network performance in scope; 20-day decisions, 3-year terms; broadcast videos self-kept 60 days minimum. Unlicensed opening draws suspension, warning, and up to 30,000 yuan; persisting enters the blacklist.
- Benefit: Network-performance units must first license: "apply to the provincial culture administration for the Network Culture Business License, with network performance expressly in scope." Game-playing commentary runs "managed by reference to these measures." All network-performance video materials "shall be preserved no less than 60 days." Unlicensed commercial internet-culture activity: suspension, warning, plus up to 30,000 yuan; continued defiance enters the culture-market blacklist. Publishing programs online is another license — the Audiovisual Program Transmission License — applicants "with legal-person status, state-sole or state-controlled," effectively beyond private companies; unlicensed operation draws warnings, correction orders, up to 30,000 yuan, and serious cases, broadcast-regulation penalties (national)

- Note: Which license matches which business — the seven-department document is clearest: monetized livestream platforms need the network-culture license plus ICP filing; program publishing needs the audiovisual license or completed registration; news needs the internet-news license. The gov.cn reprint of the Interim Culture Provisions carries the 2011 unrevised text — cite the current Ministry of Culture and Tourism version.

### 4. Domestic servers mean filing; the access provider must itself hold the value-added telecom license
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Filing is free, submitted through the provider alongside the server, days to weeks. One credential check, ten minutes.
- In plain terms: Domestic servers file before opening — free, through the provider, days to weeks. Unfiled: correction order plus 10,000 yuan, refusal closing the site; missing homepage filing numbers add 5,000–50,000.
- Benefit: "Without filing, no non-commercial internet information service within the People's Republic." Free-to-user sites on domestic servers still file. Unfiled: correction plus 10,000 yuan; refusal, closure. Providers are regulated too: they "may not provide internet access services for unfiled organizations or individuals conducting non-commercial services," and collecting providers "may not provide access or fee-collection services to units or individuals without licenses or filings" — no legitimate provider connects unlicensed, unfiled sites. Data-center hosting and cloud hosts constitute the internet data-center business and broadband access the internet access business — both licensed, so the provider itself must hold licenses. Homepage filing-number omission: correction plus 5,000–50,000 yuan (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 信息产业部 (2005). 非经营性互联网信息服务备案管理办法（信产部令第 33 号，2024 年经工信部令第 68 号修订，第五、十三、十八、二十二条）. <https://www.miit.gov.cn/gyhxxhb/jgsj/cyzcyfgs/bmgz/xxtxl/art/2024/art_84a0cfa0ebd049bbbe751dca9a008e56.html> ; 工业和信息化部 (2017). 电信业务经营许可管理办法（工信部令第 42 号，第二十、二十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2017/content_5240090.htm> ; 工业和信息化部 (2015). 电信业务分类目录（2015 年版，B11、B14、B21、B25）. <https://www.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/wjfb/tg/art/2020/art_e98406cd89844f7e92ea1bcf3b5301e0.html>
- Note: Check credentials before negotiating prices — licenses via MIIT's tsm.miit.gov.cn by company name or number, site filings via beian.miit.gov.cn. An unlicensed "data center" leaves you no one to appeal to on failure day. Offshore hosting skips filing — but users and money domestic keep items 5–10's duties intact, adding data-export costs.

### 5. Letting users sell on your platform means verification, registration, reporting, and 3-year retention
<!-- costs: money=none time=high will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free, but backend seller verification and a registration archive, quarterly tax-information reporting, 3-year transaction retention. The hard part: a permanent duty, not a one-time task.
- In plain terms: Merchants on your platform get identity, address, contact, and license verified and registered; re-verified at least every six months. Identities report to market regulation, tax situations quarterly to tax; goods and transaction records keep three years. Misses draw correction, then 20,000–100,000 yuan.
- Benefit: The law's requirements: "require operators applying to sell goods or services on the platform to submit true identity, address, contact, and administrative-license information, verify, register, establish registration files, and periodically verify and update" — "at least once every six months" per the departmental rules. You report platform merchants' identity information to market regulation and, "per tax collection laws and regulations, report platform merchants' identity and tax-related information to the tax authorities." Goods, services, and transaction information "preserved no less than three years from transaction completion." Failures: correction; overdue, 20,000–100,000; serious, suspension plus 100,000–500,000. Tax information reports "within the month following quarter-end," with the same penalties for lateness (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 电子商务法（第十、二十七、二十八、三十一、八十条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf8af050b81> ; 市场监管总局 (2021). 网络交易监督管理办法（市场监管总局令第 37 号，第八、二十四、二十五、三十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5602020.htm> ; 国务院 (2025). 互联网平台企业涉税信息报送规定（国务院令第 810 号，第二、三、四、十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2025/issue_12146/202507/content_7030983.html> ; 国家税务总局 (2025). 关于互联网平台企业报送涉税信息有关事项的公告（税务总局公告 2025 年第 15 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202506/content_7029727.htm>
- Note: Merchants handle their own market-entity registration, with statutory exemptions for "self-produced agricultural and sideline products, family handicrafts, licensed-free convenience services, and sporadic small transactions" — "sporadic small" set at 100,000 yuan annual by the network-transactions rules. Two more platform duties: flag registered versus unregistered merchants, and never delete consumer reviews.

### 6. User content is yours to manage: review systems, report entrances, immediate takedown and reporting on violations
<!-- costs: money=low time=high will=yes benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Paid reviewers — the platform's largest permanent expense — plus logged report entrances and every handling. The hard part: someone watching daily; one idle day breeds trouble.
- In plain terms: User-posted trouble lands on the platform. Build the system: registration, posting review, comment review, daily patrol, incident handling. A conspicuous report entrance. On illegal information: immediate transmission stop, records kept, authorities reported. Failures: 50,000–500,000 yuan, graver, more.
- Benefit: Platforms "bear principal responsibility for information-content management," with systems covering "user registration, account management, information-publication review, comment review, page ecology management, real-time patrol, emergency response, and rumor and black-market information handling," and "conspicuous and convenient complaint and report entrances." On discovering illegal information: "immediately take disposal measures per law, preserve records, and report to the competent authorities." The Network Security Law: "on discovering information prohibited by law or regulation from publication or transmission, immediately stop transmission, take elimination measures, prevent diffusion, preserve records, and report to competent authorities." Failures: correction, warning, circular, plus 50,000–500,000; refusal or severity, 500,000–2 million, possible suspension, closure, or license revocation; responsible persons another 50,000–200,000 (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家互联网信息办公室 (2019). 网络信息内容生态治理规定（网信办令第 5 号，第八、九、十、十六、三十七条）. <http://www.cac.gov.cn/2019-12/20/c_1578375159509309.htm> ; 全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第四十九、六十九条）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm> ; 国务院 (2000). 互联网信息服务管理办法（第十五、十六、二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1860864.htm>
- Note: Review rules need no invention — Article 15 of the information-services measures lists the nine banned content classes. Livestreaming adds three: "a livestream content-review platform," the capacity to cut any live feed instantly ("immediate blocking"), and 60-day retention of livestream content and logs; performance videos likewise 60 days.

### 7. Information-publishing or instant-messaging services must demand true identity information
<!-- costs: money=low time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: A real-name verification channel, per-call priced. Design "backstage real-name, front-stage voluntary": real names stored backstage, nicknames displayed.
- In plain terms: Posting or chatting on your site means demanding true identity at service opening; refusers get nothing. Store real names backstage, nicknames displayed as users choose, verification by phone number. Skipping real names: correction; refusal, 50,000–500,000 and possible closure.
- Benefit: The law: "network operators providing network access, domain registration, fixed or mobile phone procedures, or information-publishing or instant-messaging services shall, when contracting or confirming service, require users to provide true identity information; without it, no related service." Failures to demand or serving the unidentified: correction; refusal or severity, 50,000–500,000, possible suspension, closure, or revocation, with responsible persons fined another 10,000–100,000. Livestreaming runs "backstage real-name, front-stage voluntary" with "mobile-phone-number-based true-identity authentication"; livestream commerce demands more — "identity-document and unified-social-credit-code authentication," the code being the business-license number (national)
- Evidence grade: A

### 8. No broadcaster accounts under 16; tipping runs age-banded
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Two flows added — registration and payment: age gate plus guardian consent confirmation.
- In plain terms: Under-16s get no broadcaster accounts; 16-plus get identity verification plus parental or guardian consent. Tipping runs three bands: under 8, none; 8–16, guardian consent; over 16, guardian consent or income proof. Unauthorized tips by minors get court-ordered refunded on guardian demand.
- Benefit: The law: "livestream providers may not provide broadcaster account registration to minors under sixteen; for those sixteen and above, identity authentication and parental or guardian consent are required." The regulations add a "dynamic true-identity verification mechanism for broadcasters" and require "reasonable limits on per-transaction and per-day cumulative spending by minors at different age stages." Tipping bands: "no tipping services to minors under eight; to minors eight to sixteen, guardian consent; to minors over sixteen, guardian consent or income-document verification." And: guardians' demands for return of unauthorized tips from persons with limited civil capacity get court support — limited capacity being minors not fully self-determining (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 未成年人保护法（第七十四、七十六条）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2020-10/22/c_1604928959588622.htm> ; 国务院 (2023). 未成年人网络保护条例（国务院令第 766 号，第三十一、四十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10806/202311/content_6913813.html> ; 中央网信办秘书局 (2026). 关于加强网络直播打赏规范管理的通知（第 7 项）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-04/13/c_1777815804150225.htm> ; 最高人民法院 (2020). 关于依法妥善审理涉新冠肺炎疫情民事案件若干问题的指导意见（二）（法发〔2020〕17 号，第九条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/230181.html>

### 9. Infringement notices: act timely; 15 days of silence after forwarding counter-statements means restoration
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. A fixed pipeline: receive, forward to merchant, receive defense, publicize outcomes — every step logged.
- In plain terms: An infringement notice with preliminary evidence means timely deletion, blocking, or link-cutting, plus forwarding to the merchant. Delay makes you jointly liable for the expanded harm. On the merchant's counter-statement, forward it and wait 15 days; neither complaint nor suit means restoring.
- Benefit: Rights-holders "may notify e-commerce platform operators to take necessary measures — deletion, blocking, link-cutting, termination of transactions and services — with notices including preliminary infringement evidence." One-line claims don't qualify. On notice, platforms "shall timely take necessary measures and forward the notice to in-platform operators; failure to act timely bears joint liability for the expanded harm." On counter-statement, "fifteen days after the statement reaches the rights-holder without complaint or suit notice, the platform shall promptly terminate the measures." Knowing or should-have-known infringement unhandled: joint liability with infringers; IP authorities may order correction, with overdue fines of 50,000–500,000, serious 500,000–2 million. Conversely, "malicious false notices causing in-platform operators' losses bear doubled liability" (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 电子商务法（第四十二、四十三、四十四、四十五、八十四条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf8af050b81> ; 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千一百九十五、一千一百九十六、一千一百九十七条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/233181.html>
- Note: Not an e-commerce platform — a general site runs the Civil Code's notice-and-takedown, the difference being the wait: the E-Commerce Law fixes 15 days, the Code says only "reasonable period." Publicizing notices, counter-statements, and outcomes is Article 44's express requirement. Platforms organizing piracy themselves or knowingly hosting it find notice-takedown no shield — Section 9, item 24.

### 10. User information doesn't casually leave the country — export carries statutory conditions and volume thresholds
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Cheapest: keep user data domestic. Genuine export: standard contracts or certification.
- In plain terms: Transmitting domestic users' data to offshore machines is export — offshore servers don't mean no export. Export takes one of four roads: cyberspace-administration security assessment, professional certification, standard-contract filing, or other statutory conditions — plus telling users who receives abroad, for what, and asking their separate consent.
- Benefit: Providing domestic users' information to offshore companies or machines legally "requires" one of: security assessment organized by the cyberspace authority; personal-information protection certification by a professional body; the standard contract with the offshore recipient; or other statutory conditions — with users told the recipient's name, contact, purposes, methods, information types, and rights exercise, and "separate consent" obtained. Thresholds by cumulative count: ordinary enterprises — data processors outside critical-information-infrastructure operators — clear one line: "cumulative provision abroad of under 100,000 persons' personal information (sensitive information excluded) since January 1 of the year" waives assessment, contract, and certification. From 100,000 to under 1 million: standard contract or certification. Over 1 million, or sensitive information over 10,000: security assessment (national; since March 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 个人信息保护法（第三十八、三十九、四十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202108/t20210820_527244.shtml> ; 国家互联网信息办公室 (2024). 促进和规范数据跨境流动规定（网信办令第 16 号，第三、四、五、七、八条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11366/202405/content_6954192.html> ; 国家互联网信息办公室 (2022). 数据出境安全评估办法（网信办令第 11 号，第四条）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2022-07/07/c_1658811536396503.htm>
- Note: Offshore servers don't mean no export — domestic users' data on offshore machines is export. One class skips all three roads: cross-border shopping, delivery, and travel bookings, "necessary for concluding or performing contracts to which the individual is a party." Counting runs "cumulative since January 1 of the year," not a trailing twelve months.

### 11. Server choice: outage tolerance first, operations staff second, price last
<!-- costs: money=low time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Mainstream cloud hosts run hundreds to thousands monthly; small-provider VPSes from tens — a VPS being a small virtual server; dedicated colocation from thousands yearly with hardware bought separately.
- In plain terms: The three tiers differ not in price but in what you hold on failure day. Big clouds carry filing channels, public credentials, incident notices, compensation clauses, and reachable humans; small providers halve the price but leave you no appeal on overselling, absconding, or upstream termination. Static sites without user data run fine on tens-of-yuan VPSes.
- Benefit: The tiers differ in what you hold on failure day: mainstream clouds offer filing channels, public credentials, incident bulletins, contractual compensation, and reachable people; small providers halve prices but leave no appeal on overselling, absconding, or upstream cutoffs — so check their value-added telecom license in the MIIT system before negotiating. Dedicated colocation pays only when both hold: bandwidth or hardware distinctly cheaper, and your own operations staff. Choosing: static, no-user-data sites take the cheapest VPS; sites with registration and transactions take elastically scalable, snapshot-capable cloud hosts with backups in at least two places
