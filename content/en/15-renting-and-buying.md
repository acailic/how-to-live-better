# 15. Renting and buying

Metric: money. Renting loses money most easily through deposits and agents; buying, through loan interest and everything the seller knows that you don't.

### 1. The rental deposit's amount, refund time, and deduction conditions must be written into the contract
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Ten extra minutes at signing. The hard part: raising it in front of the landlord.
- In plain terms: How much, refunded when, deductible under what — written before signing. Deduction reasons absent from the contract can't be used against your deposit.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation is plain: "where the lessor collects a deposit, the housing lease contract shall stipulate the deposit amount, refund time, and deduction circumstances; beyond the contractually stipulated circumstances, the lessor may not deduct without justified reason." Three things in the contract: amount, refund time, deduction conditions. Unwritten reasons can't touch your money
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Note: On departure day, photograph and video together with the landlord: utility meters, walls, floors. Deposit docked without cause: complain to the housing-rental administration first; small claims for small amounts — Section 8.

### 2. Water or power cut, locks changed, door-knocking threats: report to police first — these eviction methods are barred
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One phone call, done on the spot.
- In plain terms: Landlords may not cut utilities, change locks, or threaten in person to force termination or vacancy — expressly prohibited. On it happening, report; the response record is your evidence for later damages. Don't confront physically.
- Benefit: The regulation is plain: "lessors may not use violence, threats, or other illegal means to force lessees to terminate housing leases or vacate leased housing." Report first, preserve evidence; no physical confrontation
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Note: Reporting creates the response record that later proves the claim. Notify the housing-rental administration simultaneously.

### 3. Agents may not collect or disburse rent and deposits — money goes straight to the landlord
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Verify the payee before paying, minutes. The hard part: withstanding the agent's "transfer to us."
- In plain terms: Rent and deposits may not pass through agents; pay the landlord directly. Every intermediary hop is one more chance of the agent absconding — your rent paid, your tenancy gone. Verify the payee against the property certificate.
- Benefit: The regulation lists "collecting and disbursing housing rent and deposits" among real-estate brokerage prohibitions: agents may not receive your rent or deposit on the landlord's behalf. Every hop adds absconding risk
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Note: Verify the payee against the certificate; a non-owner demands explanation plus written authorization. Agents may charge only the matchmaking service fee.

### 4. Long-term apartment operators: check the supervised-funds account first; never pay a year up front for the discount
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One check, a dozen-odd minutes.
- In plain terms: Paying a year up front buys a sliver of discount; the company's collapse takes your deposit and a dozen-odd months of prepaid rent. Rent-loans are worse: the operator runs, the installments stay yours. Check for a published supervised-funds account before signing.
- Benefit: The regulation requires subleasing operators to "establish a housing-rental supervised-funds account and publicize it." Subleasing operators master-lease from landlords and re-lease to you. The year-up-front discount never offsets collapse risk — deposit plus remaining rent vanishing together — and attached rent-loans multiply it
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Note: Read for loan bindings at signing: an installment app paying the operator the year while you repay monthly means the operator's flight leaves your installments running. Don't sign that contract.

### 5. Home sold mid-lease: the lease stands, no moving
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: Sold mid-lease, your lease survives; the new owner honors the existing contract to term. No moving, no renegotiating — provided you lawfully occupy, with contract, transfer records, and move-in evidence kept.
- Benefit: The Civil Code's "sale does not break the lease": a change of ownership during the lease term does not affect the lease contract's validity. The new owner honors the original contract to term
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Note: The precondition is lawful possession — keep the contract, transfers, and move-in timing evidence.

### 6. Before signing: verify the property certificate and mortgages; every payment by transfer with purpose memos
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One check, half an hour.
- In plain terms: The two classic total losses: rent paid to a non-owner, and a long-mortgaged house later seized. Check ownership and encumbrance at the real-estate registration center before signing. All money moves by transfer, memoed "[address] rent for [month]" — direct evidence in any dispute.
- Benefit: The two classic total losses: rent paid to a non-owner, and a mortgaged house later seized. Transfer memos of "[address] rent for [month]" serve as direct evidence
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献；证据留存与转账备注同理见第 8 节关于彩礼和借条的两条
- Note: Ownership is checkable at the local registration center. The landlord attends in person or delivers a written power of attorney. Subletters need the original landlord's written consent.

### 7. Second-hand purchases through agents: funds must move via the agent's dedicated bank transaction account
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: insisting on the dedicated account, possibly over the agent's objections.
- In plain terms: The purchase price — hundreds of thousands to millions, this book's largest single sum — routed through an agent must move via its dedicated supervised bank account, never WeChat to a salesperson. Two agencies collaborating on one deal may charge one commission; loan and title agency services require separate contracts and upfront fee disclosure.
- Benefit: The rules are plain: where the parties agree to route transaction funds through a brokerage, the funds "shall be transferred through the client transaction settlement funds dedicated deposit account opened by the brokerage at a bank" — a supervised account, not the agency's own and not a salesperson's private one
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 住房城乡建设部、国家发展改革委、人力资源社会保障部 (2011). 房地产经纪管理办法（令第 8 号，2016 年第 29 号令修改）第二十四条：「房地产交易当事人约定由房地产经纪机构代收代付交易资金的，应当通过房地产经纪机构在银行开设的客户交易结算资金专用存款账户划转交易资金。」第十八条「房地产经纪服务实行明码标价制度……在经营场所醒目位置标明房地产经纪服务项目、服务内容、收费标准」；第十九条「两家或者两家以上房地产经纪机构合作开展同一宗房地产经纪业务的，只能按照一宗业务收取佣金，不得向委托人增加收费」；第十七条代办贷款、代办房地产登记等其他服务「应当向委托人说明服务内容、收费标准等情况，经委托人同意后，另行签订合同」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm>
- Note: Hundreds of thousands to millions — this book's largest single transaction — never WeChat to a salesperson. Two common overcharges: joint-agency deals charging two commissions (one allowed), and loan/title agency fees without separate contract or upfront disclosure. Every payment by transfer with memos — item 6.

### 8. Don't rent partitioned rooms: the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; kitchens, bathrooms, and balconies house no one
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The price: living farther or smaller on the same budget.
- In plain terms: The minimum rental unit is the room as originally designed; kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and underground storage house no one. When such units get busted, you move. And mid-lease, landlords can't raise rent at will either.
- Benefit: The rules are plain: the original designed room is the minimum unit — no re-partitioning into sub-rooms; per-capita floor area no lower than the local minimum; kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and underground storage barred for habitation; and lessors may not unilaterally raise rent mid-lease
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 住房和城乡建设部 (2010). 商品房屋租赁管理办法（令第 6 号）第八条：「出租住房的，应当以原设计的房间为最小出租单位，人均租住建筑面积不得低于当地人民政府规定的最低标准。厨房、卫生间、阳台和地下储藏室不得出租供人员居住。」第九条：「出租人应当按照合同约定履行房屋的维修义务并确保房屋和室内设施安全……房屋租赁合同期内，出租人不得单方面随意提高租金水平。」<http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm>
- Note: When illegal partitions get busted, the tenant moves, deposit and prepaid rent usually unrecoverable. Safety suffers too: partition walls block escape routes, and crowded units share one circuit. Three things to check at viewing: walls added later, windows present, and meters sized for the headcount.

### 9. Urban hukou dreaming of country life: rent farmhouses only — never buy homestead land or the houses on it
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The price: the house never becomes yours, leases run 20 years maximum per signing, renewed by renegotiation.
- In plain terms: Urban-hukou residents may not buy rural homestead land, farmhouses, or "small-property" housing — state documents prohibit it outright. The village's "just write a contract" doesn't help: money out, house still not yours. For long country stays: lease, 20 years maximum per contract, renew on expiry.
- Benefit: The State Council notice is plain: "urban residents may not go to the countryside to purchase homestead land, farmers' dwellings, or 'small-property housing.'" The same document bars farmers who sold or leased out dwellings from new homestead applications. The 2019 central rural-office and agriculture-ministry document repeats it: "strictly prohibited for urban residents to purchase rural homestead land" — and provides the lawful path: urban residents may lease farmhouses for living or business, contracts capped at twenty years, renewable by new agreement
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2007). 关于严格执行有关农村集体建设用地法律和政策的通知（国办发〔2007〕71 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_2395.htm>；中央农村工作领导小组办公室、农业农村部 (2019). 关于进一步加强农村宅基地管理的通知（中农发〔2019〕11 号）第五、六部分. <https://www.moa.gov.cn/govpublic/NCJJTZ/201909/t20190920_6328397.htm>
- Note: Homestead land is allocated by the village to its own members only. Lease with a written contract covering term, rent, renovation rights, and how renovation costs settle at expiry. Twenty years is the cap — no "50-year" or "70-year" leases, no multi-year lump payments. The documents also ban villas, compounds, and private clubs on homestead land — don't fund "joint builds" with villagers.
