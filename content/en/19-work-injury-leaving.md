# 19. On the job, leaving the job, and work injuries

This section counts money: how it computes, what to sign, how deadlines run. The first three entries cover money due while still employed; six cover departure; seven cover occupational disease and work injury. Work-injury money runs an order of magnitude above layoffs, on harder deadlines — and occupational damage never comes back. Four entries close the section: workplace bullying, separation certificates, the income tax on severance, and moving social-insurance relations between cities.

### 1. Overtime at 1.5×, 2×, 3× — unpaid means a labor-inspection complaint, and late payment adds 50%–100%
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Routinely keep punch records, rosters, and overtime approvals; screenshot work-chat assignments too. The hard part: remembering monthly.
- In plain terms: Weekday overtime pays 1.5×; rest-day overtime without compensating rest, 2×; statutory holidays — New Year, Spring Festival, Labor Day, National Day — pay 3×, uncompensable by time off. Unpaid? Complain to labor inspection. Ordered to pay and still not paying adds 50%–100% of the arrears.
- Benefit: Statutory hours: no more than 8 daily, 44 weekly on average. Ordinary overtime generally caps at 1 hour daily; special reasons, 3 daily and 36 monthly. Three overtime bands: weekday extension, no less than 150% of wages; rest days without compensating rest, no less than 200%; statutory holidays, no less than 300% — the last with no compensatory option in the statute. Overtime arranged but unpaid draws a labor-administration payment order; overdue, an additional 50%–100% of the payable amount as compensation (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 劳动法（2018 年第二次修正，第三十六、四十一、四十三、四十四条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_d9aa750028b14b99a776cb93726a360d.html>（国家市场监督管理总局刊登）；全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第八十五条第三项）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: Rest days and statutory holidays count separately — statutory holidays number only the legally designated days; swapped long-weekend days count as rest days at 200%. "Overtime needs approval" and "I stayed voluntarily" don't cancel payment but complicate your proof — keep assignment and approval records. Comprehensive and flexible working-hour schemes need labor-administration approval, not company say-so. Dispute limitation: Section 8, item 19.

### 2. Annual leave by cumulative service: 5, 10, 15 days; untaken days pay 300% of the daily wage
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Total your years across all employers — contribution records prove them. The hard part: not signing the "voluntary waiver" form.
- In plain terms: Cumulative service 1–10 years: 5 days yearly; 10–20: 10; 20+: 15 — accumulated across employers. Where the employer can't schedule it and you agree to forgo, those days pay 3× daily wages. Sign the "voluntary waiver" and only normal wages remain.
- Benefit: Twelve months of continuous work earns leave. Days follow **cumulative** service across all employers: 1–10 years, 5 days; 10–20, 10; 20+, 15. Statutory holidays and rest days don't count against leave. Where work demands genuinely prevent scheduling and you consent to forgo, untaken days "pay annual-leave wages at 300% of the employee's daily wage income" — the 300% already including the ordinary day's pay, so the extra take-home is 200%. Daily wage = monthly wage ÷ 21.75; monthly wage averaged over the prior 12 months excluding overtime. Part-year entrants prorate by the remaining calendar days; departures settle by the year's worked duration, over-taken leave never clawed back. Neither scheduled nor paid: correction order, then, past deadline, the leave wages plus an equal sum as compensation (national; since 2008-01-01)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2007). 职工带薪年休假条例（国务院令第 514 号，第二、三、四、五、七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_859865.htm>；人力资源和社会保障部 (2008). 企业职工带薪年休假实施办法（人社部令第 1 号，第三、四、五、十、十一、十二、十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1265995.htm>
- Note: Cumulative service spans employers — don't count only the current one. **Only when the employee "for personal reasons and in writing forgoes leave" may the employer pay normal wages alone** — don't sign the waiver. Personal leave over 20 accumulated days without wage deduction suspends that year's leave; sick leave beyond the tier thresholds (2 months under 10 years' service, 3 at 10–20, 4 over 20) likewise. Family-visit, marriage-leave, bereavement, maternity, and work-injury recuperation don't consume annual leave.

### 3. Probation has legal caps, wages no less than 80%, and one probation only
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Minutes before signing, cross-check contract term against probation length.
- In plain terms: Contracts under 1 year: probation caps at 1 month; 1–3 years: 2 months; 3-plus and open-ended: 6 months. One probation per company — a second after transfer is illegal. Probation wages can't fall below 80% of the post's floor or the contracted wage, nor below local minimum wage.
- Benefit: Contracts of 3 months to under 1 year: probation no more than 1 month; 1 year to under 3: no more than 2; 3-plus and open-ended: no more than 6. Task-completion contracts and those under 3 months admit no probation. "One probation period per employer per worker." Contracts stating only a probation term: the probation fails and the term is the contract term. Probation pay "no less than 80% of the employer's lowest wage for the same post or the contracted wage, and no less than the local minimum wage." Overlong probation actually served pays additional compensation at the post-probation monthly wage for the excess. In-probation terminations require the statutorily listed grounds with reasons stated (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第十九、二十、二十一、八十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: Three common violations: a 1-year contract with 3-month probation; re-probation after transfer; and "no social insurance during probation" — insurance runs from day one, employer duties per Section 12, item 16. Probation dismissal isn't compensation-free: it turns on whether the ground is statutorily allowed; wrongful terminations compute 2N under item 6.

### 4. Laid off: compute N first — one month's wage per full year, half a month under six
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One calculator pass, ten minutes.
- In plain terms: Severance computes: one month's wage per full year; six months to a year counts as a year; under six months, half a month. Monthly wage means the trailing 12-month average, bonuses and allowances included. Wages above three times the local prior-year average cap at three times, with service capped at 12 years.
- Benefit: The statutory severance — the N everyone cites — computes: "one month's wage per full year... six months to less than one year counts as one year; less than six months, half a month's wage." Monthly wages above three times the local prior-year average employee wage cap at three times, with service capped at twelve years
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第四十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: Monthly wage means the 12-month pre-termination average — bonuses, allowances, and subsidies included, not base salary alone, the commonest undercount. Income tax on severance: item 18.

### 5. Terminated without 30 days' notice? One extra month's wage
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: Termination for post-illness incapacity, incompetence, or changed circumstances without 30 days' written notice adds one month's wage beyond N — the N+1. Negotiate it listed separately, never folded into N.
- Benefit: The law offers two routes: "thirty days' prior written notice to the worker" or "payment of an additional month's wage, after which the contract may be terminated." Immediate departure costs the extra month — the "notice pay," separate from N, computed on last month's wage while N uses the 12-month average: different bases
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十六、三十九、四十、四十一、四十六、四十七、八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>；国务院 (2008). 劳动合同法实施条例（国务院令第 535 号）第二十条：「用人单位依照劳动合同法第四十条的规定，选择额外支付劳动者一个月工资解除劳动合同的，其额外支付的工资应当按照该劳动者上一个月的工资标准确定」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_1107604.htm>
- Note: The extra month exists only under Article 40's three situations: post-medical-period incapacity for the original and reassigned work; incompetence persisting after training or transfer; and major change in objective circumstances failing negotiation. Economic layoffs (Article 41) require only 30 days' notice to the union or staff; mutually agreed terminations carry N; serious-misconduct dismissals carry nothing; unlawful termination carries 2N. Under Article 40: negotiate N and 1 separately.

### 6. Unlawful termination: compensation at double the severance standard
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free, but arbitration waits months. The hard part: staying the course.
- In plain terms: Unlawful terminations pay double the severance standard — the 2N. Whether unlawful depends on the facts; consult legal aid or a lawyer before choosing negotiation or arbitration.
- Benefit: The law: terminations violating it "shall pay compensation at twice the economic-compensation standard of Article 47" — compute one severance, then double it
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: This is the 2N. Unlawfulness is fact-specific — consult legal aid or a lawyer first (Section 7).

### 7. Never sign "voluntary resignation for personal reasons" — that signature forfeits N
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=yes benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: withstanding the pressure to sign on the spot.
- In plain terms: Signing "voluntary resignation for personal reasons" usually forfeits both severance and unemployment benefits. Resignations fall outside compensable grounds — save employer fault like wage arrears or unpaid insurance. Nothing forces same-day signing: sleep on it.
- Benefit: Severance grounds are statutorily enumerated; workers resigning on their own sit outside them, save statutory employer fault like arrears or unpaid insurance. Signed "voluntary resignation" usually forfeits severance and unemployment benefits together
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十八条、第四十六条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: The classic script is "sign now to smooth the process; compensation separately." Get it written into the agreement on the spot or don't sign. Refusing today carries no legal consequence — sleep on it.

### 8. Before leaving: save pay slips, attendance, contract, insurance records, and chats
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Half an hour of archiving. The hard part: remembering before you go.
- In plain terms: Once the laptop and accounts return, pay slips, attendance, contracts, insurance records, and chats are gone. Arbitration still turns on your own materials for wages and overtime. Save your own employment materials — company source code and client lists stay.
- Benefit: Returned equipment means unrecoverable records. Burden allocation may tilt employer-ward, but wages and overtime still ride on your own evidence
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献；维权路径见第 7 节
- Note: Save your own employment materials only — taking source, client lists, and technical documents risks Section 11. Demand the separation certificate too: item 17.

### 9. Before entering dust, noise, or chemical posts: check the contract for hazard disclosure; the three occupational health exams are employer-arranged and employer-paid
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. Read the occupational-hazard disclosure clause by clause at signing; keep a copy of every exam report. The hard part: asking aloud and collecting materials on the spot.
- In plain terms: The contract must state the post's occupational hazards, protections, and benefits truthfully; concealment licenses refusal without dismissal. Three exams — pre-employment, in-service, departure — are employer-arranged and employer-paid; without the departure exam, no termination.
- Benefit: The Occupational Disease Law requires four disclosures at signing: hazards the work may generate, consequences, protection measures, and benefits — "truthfully informing the worker and written into the labor contract, without concealment or deception." Later reassignment to undisclosed hazardous work requires renewed disclosure and contract amendment. On failure: "the worker has the right to refuse operations with occupational hazards, and the employer may not terminate the contract for that refusal." Three exams: pre-post, in-service, departure — "occupational health examination costs borne by the employer," results delivered in writing. No pre-post exam, no hazardous assignment; contraindicated workers, no contraindicated operations; work-related damage found means reassignment and proper placement. **"Workers without pre-departure occupational health examinations may not have their labor contracts terminated."** On departure you "have the right to obtain copies of your occupational health surveillance file, which the employer shall provide truthfully and free of charge, stamping the copies" (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 职业病防治法（2018 年修正，第三十三、三十五、三十六条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html>（北京市人民政府转载）
- Note: Three interview questions size up the employer: any occupational hazards in this post, will the contract state them, who pays the pre-post exam. Evasive answers mean your own risk math. The departure exam is the most missed — it bars termination and anchors any future occupational-disease diagnosis; collect the file copy before leaving. Occupational disease itself routes through work injury: items 11 onward.

### 10. Dust, noise, and chemical damage is irreversible: protective equipment must be employer-provided; unprotected work may be refused
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=yes benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: wearing respirators, earplugs, and goggles always, and refusing unprotected tasks — sometimes earning "difficult" from colleagues.
- In plain terms: Pneumoconiosis, noise deafness, and chemical eye burns are statutory occupational diseases; lungs and hearing never come back. You may know the hazards, demand protective facilities and equipment, and refuse unprotected work; retaliation in pay or dismissal is void.
- Benefit: Statutory occupational diseases, from 2025-08-01: 12 classes, 135 kinds, with 4 open clauses. Class one: pneumoconioses — silicosis, coal-worker's, graphite, carbon-black, asbestosis, talc, cement, mica, potter's, aluminum, welder's, founder's, plus an open clause. ENT-oral class: noise deafness and blast deafness. Eye class: chemical eye burns and arc-eye. All irreversible — no therapy restores lungs or hearing. Four on-the-spot rights: knowing the workplace's hazards, consequences, and protections; demanding compliant protective facilities, personal equipment, and improved conditions; criticizing and reporting violations and life-threatening practices; and "refusing unlawful directives and coerced work without occupational-disease protections" — with any wage, benefit, or contract retaliation for exercising these rights "void" (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委、人力资源社会保障部、国家疾控局、全国总工会 (2024). 职业病分类和目录（2025 年 8 月 1 日起实施）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202412/content_6992843.htm>；新版目录 12 大类 135 种的口径见国家卫生健康委 (2024). 新版目录将职业病调整为 12 大类 135 种. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202412/content_6992838.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2018). 职业病防治法（第三十九条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html>（北京市人民政府转载）
- Note: Match the mask: particulate respirators stop dust; gauze and surgical masks don't. No employer earplugs? Buy your own — a few yuan, hearing never returns. "Two years to save and go" fails for silicosis, which strikes years after leaving dust and keeps progressing — hence the departure exam's weight (item 9). The new catalog adds musculoskeletal disease (carpal tunnel, long-repetition manufacturing only) and mental-behavioral disorder (PTSD, emergency responders only). Chemical splashes: Section 13, item 21.

### 11. Injured at work or in a commute crash: work-injury determination first — the employer won't file, you file
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One trip to the social-insurance administration with three documents: the application form, proof of employment, and the medical diagnosis.
- In plain terms: On-the-job injuries count; commute crashes without primary fault count too. Without the determination, medical costs, recuperation wages, and disability benefits all vanish. The employer files within 30 days; unfiled, you, family, or the union file within a year.
- Benefit: Without determination, medical costs, wage-and-benefit-preserving recuperation pay, and disability subsidies all fall away. Past 30 days of employer silence, the interim benefits are the employer's own bill
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2010 修订). 工伤保险条例（国务院令第 586 号）第十四条列了七种「应当认定为工伤」，含「（六）在上下班途中，受到非本人主要责任的交通事故或者城市轨道交通、客运轮渡、火车事故伤害的」；第十七条「所在单位应当自事故伤害发生之日或者被诊断、鉴定为职业病之日起30日内，向统筹地区社会保险行政部门提出工伤认定申请」「用人单位未按前款规定提出工伤认定申请的，工伤职工或者其近亲属、工会组织在事故伤害发生之日或者被诊断、鉴定为职业病之日起1年内，可以直接向用人单位所在地统筹地区社会保险行政部门提出工伤认定申请」「用人单位未在本条第一款规定的时限内提交工伤认定申请，在此期间发生符合本条例规定的工伤待遇等有关费用由该用人单位负担」；第十九条「职工或者其近亲属认为是工伤，用人单位不认为是工伤的，由用人单位承担举证责任」；第二十条「自受理工伤认定申请之日起60日内作出工伤认定的决定」；第五十五条，对「工伤认定申请不予受理的决定不服的」「对工伤认定结论不服的」，「可以依法申请行政复议，也可以依法向人民法院提起行政诉讼」. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>；最高人民法院 (2014). 关于审理工伤保险行政案件若干问题的规定（法释〔2014〕9 号）第七条：「由于不属于职工或者其近亲属自身原因超过工伤认定申请期限的，被耽误的时间不计算在工伤认定申请期限内」，所列情形为「（一）不可抗力；（二）人身自由受到限制；（三）属于用人单位原因；（四）社会保险行政部门登记制度不完善；（五）当事人对是否存在劳动关系申请仲裁、提起民事诉讼」. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/6775.html>
- Note: Take the one year seriously — but delay not your own fault doesn't count: force majeure, restricted liberty, employer causes, defective registration, or labor-relation arbitration and litigation. Employer-dragged-past-deadline: file anyway, with evidence of the delay. Refused acceptance or adverse conclusion: administrative review or suit. Employer denial puts the burden on the employer — but keep your own materials: same-day photos, colleagues' contacts, treatment records, attendance. Article 16 lists three exclusions: intentional crime, drunkenness or drugs, self-harm or suicide. Article 15 adds "deemed work injury": death from sudden illness during work hours at the post, or rescue failing within 48 hours.

### 12. Don't believe "make it to your desk and it counts": sudden illness means 120 first, not clocking in
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: Rushing to the office during sudden illness buys no money. Deemed-work-injury requires onset during work hours at the post — en-route illness without arrival never qualified, and rescue past 48 hours disqualifies too. The minutes spent qualifying trade away your survival odds.
- Benefit: Deemed work injury requires onset "during work hours and at the work post," with death or failed rescue within 48 hours. Delaying care to satisfy two conditions pays with survival chances for compensation you may never collect
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工伤保险条例第十五条第一款第（一）项：「在工作时间和工作岗位，突发疾病死亡或者在48小时之内经抢救无效死亡的」视同工伤；第十四条第（六）项只把上下班途中「受到非本人主要责任的交通事故或者城市轨道交通、客运轮渡、火车事故伤害的」列为工伤，途中突发疾病不在其中. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Note: Both conditions simultaneously: during hours and at the post. En-route never applies; onset at the post followed by being sent home or to hospital with death within 48 hours may. It reads where illness struck, not where death came. Short videos recasting this as "reach the office for money" have it exactly backward — what changes outcomes is reaching a hospital within the first minutes. And 48 hours is a hard line: rescue past it disqualifies.

### 13. No work-injury insurance paid? The benefits still exist — the employer pays them in full at the same standard
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free, though employer denial means arbitration or suit, months minimum. The hard part: staying the course.
- In plain terms: Uninsured employment doesn't erase work-injury benefits — the employer pays the same items and standards in full. Non-enrollment is the employer's added penalty: back-payment orders, 0.05% daily late fees, and 1–3× arrears fines. "No insurance, no compensation" is a lie.
- Benefit: The regulation states plainly: employers that should have enrolled but didn't pay the work-injury benefits per the regulation's items and standards — the same sums, from the employer's own pocket
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工伤保险条例第六十二条第二款：「依照本条例规定应当参加工伤保险而未参加工伤保险的用人单位职工发生工伤的，由该用人单位按照本条例规定的工伤保险待遇项目和标准支付费用。」同条第一款：责令限期参加、补缴，「自欠缴之日起，按日加收万分之五的滞纳金；逾期仍不缴纳的，处欠缴数额1倍以上3倍以下的罚款」. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Note: "No insurance, no compensation" is the classic lie here. Non-enrollment only adds the employer's penalties; your benefits never shrink.

### 14. Once stable: labor-capacity assessment — disability grades convert straight into money
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One assessment process with waiting on both ends.
- In plain terms: Once the condition stabilizes, assess. Grades convert to money: the one-time disability subsidy runs on your wage — grade 5, 18 months; 6, 16; 7, 13; 8, 11; 9, 9; 10, 7. Before results, sign no "lump-sum settlement."
- Benefit: The one-time disability subsidy converts grades into months of wages paid at once: grades 5 and 6 at 18 and 16 months, 7 at 13, 8 at 11, 9 at 9, 10 at 7 of one's own wage
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工伤保险条例第二十一条「经治疗伤情相对稳定后存在残疾、影响劳动能力的，应当进行劳动能力鉴定」；第二十二条「劳动功能障碍分为十个伤残等级，最重的为一级，最轻的为十级」；第三十六条五、六级待遇（一次性伤残补助金 18/16 个月本人工资，按月伤残津贴为本人工资的 70%/60%）；第三十七条七至十级待遇（13/11/9/7 个月本人工资）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Note: Grades 5 and 6 retain the employment relationship with monthly disability allowances; grades 7–10 draw two further sums on contract expiry or worker-initiated termination — the fund's one-time medical subsidy and the employer's one-time employment subsidy, provincially set. Before the assessment lands, sign no "final settlement."

### 15. Work-related death: three sums — funeral grant, dependents' pension, one-time death benefit
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free, one round of paperwork.
- In plain terms: Work death pays three sums — negotiate all three. Funeral grant: 6 months of the locality's prior-year average monthly wage. Dependents' pension: monthly on the deceased's wage — spouse 40%, other dependents 30% each. One-time death benefit: 20× the prior-year national urban per-capita disposable income — one number nationwide.
- Benefit: Three sums. First, the funeral grant: 6 months of the pooling area's prior-year average employee monthly wage. Second, dependents' pensions: monthly on the deceased's wage — spouse 40%, other dependents 30% each, childless elders and orphans plus 10 points. Third, the one-time death benefit: 20× the prior-year national urban per-capita disposable income, one figure nationwide, region-blind. For 2026 work deaths, that sum runs about 1.13 million yuan (1,130,040) — the prior year's 56,502 yuan × 20
- Evidence grade: A

### 16. Long-run bullying, humiliation, obstruction at work: don't just endure — document first, then complaint, police, or arbitration by nature
