# 11. Red lines for programmers and technical people

This section's metrics are personal freedom and money — what returns is "don't get locked up" and "don't pay damages." Coders and technical people often take what follows as "merely technical questions," but they map to specific crimes or specific civil liabilities. Each entry gives statutory numbers and cases published on the Supreme People's Procuratorate and Supreme People's Court sites. The criminal law follows the Beijing traffic-authority reprint "as amended by Amendment XI." Entries run by "easiest for technical people to assume it's fine × heaviest consequences." Item 1 is the universal one: how to judge before acting, and what to do first when pursued.

### 1. Before acting, ask one question: would the vendor, platform, or company report it? On pursuit, get a criminal lawyer immediately
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Replace "is it technically possible" with: would the vendor, platform, or company report it? The hard part: resisting "nobody bothers with something this small" — server logs, payment records, and group chats all surface by default in investigations.
- In plain terms: Ask before acting: would they report? Logs, payments, and chats are all retrievable. Under criminal detention, the arrest decision comes within 37 days at most. From the first questioning you may retain a lawyer — those 37 days are when a criminal lawyer matters most.
- Benefit: Any unit or individual discovering crime may report it; investigators finding crime facts shall file. After detention, police request arrest approval within 3 days (formal arrest by the procuratorate), extendable 1–4; itinerant offenders extend to 30; the procuratorate decides within 7 more — 37 days total at most. From first interrogation or compulsory measures, you may retain counsel; during police investigation, only lawyers. Requested meetings: within 48 hours at the detention center. Those facing control, custody, or supplementary punishment only may get bail: pay a deposit or find a guarantor and wait at home — bail up to 12 months (national)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 全国人大 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十九、六十七、七十九、九十一、一百一十、一百一十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>
- Note: The 37 days run from detention to the arrest decision — not a custody cap; post-arrest investigation detention follows. The lawyer's leverage peaks in those 37 days: meeting, submitting a "no-arrest" opinion, seeking bail. Post-indictment is late. Every other entry in this section is the same question: does the other side have grounds to report?

### 2. Never build, sell, or distribute game cheats and assists — including "screen-reading only, no memory access" AI helpers — nor modified clients adding features to apps like WeChat
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. The price is walking away from these gigs and "little tools." The hard part: the money comes fast, and you can't see who'd pursue.
- In plain terms: Selling cheats to 20 person-times, or earning 5,000 yuan, reaches up to 3 years; especially serious, 3–7. One wallhack/aimbot maker sold 80,000-odd yuan in 3 months — 1 year suspended 1, fine 5,000. A private-server operator collected 20-million-odd yuan of top-ups — top sentence 4 years, fines up to 10 million yuan.
- Benefit: Providing programs or tools specifically for intruding into or illegally controlling computer systems, serious: up to 3 years plus or instead of fines; especially serious, 3–7 plus fines. "Serious" thresholds: tools to 20+ person-times, or illegal gains of 5,000+. Deliberately circumventing or destroying rights-holders' technical protections may also constitute the copyright crime — up to 3 years, serious 3–10. Official cases: Wang's group built wallhack/aimbot cheats reading and modifying game memory, selling 80,000-odd yuan in 3 months for 30,000-odd profit — 1 year suspended 1, fine 5,000. Xiang sold the "TZ" cheat series for 200,000-odd yuan — 3 years suspended 3, fine 20,000. Liu's group obtained a game server program, tweaked parameters, ran a private server collecting 20-million-odd yuan in top-ups — 4 years down to 2 years 6 months, fines 10 million down to 1.3 million. Not just games: a Shanghai company's ROM added dozens of WeChat functions — auto-forwarding, liking, mass-messaging — selling 2,381 "marketing phones"; in 2019 Xuhui court fined the company 500,000 yuan for copyright infringement, and the executive drew 1 year 6 months suspended 1 year 6 months (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第三款、第二百一十七条第六项）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第三条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>（深圳市公安局转载）；最高人民检察院 (2026). 上海静安：严惩制售游戏外挂黑灰产共护清朗网络空间. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202608/t20260825_735480.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2021). 2020 年度检察机关保护知识产权典型案例（案例五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202104/t20210425_516525.shtml>；国家版权局 (2020). 2019 年度上海十大版权典型案件发布（上海市版权局发布，案例八：上海昱宫网络科技有限公司等侵犯著作权案）. <https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/ywxx/202004/t20200430_47269.html>
- Note: Published cheat cases all involve memory read/write and anti-cheat bypass. "Screenshot-only, key-simulation" assists have no published precedent — but item 3's ticket case shows request-only, no-memory work still charges as illegal data acquisition. Whether and under which count is the investigators' call on the vendor's materials, not the author's. Twenty person-times is a very small number.

### 3. No writing or selling ticket-grabbing, flash-sale, brushing, or coupon-farming scripts — even "just automated button-clicking"
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free, no time.
- In plain terms: Scripts that touch no memory and break nothing — merely faster than humans — still convict: 5,000 yuan earned or 10,000 yuan of loss suffices. One man used bought 12306 accounts to grab 200-odd tickets for 20,000-odd yuan — 1 year 6 months suspended, fine 5,000. A shop selling ticket-grabbing software paid 20,000 yuan in unfair-competition damages.
- Benefit: Intruding into computer systems or acquiring data by other technical means, serious: up to 3 years plus or instead of fines; especially serious, 3–7. "Serious": gains of 5,000+, or losses of 10,000+. Official cases: one man used cheats to batch-import bought 12306 accounts and flood purchase requests multithreaded, grabbing 200-odd tickets for 20,000-odd yuan — illegal acquisition of computer-system data, 1 year 6 months suspended 1 year 6 months, fine 5,000. Zheng sold ticket-grabbing software for a ticketing app in an online shop — unfair competition, 20,000 yuan damages (national)
- Evidence grade: A

### 4. Crawlers: public, login-free pages only — no anti-crawl bypassing, no personal information, no selling what you scrape
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. A crawler is a program that walks pages and extracts data. Check the robots file (the site's root-level note of what may not be scraped) and API terms first. Anything needing login, tokens (temporary access credentials), or signature-breaking: don't. The hard part: the data is right there technically — restraint is all on you.
- In plain terms: Login-, token-, or signature-gated data counts as bypassing protection: up to 3 years, serious 3–7. Personal information is worse: 50 records of location, communications, credit, or assets is already serious; lodging, health, and transaction records at 500; other categories at 5,000; or 5,000 yuan earned.
- Benefit: Bypassing protections for system data matches item 3's sentencing: up to 3 years, or 3–7. Illegally acquiring, selling, or providing citizens' personal information reaches "serious" at any of: 50+ records of whereabouts, communication content, credit, or property; 500+ of lodging, communication logs, health, or transactions; 5,000+ of other personal information; or 5,000+ yuan gains. Serious: up to 3 years plus or instead of fines; especially serious, 3–7. Official cases: developer Li disguised phone-album harvesting as a "looks-rating" app, taking 1,751 face photos and reselling "social-work database" records — 3 years suspended 3. A social-work database pools leaked personal information from everywhere. A network company crawled a food-delivery platform's store and order data, causing 40,000-odd yuan of loss — prosecuted for illegal data acquisition (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款、第二百五十三条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2017). 关于办理侵犯公民个人信息刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（第三、四、五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/201705/t20170509_190088.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 检察机关依法惩治侵犯公民个人信息犯罪典型案例（案例二）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202212/t20221207_594915.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>
- Note: The 2017 interpretation counts "providing lawfully collected information to others without consent" as "providing" — exporting the user table to a partner company falls inside. Five thousand records is a small table.

### 5. No dev contracts for gambling, fraud, or porn apps; no building payment channels or unblocking accounts for "clients"
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. The price: walking away from high-priced, vaguely-specified outsourcing. The hard part: the pay often towers over normal contracts.
- In plain terms: High-quote, vague-requirement jobs make you an accomplice of the gambling ring: casino-operation charges, up to 5 years, serious 5–10 — heavier than the 3-year cap for mere helping. One e-sports betting app's 28 coders drew 1 to 3 years 6 months. A QQ-unblocker for fraud rings drew 1 year 6 months plus a 20,000-yuan fine.
- Benefit: Knowingly providing technical support or payment settlement for cybercrime, serious: up to 3 years plus or instead of fines; simultaneously constituting other crimes, the heavier count governs. Running casinos: up to 5 years plus fines; serious, 5–10. Official cases: the Zhangsheng group developed gambling games for "turnkey export," collecting site-building and reporting fees — nearly 7 billion yuan involved; Zhangsheng drew 9 years 9 months plus a 50-million-yuan fine, and outsourced-development head Wang Qiang and 78 others drew 6 months to 9 years 9 months. Chen Dong's team built and ran an e-sports betting app, coders in Hainan — 28 people drew 3 years 6 months to 1 year for casino operation plus fines. Zhang ran a studio unblocking fraud-ring QQ accounts — 1 year 6 months, fine 20,000 (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十七条之二、第三百零三条第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2023). 建赌博网站并出售，涉案近 70 亿元. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202307/t20230711_621248.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 上海闵行区：办理一起新型开设网络赌场案. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202208/t20220805_569952.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 依法惩治帮助信息网络犯罪活动及相关犯罪典型案例（案例一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202507/t20250728_702377.shtml>
- Note: "Knowing" doesn't require them to say it aloud: far-above-market quotes, recharge-withdraw-odds functionality, and purpose-evasive clients all feed the inference. Gambling-app developers charge as casino-operation accomplices — not the 3-year-capped aiding count. ("Aiding" is short for the aiding-cybercrime offense.)

### 6. On leaving, hand over accounts and access — no database deletion, backdoors, or password-locking the system, even if the company owes you
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Owed wages go through labor arbitration (Section 7), not your own hands. The hard part: not touching the system in anger.
- In plain terms: Company-issued credentials don't stay usable after departure — logging in counts as illegal control, up to 3 years. Deleting data that stops the system: up to 5 years; especially grave consequences, 5+. One dismissed senior engineer used his old admin account to delete data and logs — 120,000-odd yuan of loss, 3 years suspended 5, fine 30,000.
- Benefit: Illegally controlling computer systems, serious: up to 3 years plus or instead of fines; especially serious, 3–7. Deleting or altering functions or data causing system failure, grave consequences: up to 5 years; especially grave, 5+. Official cases: IT senior engineer Lü, dismissed, logged into the company's shared server with his old admin account, changed the password, deleted data and logs — 120,000-odd yuan loss; 3 years suspended 5, fine 30,000. Software employee Bai planted faulty code into an upgrade program, locking customers out — 40,000-odd loss, prosecuted for destroying computer systems (national)
- Evidence grade: A

### 7. On leaving, no source code, client lists, or technical docs — no personal cloud drives, no reuse at the next employer
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Wipe company materials from personal machines and drives before leaving. The hard part: some files you wrote line by line.
- In plain terms: Code you wrote yourself belongs to the company, and client lists are trade secrets — taking them is taking company property. One leaver carried security-scanner source to a new employer: 5 years plus a 500,000-yuan fine, and the new company fined 4 million. Another exploited a vulnerability to download 33,000-odd design files — 1 year 10 months. The company can sue you separately.
- Benefit: Breaching confidentiality duties by disclosing or using trade secrets, serious: up to 3 years plus or instead of fines; especially serious, 3–10 plus fines. The Anti-Unfair Competition Law adds: acquiring secrets by "electronic intrusion" (technical means into systems), and instigating or helping others breach confidentiality. Official cases: Sun and departing colleagues carried security-scanner source and core algorithms into the new employer's products — Sun 5 years plus a 500,000-yuan fine, the new company 4 million. Tian exploited a system flaw to download 33,000-odd design files — 1 year 10 months plus 100,000. Engineer Yang stored app-module source on personal computer and cloud, then built a competing app — prosecuted for trade-secret infringement (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百一十九条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 反不正当竞争法（2025 年修订，第十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699862.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关知识产权保护典型案例（案例七）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202504/t20250423_693691.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2020). 2019 年度检察机关保护知识产权典型案例（案例十六）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202004/t20200425_459623.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>

### 9. No testing others' systems without written authorization — "good intent" and "reported it afterward" are not defenses
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Before joining an SRC (a vendor's own security-response center receiving outside reports) or crowd-testing, obtain written scope, target lists, and time windows. On confirming a vulnerability, stop. The hard part: giving up "just trying one thing."
- In plain terms: Whether it convicts turns on authorization, not intent. 500 sets of ordinary credentials, or 5,000 yuan earned or 10,000 yuan loss, reaches up to 3 years. Exporting one user table to prove a vulnerability often clears 500 sets in a single go. Credentials in hand, use beyond scope still counts as intrusion.
- Benefit: Ordinary company and personal systems sit outside state-affairs, defense, and cutting-edge-science systems. Intruding into them or acquiring stored, processed, or transmitted data by technical means, serious: up to 3 years or custody plus or instead of fines; especially serious, 3–7 plus fines. "Serious": 10+ sets of payment, securities, or futures authentication credentials; 500+ sets of other credentials; 20+ systems controlled; 5,000+ yuan gains or 10,000+ yuan loss. Five times those numbers reaches "especially serious." Official case: Guiding Case 36 established that "logging into a computer system with credentials beyond the authorized scope constitutes intrusion." The three defendants used work-held credentials and tokens to log in remotely, download out-of-scope data, and sell it — 37,000 yuan gains; sentences of 4 years, 3 years 9 months, and 4 years, each fined 40,000 (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第一款、第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第一条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>（深圳市公安局转载）；最高人民检察院 (2017). 第九批指导性案例（检例第 36 号，卫梦龙、龚旭、薛东东非法获取计算机信息系统数据案）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/jczdal/201710/t20171017_202593.shtml>；央广网 (2016-06-29). 白帽子发布漏洞后被抓 世纪佳缘否认"钓鱼"使其上套（记者赵珂，实习记者刘香玺）. <https://m.cnr.cn/news/yctt/20160629/t20160629_522531823.html>（网页存档 <https://web.archive.org/web/20261005043859/https://m.cnr.cn/news/yctt/20160629/t20160629_522531823.html>）

### 10. Report vulnerabilities per the rules — no pre-patch detail disclosure, no exploit tools, no handing to foreign parties
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Report to the product provider or the official platform. Until the vendor patches: no details, no proof-of-concept code.
- In plain terms: Individuals fall under the Network Product Security Vulnerability Regulations too. Before the vendor ships a fix, you may not publish the vulnerability or details of live systems' flaws, may not release exploit programs or tools, and when publishing must state the fix. Undisclosed vulnerabilities may not go to offshore organizations or individuals beyond the vendor.
- Benefit: The regulations govern three classes: domestic network-product providers, network operators, and organizations or individuals discovering, collecting, or publishing vulnerabilities. No one may use vulnerabilities to harm network security or illegally collect, sell, or publish vulnerability information. Public release obeys five rules: not before the provider ships remedies; no details of vulnerabilities in operators' live networks, systems, and devices; no exaggeration, hype, or fraud; no release of exploitation tools; and simultaneous publication of fixes or defenses. Undisclosed vulnerabilities may not be provided to offshore organizations or individuals other than the provider. The regulations encourage reporting to four official platforms: MIIT's cybersecurity threat and vulnerability sharing platform; the National Network and Information Security Notification Center's platform; CNCERT's platform; and the China Information Technology Security Evaluation Center's database. Violations are handled by MIIT and MPS; Network Security Law cases follow that law. Publishing system vulnerabilities must follow national rules — violators face correction orders and warnings plus 10,000–100,000 yuan; refusal or severity, 100,000–1 million, plus possible business suspension, rectification, site or app closure, and license revocation; directly responsible managers, 10,000–100,000 (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工业和信息化部、国家互联网信息办公室、公安部 (2021). 网络产品安全漏洞管理规定（工信部联网安〔2021〕66 号，第二、四、九、十、十四条，2021 年 9 月 1 日施行）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5641351.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第二十八、六十五条；2016 年文本为第二十六、六十二条，规定第十四条援引的是 2016 年文本的条号）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>
- Note: The regulations name individuals — "just an amateur" is no exception. Submitting domestic-system bugs to offshore bounty platforms lands squarely on "undisclosed vulnerabilities shall not be provided offshore." Authorization to test (item 9) and handling of found vulnerabilities are separate questions: a lawfully found vulnerability can still be punished for how it's published, and an unauthorized test is wrong at both ends. The most-used channels: MIIT's platform and CNCERT. The regulations don't address "vendor notified but won't fix" — in practice, document the negotiation and file with the official platforms in parallel.

### 11. No selling circumvention tools or VPN accounts, no building such nodes for others

- Cost: Free, no time.
- In plain terms: Self-built lines abroad, or international channels outside the state public network, draw police-ordered disconnection, warnings, fines up to 15,000 yuan plus confiscation. Making a business of it can charge as illegal business operation — serious, up to 5 years. This entry covers selling and node-building; personal use caps at warning plus fine.
- Benefit: Direct international networking must use the state public telecom network's international gateways. Violations: police-ordered disconnection and warning, plus up to 15,000 yuan and confiscation. ### 12. Signed a non-compete? If the company stops paying monthly, demand in writing; 3 months unpaid means you may rescind; roles never touching trade secrets may seek invalidation
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Keep the agreement's original and evidence of non-payment at departure.
- In plain terms: Non-compete means no working for competitors for up to 2 years post-departure, with monthly compensation. Unspecified amounts run at 30% of the prior 12 months' average wage, floored at local minimum. Three months unpaid for company reasons lets you seek rescission. From 2025, roles never exposed to trade secrets may seek invalidation.
- Benefit: Non-competes bind only senior management, senior technical personnel, and others with confidentiality duties, for at most 2 years, with mandatory monthly compensation. Unspecified: 30% of the trailing 12-month average wage, not below local minimum. Three months unpaid for company reasons lets the worker rescind. Company-initiated rescission costs an extra 3 months. From 2025, workers whose roles never involved trade secrets may seek invalidation; scopes exceeding what they actually knew are void in excess. Official case: a pharmaceutical company sued its CTO for 7.1 million yuan in penalties; the court found the new employer's product non-substitutive and the scope limited to what he actually knew — claim dismissed entirely (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2012). 劳动合同法（2012 年修正，第二十三、二十四条）. <https://yjglj.fushun.gov.cn/008/20260622/d846d3c2-df3d-4c4c-af16-898ede43e1ee.html>（抚顺市应急管理局转载）；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）（第三十六至四十条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/282121.html>；最高人民法院 (2025). 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（二）（法释〔2025〕12 号，第十三至十五条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/472691.html>；最高人民法院 (2025). 劳动争议典型案例（案例四）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/472681.html>

### 13. Code written on work time with company resources belongs to the company; side projects on your own time and devices, no company code mixed in
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Read your contract's IP clauses at onboarding. The hard part: side projects and work often share one machine — separate them.
- In plain terms: Software built mainly on company equipment, intranet, or business data, with the company answerable, belongs to the company — you keep the authorship right, contract or no. Off-hours code that used the company laptop or data may still count as the company's. Inventions made with company resources patent to the company too.
- Benefit: Works created to fulfill work tasks are works-for-hire. Ordinarily copyright stays with the author, the unit enjoys priority use within business scope for 2 years. But two classes differ: works mainly using the unit's material-technical conditions with the unit responsible — engineering designs, software — and works contractually assigned to the unit; in both, authors keep only the attribution right. "Material-technical conditions" means company equipment, intranet, business data. Inventions from the unit's tasks or mainly its resources are service inventions — patent application rights and granted patents belong to the unit (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第十八条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 专利法（2020 年修正，第六条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308800.html>
- Note: Software is written directly into the "copyright to the unit" class — programmers almost never land in the "author keeps copyright, unit gets 2-year priority" band. "Off-hours" code that touched company machines, intranet, or data may still count as mainly unit-resourced. Clean side projects: your own machine, no company repos, no business overlap.

### 14. Using GPL-family open source in products: comply with the license or swap libraries — don't bet on "nobody enforces"
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Read the license (the author's stated terms) before adopting code. The GPL family requires your product's source released too, clashing with closed-source sales — clash means replace. The hard part: dropping a comfortable library.
- In plain terms: Copyright infringement pays the other side's loss or your gain; intentional and serious, 1–5×. One company hired a rival's ex-employees and shipped substantially similar software, arguing the rival used GPLv2 and should have opened its source anyway. The Supreme Court held the rival's own GPL compliance irrelevant to its copyright — injunction plus 500,000 yuan.
- Benefit: Infringement damages run on the right-holder's actual loss or the infringer's gain; both unclear, by reference to a normal license price. Intentional and serious: 1–5×. Public-interest harm adds confiscation and fines. Official case: (2021) SPC IP-Final-51 — the defendant hired the plaintiff's ex-employees and built substantially similar gateway software, arguing the plaintiff's GPLv2 base required openness; the SPC held "whether the developer itself violated GPLv2 and whether it holds software copyright are two independent questions," granting the injunction and 500,000 yuan (national)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第五十三、五十四条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；最高人民法院知识产权法庭 (2022). 涉「开源软件」著作权侵权案（（2021）最高法知民终 51 号）. <https://ipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-3042.html>

### 15. Apps collect only business-necessary personal information; under-14s need guardian consent; write the privacy policy clearly

### 16. Before launching a site or app: ICP filing first, and keep 6+ months of logs per multi-level protection

### 18. No tools that auto-skip or block other apps' ads — free and offline can still be unfair competition
