# 12. Starting a business: don't bet the family savings

This section counts money and legal liability, not lifespan. Opening a shop or founding a company — which steps drag your own money, the family's money, and your spouse's money down together? What does the law say, and can it be ring-fenced in advance? Entries that most easily sink the family savings and are cheapest to avoid come first — hence signing entries up front, DIY methods later; experience-only, literature-free entries carry Grade C.

Entries in this section are grouped by theme, with item numbers in parentheses.

**Before opening, don't stake the household**: start only with money you can lose (item 1), no personal guarantees on company loans (item 2), choose the right entity (item 3), never a nominee shareholder (item 4), check franchise filing first (item 5), ask before paying shop-agency operators (item 24).

**Registration and licenses**: fix name and business scope before registering (item 6), no opening before the license arrives (item 7).

**Making food**: find your food tier first (item 8), prepackaged-food labels (item 9), ordinary food may not claim to cure (item 10), food's criminal lines (item 11).

**Tax and invoices**: file zero returns even without revenue (item 12), invoice only real transactions (item 13), anyone claiming to be the tax bureau demanding money is a scammer (item 14).

**Contracts and staffing**: earnest money, not "reservation money" (item 15), contracts within the first month of employment (item 16), wages per contract and law (item 17), follow procedures on the job (item 22).

**Product and sourcing**: sell first, then build (item 18), samples before mass-production lists (item 19), keep invoices and supplier info (item 20), make or license your designs (item 21).

**Exit**: losing means exiting by procedure (item 23).

### 1. Start a business only with money you can afford to lose — never the household's, never borrowed
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Before starting, set your number: an amount whose total loss still leaves the mortgage, the children, and medical care intact. Beyond that, don't invest. Family members' money and borrowed money don't count toward the number. The hard part: not adding more just as the business looks promising.
- In plain terms: Sole-proprietor debts are repaid from your own assets — and if it can't be separated from a family business, from the family's. Companies aren't safe either: when the company can't pay, the capital you subscribed but haven't paid in gets called early. So size the investment by "fully lost, mortgage and medical care unaffected."
- Benefit: Losing means more than money gone. A sole proprietor's debts, if the business is run alone, are repaid from personal property; if run by the family or inseparably, from family property. Debts signed by both spouses are joint; one spouse's after-the-fact nod counts too; a loan in one name that the lender proves funded the couple's joint business is joint as well. Companies carry their own claw: when the company can't pay due debts, the shareholder's subscribed-but-unpaid capital can be demanded early (national)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Note: "Affordable to lose" means: fully lost, you sell nothing and borrow from nobody. Official survival-rate and lifespan statistics weren't verifiable on the statistics and market-regulation sites this round — no numbers, legal consequences only, hence B.

### 2. Never sign personal guarantees on company loans — spouses least of all
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. When the bank, supplier, or landlord demands a "shareholder joint personal guarantee" or "spouse's signature," pause. Signing that page waives, in your own hand, the "company debts don't reach the individual" shield (limited liability). Don't sign if avoidable; unavoidable, sign only "ordinary guarantee" with a capped amount. The hard part: withstanding "no signature, no loan."
- In plain terms: Signing "joint and several guarantee" means the creditor can skip the company and come straight for you. "Ordinary guarantee" means suing the company first, executing its assets, and only the shortfall reaching you. A spouse's signature stakes the household — the "limited" of limited company stops counting for you personally.
- Benefit: The sentence on the guarantee means "if the debtor doesn't pay, you do." Under "joint and several," the creditor may bypass the company and knock on your door. Under "ordinary," the creditor must sue or arbitrate first and have the company's assets forcibly sold; only the remainder reaches you. Debts signed by both spouses are joint; one spouse's ratification counts; a signed spouse means the household stakes together (national, since 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第六百八十一、六百八十七、六百八十八、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Note: Small-business bank loans almost always demand shareholder joint guarantees. Borrow you still may — just know what the page waives, and keep the guaranteed amount inside item 1's affordable-loss number. Guarantees generally: Section 8, item 18.

### 3. Choose the right entity before opening: sole proprietors and general partners pay to the end; only the limited company is "limited"
<!-- costs: money=low time=medium will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Registration runs on the local government-services site, hours once. A limited company then means monthly bookkeeping and tax filing — a permanent cost: your time or paid agency, priced locally.
- In plain terms: Sole proprietors and general partners pay to the end — every debt, however long it takes. A limited company's shareholders pay only what they subscribed. But subscribed means genuinely paid within 5 years: writing 1 million is a potential 1-million debt. Mixing company and personal accounts voids the "limited."
- Benefit: Sole proprietors' debts run against personal or family property. General partners bear unlimited joint liability for partnership debts — every debt, and creditors may seize any one partner for the whole. Limited partnerships differ: limited partners lose only their subscribed capital, as do limited-company shareholders. But "limited" fails in three ways: subscribed capital must be paid in within 5 years of formation, callable early on default; withdrawn capital must return; and the company-as-shell — including a single-shareholder company unable to prove separate books — makes you jointly liable personally (national, since July 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2006). 合伙企业法（2006 年修订，第二条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413955.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四、二十三、四十七、五十三、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Note: "Limited" presupposes clean separation: no mixing company and personal accounts, no company money for household spending, complete books. Don't inflate registered capital for looks: subscribed means paid within 5 years, and 1 million written is 1 million owed.

### 4. Never be a nominee shareholder, never hold shares for others
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. "Lend your ID for a shareholder seat" or "park the shares under your name" — refuse both.
- In plain terms: Your name on the register makes you the legal shareholder. The subscribed capital must arrive on schedule; shortfall means making it up plus compensating the company. Other founders' unpaid capital at founding becomes your joint debt for the gap. They run the business; the debts land on you.
- Benefit: Registered shareholders are legal shareholders: subscribed capital must be paid in full per the articles' schedule; late payment means makeup plus damages to the company. Founding shareholders' unpaid capital draws the other founders into joint liability for the gap. On the company's default, creditors can demand the unpaid balance early. The real operator stays off the register; the debts stay on yours (national, since July 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、四十九、五十、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 修正). 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的规定（三）（法释〔2011〕3 号，2014 年法释〔2014〕2 号、2020 年法释〔2020〕18 号两次修正，第二十四、二十五条）：「如无法律规定的无效情形，人民法院应当认定该合同有效」「实际出资人未经公司其他股东半数以上同意，请求公司变更股东……人民法院不予支持」「名义股东处分股权造成实际出资人损失，实际出资人请求名义股东承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持」. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181799def980179ac07a9ca117c>（国家法律法规数据库）；最高人民法院 (2025). 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的解释（征求意见稿）第九十条. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/477881.html>
- Note: Nominee agreements bind between the parties absent invalidity grounds. But the real investor's name swap needs majority-shareholder consent; a nominee selling or pledging the shares routes through good-faith acquisition — an unknowing buyer at fair price with registration done keeps the shares, leaving the investor to sue the nominee. A new interpretation's draft appeared September 2025, repealing this one upon effect — the 2020 text governs as of 2026-09. Nominee legal representatives: Section 8, item 28.

### 5. Before franchising, check the commerce ministry's filing, get written disclosure, and write a "cooling-off" into the contract
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Check the brand's filing on MOFCOM's commercial-franchise information system, then demand the written disclosure and contract before signing. Hours.
- In plain terms: The brand must run 2 of its own stores for 1+ year, and file with commerce authorities within 15 days of first signing. Before signing, it must hand you 12 items in writing — fee structure, deposit refunds, how existing stores perform. No filing, no disclosure, or "pay first, then read the contract" — walk away.
- Benefit: The regulation requires the franchisor to operate at least 2 directly owned stores for over 1 year, and file within 15 days of the first franchise contract. Non-filing: 10,000–50,000 yuan fines; overdue, 50,000–100,000 plus public notice. Pre-signing, it must deliver 12 written items, including which fees and amounts, deposit-refund conditions, store counts and performance, 2 years of audited summaries, and 5 years of litigation and arbitration. The contract must state the franchisee's unilateral withdrawal window; concealment or false information makes the contract rescindable (national, since May 2007)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2007). 商业特许经营管理条例（国务院令第 485 号，第七、八、十二、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_4179.htm>；商务部 (2012). 商业特许经营信息披露管理办法（商务部令 2012 年第 2 号，第五、九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2177025.htm>；商务部. 商业特许经营信息管理系统. <https://txjy.syggs.mofcom.gov.cn/>
- Note: No filing, no disclosure documents, pay-before-reading — walk. The cooling-off is the post-signing unilateral-exit window; its length is contractual, the regulation saying only "a certain period," so negotiate the days in. Question the sources of "average sales, gross, and net" figures — the disclosure rules require sourcing. "Operation agency" and "shop-opening training" fee models: item 24.
### 6. Before registering: fix the name, premises, business scope, and registered capital — complete documents mean the license on the spot
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Registration itself is free — enterprise and sole-proprietor registration fees stopped 2015-01-01. File on the local government-services site or at the hall, hours once. Seal carving, bookkeeping agency, and registered-address hosting are market services, not government fees.
- In plain terms: Registration costs nothing — fees stopped in 2015. Five items only: application, identity or qualification proof, premises documents, articles or partnership agreement (sole proprietors exempt), plus any legally named extras. Complete documents issue the license on the spot; otherwise within 3 working days. Any fee you're charged is mostly an agent's errand fee.
- Benefit: The regulation fixes five items: "(1) application; (2) applicant qualification documents or natural-person identity; (3) domicile or main business-premises documents; (4) articles of association for companies, non-company legal persons, and specialized farmer cooperatives, or partnership agreements; (5) other materials required by law, administrative regulation, and the market-regulation department." In plain words: application; who-you-are papers; premises proof; articles or partnership agreement; legal extras. Companies additionally file appointments and IDs of the legal representative, directors, supervisors, and management. Sole proprietors skip articles. Names are "independently declared by applicants" — your own. Registered capital uses the subscription system: write the promise, pay later. Business scope picks from the registry's published categories. Complete and correct documents: "confirmed and registered on the spot"; otherwise "within 3 working days," extendable 3 more for complexity. "The license issuance date is the market entity's founding date" (national, since March 2022)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2021). 市场主体登记管理条例（国务院令第 746 号，第十、十三、十四、十六、十九、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5636139.htm> ; 市场监管总局 (2022). 市场主体登记管理条例实施细则（市场监管总局令第 52 号，第十二、二十五、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/02/content_5676403.htm> ; 财政部、国家发展改革委 (2014). 关于取消、停征和免征一批行政事业性收费的通知（财税〔2014〕101 号，附件 1）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2014-12/23/content_5023513.htm>
- Note: Registration itself is free; charged fees are mostly agent errands. Seal prices are market-set — the State Council forbids designating seal makers, so "must carve at the designated shop" needn't be accepted. Registered capital sets your liability ceiling, payable within 5 years — item 3. Licensed scope items: no opening before the license — item 7.
### 7. Businesses needing licenses don't open before the license arrives
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Before opening, check whether your trade needs a license. Food service and food sales need the food-operation license; selling only others' fully packaged food needs no license — filing instead. Open after the license lands.
- In plain terms: Opening unlicensed draws, at the light end, closure orders, confiscation of earnings, and up to 10,000 yuan. Trading in state-monopolized goods or running securities, futures, insurance, or payment settlement without approval, serious, constitutes the illegal-business crime from 5 years down. "Renovate first, license later" and "soft opening before the license" are the commonest stumble.
- Benefit: Operating without the required license draws enforcement by the designated authority. Operating without a business license where the law sets no specific penalty: shutdown, confiscation of illegal gains, plus up to 10,000 yuan. Heavier: dealing in law-designated monopoly or restricted goods without permission, or running securities, futures, insurance, or payment settlement unapproved — serious cases constitute the illegal-business crime: up to 5 years or custody plus fines of 1–5× gains; especially serious, 5+ (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2017). 无证无照经营查处办法（国务院令第 684 号，第五、六、十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-08/23/content_5219861.htm>；市场监管总局 (2023). 食品经营许可和备案管理办法（市场监管总局令第 78 号，第四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Note: Finance, medical, and education licensing have their own statutes — this entry verifies the general enforcement rules and the criminal clause only. Tea and snack shops are food service needing the food-operation license. Renovate-first and soft-opening are the common traps. Food tiers and whether raw meat and vegetables need papers: item 8.

### 8. Making food: find your tier first — production and catering need licenses, packaged-only resale needs filing, raw meat and vegetables need none
<!-- costs: money=low time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: The license itself is free, but premises and facilities must qualify — inspection plus processing takes weeks. Meat sellers keep quarantine certificates and quality-inspection passes per batch.
- In plain terms: Making food to sell — snack shops, braised goods, baking, homemade sauces — needs a license. Selling only others' packaged food needs none, just filing with county-level-plus market regulators. Raw meat and vegetables need nothing. Operating without: minimum 50,000–100,000 yuan, more than many small businesses' capital.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: food business requires a license first — production, sales, catering. "However, selling edible agricultural products and selling only prepackaged food require no license; prepackaged-only sellers shall file with the county-level-or-above food regulator." Edible agricultural products are unprocessed raw meat and vegetables; prepackaged food is others' finished products sold in packaging. Workshops and street vendors follow provincial rules. Unlicensed food business: confiscation of gains, food, tools, equipment, and materials; goods value under 10,000 yuan adds 50,000–100,000 yuan; over 10,000, 10–20× the goods value. Pig Slaughter Regulations: pigs are slaughtered only at state-designated plants with centralized official quarantine — "apart from rural self-slaughter for self-consumption, no unit or individual may slaughter pigs without designation," and "pork sold, processed, or served must come from designated plants passing quarantine and quality inspection." Private slaughter: shutdown, confiscation of pigs, tools, and earnings, fined as unlicensed food business. Animal Epidemic Prevention Law: animal products sold or shipped carry quarantine certificates and marks; absence draws correction plus fines up to one times the goods value of compliant equivalents. Judicial interpretation: workshop and vendor food below safety standards draws court-supported 10× punitive damages (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025 年第三次修正). 食品安全法（第三十五、三十六、一百二十二条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3>；国务院 (2021). 生猪屠宰管理条例（国务院令第 742 号，第二、二十三、三十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-07/22/content_5626534.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2021 修订). 动物防疫法（第五十一、一百条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080817703add2017737395a973e31>；最高人民法院 (2024). 关于审理食品药品惩罚性赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2024〕9 号，第四条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/bf531a70471f10d377788f28d3d8f8.html>
- Note: Workshop, small-catering, and vendor thresholds are provincial — no numbers here; search your province's regulator site for its "small workshops and vendors" regulation. Selling homemade food online doesn't shed the license. Beyond licensing, this section's general rules apply: entity and license in items 6–7. Buyer compensation: Section 5, item 31.

### 9. Bagged for sale means prepackaged: labels carry production date, shelf life, ingredient list — nothing optional
<!-- costs: money=low time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Labels run a few cents to a few mao each; each batch's dates and ingredients take time.
- In plain terms: Food packed for sale is prepackaged food: the label is mandatory, with nine items — production date and shelf life among them. Bulk food still needs name, date, and seller's name and address. Missing mandatory items: under 10,000 yuan value, 5,000–50,000; over, 5–10× goods value.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: prepackaged-food packaging must carry labels stating name, specification, net content, production date, ingredients, producer's name, address, and contact, shelf life, product-standard code, storage conditions, additive generic names, and production-license number — plus anything else law or standard requires. Bulk food (unpackaged, weighed) containers and wrappers state name, production date or batch, shelf life, and the operator's name, address, and contact. Label-less or noncompliant prepackaged food: confiscation of gains and food; goods value under 10,000 yuan adds 5,000–50,000; over, 5–10× value; serious, production suspension up to license revocation. Pure blemishes — typos, irregular type — that neither affect safety nor mislead: correction first, refusal drawing up to 2,000 yuan. The new General Rules for Prepackaged Food Labeling, GB 7718-2025, published 2025-03-16, effective 2027-03-16 with a two-year transition, requires a dedicated area displaying production date and expiry together, high-contrast legible type, and year-month-day ordering (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025 年第三次修正). 食品安全法（第六十七、六十八、一百二十五条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3>；国家卫生健康委员会、国家市场监督管理总局 (2025). 关于发布《食品安全国家标准 预包装食品标签通则》（GB 7718-2025）等 50 项食品安全国家标准和 9 项修改单的公告（2025 年第 2 号）. <https://jkw.lasa.gov.cn/wswyh/wjzl/202504/a90766908ef24869a74f7373bf42463f.shtml>（拉萨市卫生健康委员会转载公告全文）；最高人民法院 (2024). 关于审理食品药品惩罚性赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2024〕9 号，第六、八条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/bf531a70471f10d377788f28d3d8f8.html>（标签瑕疵与必标事项缺失的分档）；北京市市场监督管理局 (2026). 找不到生产日期？食品标签要「变脸」！新规这些重点必看. <https://scjgj.beijing.gov.cn/zwxx/scjgdt/202605/t20260507_4639154.html>（实施日期、过渡期与日期展示新要求）
- Note: Label defects come in two kinds, setting both fine and damages. Blemishes — typos, irregular type — that neither affect safety nor mislead: correction first, up to 2,000 on refusal, no 10× damages. Missing mandatory items — no production date, no shelf life — are expressly not blemishes: fines by goods value (5,000–50,000 under 10,000; 5–10× over), plus the buyer's 10× price claim, minimum 1,000 yuan (Section 5, item 31). GB 7718-2025's clause-by-clause requirements were verified only to the notice and official readings; the standard text governs.

### 10. Ordinary food may not claim to cure: labels, instructions, ads, and livestream scripts all count
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Run through your copy, product pages, and livestream scripts. The hard part: resisting "lowers the three highs" and "cures constipation."
- In plain terms: Ordinary food's labels, instructions, and ads may not claim disease prevention or treatment. Save for medical, drug, and device ads, no advertisement may claim cures or use drug-sounding words. Fines run 1–3× advertising costs, or 100,000–200,000 when costs can't be computed; serious cases can lose the business license.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: "labels and instructions of food and food additives shall contain no false content and shall not involve disease prevention or treatment functions; producers and traders are responsible for their content," and "food advertising content shall be truthful and lawful, containing no false content and involving no disease prevention or treatment." The Advertising Law adds: "apart from advertisements for medical treatment, drugs, and medical devices, all other advertisements are prohibited from involving disease-treatment functions and may not use medical terminology or terms likely to confuse the promoted goods with drugs or devices." Violations: ad termination, publisher's public clarification within the published scope, fines of 1–3× advertising costs; costs incomputable or obviously understated, 100,000–200,000 yuan; serious, 3–5× or 200,000–1 million, plus possible license revocation (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025 年第三次修正). 食品安全法（第七十一、七十三、一百二十五、一百四十条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3>；全国人大常委会 (2021 年修正). 广告法（第十七、五十八条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081817ab231eb017abd6bd860052d>
- Note: "Health functions" are off-limits to ordinary food too: health-food function claims must come from the state catalog with registration or filing — a step ordinary food never passes. Spotting such scripts as a buyer: Section 6, item 10. Detail pages, livestream scripts, and social posts all count as advertising, and posting leaves traces.

### 11. Food has criminal lines: selling diseased meat or over-limit goods convicts; toxic non-food additives start at five years regardless of amount
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Follow item 8's routine: keep quarantine certificates and purchase receipts; put only approval-numbered items in the recipe.
- In plain terms: The earlier items fine money; this one takes people. Selling diseased or failed-inspection meat, or goods with pesticide, veterinary, or heavy-metal excess, convicts on "sufficiency" to cause serious poisoning — nobody need actually fall ill — up to 3 years. Mixing toxic non-food raw materials in starts at up to 5 years, amounts irrelevant.
- Benefit: Criminal Law Article 143: producing or selling food below safety standards, "sufficient to cause serious food-poisoning accidents or other serious foodborne disease," convicts — up to 3 years or custody plus fines; serious harm or circumstances, 3–7; especially grave consequences, 7+ to life plus fines or full property confiscation. Article 144: mixing toxic, harmful non-food raw materials into food, or knowingly selling such — up to 5 years plus fines; serious harm, 5–10; death or especially grave, per Article 141 (10+, life, or death). The two-supreme interpretation lists "sufficient" situations: pathogenic microorganisms, pesticide, veterinary-drug, biotoxin, and heavy-metal pollutants "seriously exceeding standard limits"; meat and products from diseased, unexplained-death, or failed-inspection livestock, poultry, game, and aquatic animals; goods the state bans for disease control; and infant and special-medical foods with nutrients seriously below standards. Additives overdosed or misapplied to the same effect charge under Article 143. What counts as "toxic, harmful non-food raw material" isn't yours to define: legally banned substances and ministry-listed items — banned pesticides, drugs barred from food animals. "Other serious circumstances" include sales of 100,000–200,000 yuan; locations at schools, kindergartens, and elder-care facilities and their surroundings; and sales targeting minors or the elderly. Private pig slaughter off designated plants, serious, charges as illegal business — 100,000+ yuan or 50,000+ gains counting as "serious" (national)
- Evidence grade: A

### 24. Before paying shop-agency operators, store-opening courses, or "no-inventory" schemes: ask what more it will cost and where sales come from
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Before paying, obtain three things: written contract, the company's full registered name, refund terms. Pay only the contracted company, never personal accounts. The hard part: withstanding "students earning 10,000 monthly" screenshots.
- In plain terms: These scams take a small fee, then escalate with "upgraded packages," fronted goods payments, and unfreezing deposits. Backend orders and earnings may be fabricated, withdrawals frozen. Rank-brushing itself is illegal, and caught operators pay.
- Benefit: The Guangdong High Court adjudicated one such ring: Jiang, Xu, and 55 others ran multiple companies across Guangzhou, Nanning, and Hangzhou; the court found them "knowing full well they lacked e-commerce agency capability," inducing 7,711 victims to buy and upgrade "service packages" for Taobao shops — 52.994 million yuan defrauded; two principals drew 14 and 13.5 years for fraud, the scheme "airtight" with "victims repeatedly defrauded." Jiangsu police's 2025 dissection of "no-inventory" cross-border scams: (1) social-platform ads "zero basis, no stock"; (2) "operation coaches" befriend you onto a designated platform; (3) your shop's first order arrives fast — you front the goods cost, "buyer payment frozen until receipt"; fabricated orders then bleed you; (4) withdrawals meet "account abnormality" and "system maintenance" demands for unfreezing fees. One Ms. Zhang fronted 900,000-odd yuan; the site died 3 days later. The brushing side: an agency organizing fake group-buys for ranking drew a 100,000-yuan regulator fine plus the platform's 50,000-yuan deposit forfeiture, court-upheld; the SPC's 2026 typical-case line: "false transactions and rank-brushing are expressly prohibited by law." SAMR's 2025 case: a company selling "one-click migration" and "one-click dropship" software that crawled other platforms' listings drew a 530,000-yuan fine, with the regulator warning against "shop-theft migrations" and "no-inventory operations." The exit runs through the Civil Code: contracts induced by fraud are rescindable via court or arbitration — within 1 year of discovering — with payments returned on rescission (national)
