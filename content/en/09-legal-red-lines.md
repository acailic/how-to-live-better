# 9. Legal red lines ordinary people step over

This section counts two things: your personal freedom, and your money. What follows, ordinary people often take for "nothing much" — in fact it draws detention, fines, and at the heavy end, prison. Each entry gives the statute's specific numbers plus officially published cases. The Public Security Administration Punishments Law cited is the 2025 revision effective 2026-01-01 — article numbers differ from the old law. The criminal law follows the Beijing traffic-authority reprint "as amended by Amendment XI," with amendments noted inline. Entries run from "easiest to stumble into" plus "heaviest consequences" downward.

Entries in this section are grouped by theme, with item numbers in parentheses.

**Posting and forwarding online**: don't forward unverified disaster/epidemic/police news (item 1), don't share content insulting heroes and martyrs (item 2), close and forget what's on offshore sites (item 3), no porn in group chats (item 4).

**Handling money, loan fraud, claim fraud**: "side jobs" collecting money on your own card (item 5), dressing up loan applications (item 6), don't withdraw on a found bank card (item 7), claim offers under your real identity (item 8), never lend your ID (item 16), faking accidents for claims (item 21).

**Small daily things**: nothing thrown from balconies and windows (item 9), no replica guns online (item 10), register drones first (item 11), card games without rake or hosting (item 13), no wild game (item 14).

**Conflict and police**: debt collection without detaining people (item 15), cooperate when police stop you (item 17), do the math before striking (item 19), no batons or stun guns "for protection" (item 26).

**Sex, children, the body**: no secret filming (item 12), no sex under 14 (item 18), a child you can't raise goes through civil-affairs registration (item 20), never sell your organs (item 22), soliciting prostitution defaults to detention (item 23).

**Copyright and derivative works**: fan translation and pirated e-books (item 24), selling fan fiction needs permission first (item 25).

### 1. Don't forward unverified disaster, epidemic, or police news in groups — no photoshops, no AI-fabricated scene images
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Before forwarding, check whether it's an official notice. Unsure: don't forward, don't edit images, don't add "it's said." The hard part: resisting the urge to be first with the news.
- In plain terms: Forwarding disaster/epidemic/police news you know is false is punished as heavily as inventing it. Not your invention doesn't matter: 5–10 days' detention plus 1,000 yuan. Throwing social order into disorder: up to 3 years; serious consequences, 3–7. Photoshopped and AI-faked scene images count as your own invention.
- Benefit: Deliberately spreading rumors, false alarms of danger, epidemic, disaster, or police matters disturbing public order: 5–10 days, plus up to 1,000 yuan; the lighter band, up to 5 days or 1,000 yuan. Fabricating false danger/epidemic/disaster/police information and spreading it online: up to 3 years; knowingly spreading it, same band — precondition being "seriously disturbing social order." Serious consequences: 3–7 years (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第二十九条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（第三十二条，即刑法第二百九十一条之一第二款）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百九十一条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>（北京市公安交通管理局转载）
- Note: Knowing-forwarding and self-invention fall under the same provision for police. "I just forwarded it" is no defense. Photoshop and AI fakes count as invention. The MPS published several batches of disaster-rumor cases in 2025; the MPS site was unreachable during writing, so no specific cases cited.

### 2. Don't post or forward images, memes, or comments insulting heroes and martyrs; no jokes about the flag or anthem
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Content touching heroes, the flag, emblem, or anthem: no comments, no edits, no forwards. The hard part: staying quiet when the group plays along.
- In plain terms: Jokes about martyrs, the flag, the anthem: lighter, 5–10 days or 1,000–3,000 yuan; heavier, 10–15 days. Criminal-grade: up to 3 years. One villager posted 2 lines defaming Yuan Longping in a 499-member WeChat group: 6 months' control — no cell, but restricted movement.
- Benefit: Insulting or fabricating against heroes' and martyrs' names, likenesses, reputation, or honor harming the public interest: 5–10 days or 1,000–3,000 yuan. Desecrating or denying their deeds and spirit, or producing and spreading content glorifying wars of aggression: same band. Heavier: 10–15 days plus up to 5,000 yuan. "Serious circumstances" become crime: up to 3 years. Publicly insulting the flag or emblem, or altering the anthem's words or music or singing it with distortion and disparagement, serious: up to 3 years. Official cases: Xiao posted 2 messages defaming Yuan Longping in a 499-member village group — 6 months' control (control meaning confinement-free punishment with restricted movement). Qiu, with 2.5-million-plus followers, posted 2 pieces distorting border-defense soldiers' deeds — 8 months plus a national-media public apology (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第三十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2018). 英雄烈士保护法（第二十二、二十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/gyssshmhsh/201912/t20191202_440079.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第三十五条，即刑法第二百九十九条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百九十九条、第二百九十九条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院 (2022). 涉英烈权益保护十大典型案例（案例二、案例三）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/382301.html>
- Note: The punishments-law clause arrived only in the 2025 revision; before, such matters went through criminal law or procuratorial public-interest suits. A several-hundred-member group counts as a public venue in courts' eyes.

### 3. Offshore sites: read and close — no screenshots, no forwarding, no carrying into groups, no joining the comments
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Close the page and it ends. No archiving, forwarding, or republishing domestically. Don't run an identity-linked account posting political content long-term on offshore platforms. Don't take fees or funding from offshore institutions, organizations, or individuals. The hard part: not screenshotting striking content.
- In plain terms: These provisions punish outward transmission, not viewing. Forwarding, carrying into groups, and echoing in comments all count as dissemination in practice. Reaching the incite-subversion or incite-secession band: up to 5 years; ringleaders, 5+. Terrorist and extremist books and audiovisuals are the exception: knowingly holding them, serious, up to 3 years.
- Benefit: Inciting subversion of state power or overthrow of the socialist system by rumor or defamation: up to 5 years; ringleaders ("principal elements") or grave crimes, 5+ with a 15-year cap. Inciting secession or undermining national unity: identical terms. Colluding with offshore institutions, organizations, or individuals: aggravated. Funding others to do so: responsible individuals, up to 5 years; serious, 5+. Stealing, spying on, buying, or illegally providing state secrets or intelligence for offshore parties: 5–10 years; especially serious, 10+ or life; lighter, under 5. Crimes endangering national security carry supplementary political-rights deprivation of 1–5 years — voting and public-office eligibility suspended — plus possible property confiscation. Administratively: no individual or organization may use networks for activities endangering national security, honor, or interests, nor incite subversion or secession; publishing or transmitting such information is punished per law and regulation (national). One class stands without transmission: knowingly holding terrorist or extremist materials — books, audio-video, or other items — serious, up to 3 years, custody, or control, plus or instead of fines, per Criminal Law Article 120-6 (added by Amendment IX)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第四十五、五十五、五十六、一百零三、一百零五、一百零六、一百零七、一百一十一、一百一十三条，这几条未经修正案修改）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第十三条第二款、第七十一条第一项）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（新增第一百二十条之六）. 国家信访局转载全文 <https://www.gjxfj.gov.cn/gjxfj/fgwj/flfg/webinfo/2017/03/1601761496614423.htm>
- Note: The cited provisions punish inciting, transmitting outward, providing to others — viewing is not among them. Terrorist and extremist materials are the statutory exception, criminal by mere knowing possession (Article 120-6): never archive that class. See it, and it ends there: no screenshots, no forwards, no comments, no recruiting. Forwarding and comment-echoing count as dissemination — "just forwarded" is no defense — and offshore provenance can aggravate. Unsure of truth: all the more reason not to forward (item 1). Circumvention tools: Section 11, item 11. This class of content buys nothing real and costs sentences, political rights, and property. Publicly findable cases appear only in offshore media and family-released documents — e.g., a long-term offshore-platform political blogger sentenced to 7 years in 2023; no officially verifiable judgment was obtainable, so statutes only, no case attached.

### 4. Porn: private viewing is your business — no group sharing, no selling "resources," no group-building
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. No forwarding, no group-file storage, no administering such groups.
- In plain terms: Private viewing isn't in the punishment clauses. One group post counts as dissemination to the whole group: 10–15 days plus up to 5,000 yuan; serious, up to 2 years. Selling "resources" for money: up to 3 years, topping out at life. Building a dissemination group of 30+: founders, admins, and main posters all count as criminals.
- Benefit: Spreading obscene information online: 10–15 days plus up to 5,000 yuan; the lighter band, up to 5 days or 1,000–3,000 yuan; content involving minors, aggravated. Dissemination reaching "serious circumstances": up to 2 years. For profit: up to 3 years plus fines; serious in that class, 3–10; especially serious, 10+ or life. Groups primarily for spreading obscene electronic information with 30+ members are handled criminally — founders, administrators, and principal posters all charged with dissemination of obscene materials (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第八十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第三百六十三、三百六十四条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2010). 关于办理利用互联网、移动通讯终端、声讯台制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）（第三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201002/t20100204_25001.shtml>
- Note: Both laws punish "dissemination"; private viewing isn't in the clauses. But one group post counts as dissemination to the group. The quantity thresholds for "serious" are judicially interpreted — unverified here.

### 5. "Side jobs" asking you to collect, withdraw, or transfer money on your own card: refuse at any fee
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Walk away from "daily hundreds, bring your card to the bank" work.
- In plain terms: These jobs pay hundreds and cost a criminal record. One man brought his ID and 1 bank card to a designated spot to help transfer and withdraw, earning 500-odd yuan — prosecutors declined to charge, and police still detained him 10 days with a 1,000-yuan fine. Another handed his own and others' cards and SIMs to a ring for collection and withdrawal: 2 years 3 months plus a 22,000-yuan fine.
- Benefit: Knowingly transferring, acquiring, or selling criminal proceeds, or otherwise helping conceal them: up to 3 years plus or instead of fines. Official cases: Wang, on instruction, brought ID and 1 card to a spot to help transfer and withdraw, earning 500-odd yuan; unprosecuted, yet police detained him 10 days and fined 1,000 under the Anti-Telecom-Fraud Law. Man surrendered his own and others' cards and SIMs to a ring for collection, transfer, and withdrawal: 2 years 3 months plus 22,000 yuan (national). Converting to crypto is the same: An and two others, on a fraud ring's instruction, received funds on their own cards, bought virtual currency on a platform, and forwarded it to designated accounts on commission — verified victim funds of 500,000-odd yuan; all three convicted of concealing criminal proceeds, 2 years 8 months to 3 years 3 months, fines of 30,000–40,000 (Tongzhou, Beijing)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第三百一十二条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2023). 检察机关依法惩治电信网络诈骗及其关联犯罪典型案例（案例九）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202311/t20231130_635188.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2025). 依法惩治掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益犯罪典型案例（案例一、五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202508/t20250825_704514.shtml>
- Note: Lending cards and "money muling": Section 8, item 8. This entry covers your own hands withdrawing and transferring. Receiving and converting to Tether-type coins is the same act. Why your own crypto losses are unrecoverable: Section 5, item 45. "I didn't know where the money came from" doesn't necessarily hold: absurd pay, face-scan cooperation, and glaringly abnormal transactions suffice as knowledge. The floor is 10 days' detention plus a record.

### 6. Anyone recruiting you to "dress up" loan applications for a cut of the loan: refuse every one
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Refuse outright. Don't lend your ID or credit record, don't sign unseen loan contracts, don't attend bank interviews for others.
- In plain terms: Helping "dress up" loan files for a share is fraud. One 126-person ring dressed up jobless people as "quality customers," defrauding 30-million-plus yuan across 80-odd banks. The signers took 30% of loan value; of 80 sentenced, 76 drew 1 year 4 months to 6 years 6 months. The debt sits in whosever name the loan was taken.
- Benefit: Obtaining loans, acceptances, letters of credit, or guarantees from banks by deception causing major loss: up to 3 years plus or instead of fines; especially major loss or especially serious circumstances, 3–7 plus fines. Police file from 500,000 yuan of direct loss. Never intending repayment, using fabricated project pretexts, fake contracts, fake certificates, or fake title documents: loan fraud — 50,000 yuan files the case, up to 5 years plus 20,000–200,000 yuan fines; huge, 5–10; especially huge, 10+ to life. Forging, altering, or trading state-organ documents, certificates, or seals: up to 3 years; serious, 3–10. Official case: a 126-person ring across 16 provinces dressed jobless people as "quality customers," defrauding 30-million-plus yuan at 80-odd bank branches; the fronting "blank-credit" signers took 30% of loan value; prosecutors pursued 105 of them; of 80 sentenced at first instance in 2026, 76 drew 1 year 4 months to 6 years 6 months for loan fraud plus fines, and 4 who withdrew cash for the ring drew 2 years 4 months to 7 years 6 months for loan fraud and money laundering cumulatively (national). The fraudulent-loans-offense text here follows Amendment XI from March 2021
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第十一条，修改刑法第一百七十五条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202012/t20201227_503700.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第八十八、一百九十三、二百八十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；最高人民检察院、公安部 (2022). 关于公安机关管辖的刑事案件立案追诉标准的规定（二）（第二十二、四十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202204/t20220429_555906.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2026). 骗贷流水线背后藏洗钱黑手（检察日报）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202604/t20260428_726494.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2012). 出境入境管理法（第十二条）. <https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741547/c1013311/content.html>（国家移民管理局）；全国人大常委会 (2023 修正). 民事诉讼法（第二百六十六条）. <https://amr.guizhou.gov.cn/zwgk/xxgkml/jcxxgk/zcfg/fl/202401/t20240129_83645867.html>（贵州省市场监管局转载）
- Note: "I meant to repay" didn't hold in that case: prosecutors established joblessness, no assets, unpayable, and the shares already spent — hence no repayment intent from the start. Withdrawing cash for the ring adds money laundering. Whose name the loan sits in, whose signature — that's the borrower; short of crime, the debt and the credit record remain yours. Fleeing abroad settles nothing: once filed, years in hiding don't bar prosecution; the unsentenced, suspects, and defendants are barred from exit; undecided civil cases and non-performing judgment debtors can be exit-restricted too. Withdrawing and transferring: item 5. Discovering impersonation loans: Section 8, item 9.

### 7. Don't withdraw on a found bank card; found phones and wallets go to the owner or the police — never the pocket
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free, minutes. Call the owner or deliver to a police station.
- In plain terms: Keeping found items for yourself, at "relatively large" amounts and refusing surrender: up to 2 years; huge, 2–5. Withdrawing at an ATM on a found card is heavier — "unauthorized use of another's card": relatively large alone draws up to 5 years plus 20,000–200,000 yuan. That one, police file directly — no victim complaint needed.
- Benefit: Found property must be returned, the owner notified, or items delivered to police. Keeping lost property, "relatively large" and refusing surrender, is embezzlement: up to 2 years; huge, 2–5. Using a found card at an ATM is "unauthorized use of another's credit card," criminal as credit-card fraud: relatively large, up to 5 years plus 20,000–200,000; huge or with serious circumstances, 5–10 plus 50,000–500,000 (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第三百一十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第一百九十六、二百七十条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2008). 关于拾得他人信用卡并在自动柜员机（ATM 机）上使用的行为如何定性问题的批复. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/flfg/sfjs/201208/t20120830_47871.shtml>
- Note: Embezzlement is "handled only upon complaint" — the owner sues; police don't act on their own. But ATM withdrawal on a found card files directly. "Relatively large" thresholds are judicially set — unverified here. Delivered items charge the owner storage costs on pickup (Civil Code Article 317).

### 8. Claim offers under your real identity; no virtual numbers, no batch-registered alts, no exploiting bugs for repeats
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Walk away from "coupon-task groups." Platform bugs: don't touch. The hard part: not treating coupons as scalable income.
- In plain terms: One real identity, one account, one claim — outside punishment. Batch-registering fake users and self-ordering-self-delivering for multiples is fraud: 3,000 yuan reaches up to 3 years; from 30,000, 3–10. One rider bought 0.1-yuan virtual numbers to brush coupons, cashing out 90,000-odd yuan — all 12 co-defendants sentenced.
- Benefit: Defrauding others or the public, "relatively large": up to 3 years plus or instead of fines; huge or serious circumstances, 3–10; especially huge, 10+ or life plus fines or confiscation. Bands set provincially: large 3,000–10,000+; huge 30,000–100,000+; especially huge 500,000+. Teaching methods or selling "courses": the teaching-crime offense, up to 5 years; serious, 5–10; especially serious, 10+ or life. The phone-number and verification-code supply chain: the personal-information crime, up to 3 years; especially serious, 3–7. Batch accounts, auto-switching network addresses, and SMS-receiving code platforms are banned outright by Anti-Telecom-Fraud Law Article 14; trading, renting, or lending cards and accounts and passing real-name checks sit in Article 31. Article 14 violations: confiscation plus 1–10× gains; no gains or under 50,000 yuan, up to 500,000 yuan and up to 15 days when serious. Article 31: 1–10× similarly, under 20,000 or none up to 200,000 plus detention when serious. Telecom-fraud participation short of crime: 10–15 days, confiscation plus 1–10×; under 10,000 or none, up to 100,000. Official cases: delivery rider Liu bought virtual numbers at 0.1 yuan each to register platform new-users, claimed the 29-minus-15 newcomer coupons, resold goods to small shops for cash, and cross-invited his virtual numbers for 2–20-yuan bounties — he and rider Tan defrauded 90,000-odd and 70,000-odd respectively; Shanghai Yangpu prosecutors charged 12, courts sentencing 3 years 6 months to 3 months' custody. Case two: Wang exploited the time gap between refunds and virtual-lottery-coin recovery — 1,156 refunds, 224 figurines extracted, 249,000-odd yuan, monetized on secondhand platforms; 3 years suspended 4, plus a 30,000-yuan fine, with 330,000 yuan in restitution and prepaid fines during trial. Case three: a ride-hailing platform's new-user coupons and driver subsidies defrauded via "self-ordering, self-delivering" fake runs across 19 provinces and 70-odd cities, 2-million-plus yuan; Chaoyang Longcheng district prosecutors in Liaoning charged 163, all convicted; principals Huang and Cai drew 3 years 6 months each for fraud, personal-information crime, and teaching methods cumulatively; 34 minor accomplices went unprosecuted but referred for administrative punishment (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百五十三条之一、第二百六十六条、第二百九十五条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>（北京市公安交通管理局转载）；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理诈骗刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2011〕7 号，第一条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/sfjs/201802/t20180201_363736.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第十四、三十一、三十八、四十二、四十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第九百八十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2021). 剑指电信网络诈骗｜不法分子盯上了电商经济的漏洞（检察日报）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202106/t20210607_520557.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 如此「薅羊毛」涉嫌诈骗（检察日报）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202508/t20250826_704625.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2024). 辽宁朝阳龙城区：办理系列电信网络诈骗案（法治日报）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202401/t20240124_641099.shtml>；看看新闻 (2026-08-25). 男子网购870多个手机号薅车企羊毛 窃电40余万（记者陈敏佳）. <https://www.kankanews.com/detail/80290xAJDw6>（网页存档 <https://web.archive.org/web/20261005044233/https://www.kankanews.com/detail/80290xAJDw6>）
- Note: The line isn't "how much you grazed" but whether you faked anything. Real identity, one account, coupons used on yourself: that's the platform's offer. Fake users, fabricated transactions, or concealing prior refunds make the other side hand things over under false impressions — fraud — and amounts only set the sentence. Carmaker free charging has been grazed the same way: per August 2026 reporting, two Shanghai residents bought masses of virtual numbers, rotated them as sub-accounts on an EV maker's app, charged twice per number on "charge-now-pay-later," then unbound — bills unpaid, charging resold to truck drivers; Pudong prosecutors approved arrest as fraud, not theft; media report only, no judgment or official notice.
