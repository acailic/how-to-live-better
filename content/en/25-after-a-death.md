# 25. What to handle after someone dies

Metric: money. Only procedures and collections — the first moves at death, which fees carry checkable lists, which accounts release money, and where close relatives hold standing. The first five entries follow chronology: the scene, the death certificate, the body and cremation, disputing the cause, deregistration. The rest rank by value. The living arrangements — agreed guardianship, wills, accounts — sit in Section 17; work-death's three benefits in Section 19.

The funeral part follows the new Funeral Regulations, effective 2026-03-30. Pension funeral grants and survivor pensions are separately regulated by the HR ministry; verbatim originals weren't obtained, so no amounts appear.

### 1. A death at home or away: first separate natural from non-natural — accident, unknown cause, or found-after-days means police now, scene and body untouched
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. One phone call.
- In plain terms: Accidental deaths, unknown causes, and solo-living discoveries get death certificates only from the police. A disturbed scene muddles everything and the certificate besides — and without that paper, cremation, deregistration, and insurance claims all stall. Unsure which kind? Call 110 first; the police issue after investigating.
- Benefit: Household Registration Regulations, Article 8(2): "where a citizen dies by accident or of unknown cause, the household head or discoverer shall immediately report to the local police station or township people's committee." The Funeral Regulations, Article 23: "for natural deaths, medical institutions shall promptly issue the death certificate; for non-natural death cases handled by public security without medical treatment, the public security organs issue the death certificate." Natural illness deaths: the treating institution certifies. Untreated, police-handled: the police certify. Non-natural death certificates come only from the police — disturbed scenes both obscure and block. Everything after hangs on that one sheet
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html>（广东省人民政府门户网站刊登）；国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Note: Long-bedridden home deaths count as natural — the treating institution, the neighborhood community health center, or the township clinic can certify; whoever treated issues (item 2). Unsure which kind: treat as non-natural, call 110; the police certify after investigating. Tidying before calling leaves nobody able to help. Move nothing — not the body, no washing or changing clothes, no clearing bottles or notes.

### 2. The death certificate keys everything after: whoever treated issues; home natural deaths go to community health services, issued within a day
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: Died in hospital — the treating hospital issues. Died naturally at home — the neighborhood community health institution or township clinic. Clear cause: signed within a day. Deregistration, cremation, housing fund withdrawal, benefits, claims, and inheritance all need it. Copy it several times on receipt.
- Benefit: The death-registration rules: in-facility deaths are certified "by the treating institution"; natural deaths at home or elsewhere "by the district community health institution or township (subdistrict) clinic." Police- or justice-determined non-natural deaths follow existing police and judicial procedures. The 2026 five-department notice: paper and electronic death certificates "carry equal legal force." Natural deaths with clear cause are issued within one day of death. Lost originals may be reissued once; erroneous information may be re-issued once on documentation
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生计生委办公厅 (2014). 人口死亡信息登记管理规范（试行）（国卫办规划发〔2014〕68 号，第九条）. <https://mzj.sz.gov.cn/szmz/pc/zwgk/jcxxgk/zcfg/byfw/content/post_2952215.html>（深圳市民政局转发）；国家卫生健康委、公安部、民政部、国家中医药局、国家疾控局 (2026). 关于加强居民死亡医学证明信息登记和电子证照管理工作的通知（国卫规划发〔2026〕5 号）. <https://wjw.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/fgwj/gjwj/202602/t20260228_7102810.htm>（福建省卫生健康委员会转发）
- Note: Deregistration, cremation, housing fund, benefits, claims, inheritance — each needs it. Copy repeatedly on receipt; request the electronic certificate too. Reissue comes once — guard the original. Name and ID errors get fixed on the spot; discovered at inheritance time, every window reruns.

### 3. Body transport, storage, and cremation run only through funeral homes; hospital morgues host no funerals; storage generally caps at 3 days
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: Transport, storage, preservation, cosmetology, and cremation belong to funeral homes alone. Hospital morgues park bodies temporarily, nothing more. Storage generally runs 3 days — longer needs extension paperwork. Cremation takes the death certificate and the cremation confirmation; the cremation certificate issued after feeds inheritance and claims.
- Benefit: Article 25: "body transport, storage, preservation, cosmetology, and cremation services are exclusively provided by funeral homes." Article 24: "hospital morgues may not outsource or conduct funeral services, and may provide convenience parking for bodies." Article 27: "funeral homes generally store bodies no more than 3 days; extensions are handled at the funeral home by the family or transferring party." Article 28: "funeral homes cremate on the death certificate and cremation confirmation, issuing a cremation certificate." Article 29: "in cremation regions, bodies are cremated locally and nearby at funeral homes"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第八、九、二十四、二十五、二十七、二十八、二十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Note: The "one-stop" services swarming hospital deaths are mostly not funeral-home staff. Transport sits on the basic list with set pricing (item 6). Keep the cremation certificate — inheritance, insurance, and ash relocation all need it. Cremation versus burial regions are provincially drawn; minority customs carry Article 9 protection. Cremation-region burial or graves outside cemeteries draw correction orders from civil affairs.

### 4. Doubting the cause of death: autopsy within 48 hours, records sealed alongside
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: remembering to raise it then.
- In plain terms: Questioning the cause means proposing autopsy within 48 hours of death — 7 days where the hospital freezes bodies. Autopsy needs close relatives' signed consent; refusal or delay shifts the unclear-cause responsibility onto the refusing or delaying side. Demand record sealing alongside. Cremation ends autopsy forever.
- Benefit: The time limits sit in Article 26 of the medical-dispute regulations: "where the patient dies and the parties dispute the cause, autopsy shall proceed within 48 hours of death; with body-freezing conditions, up to 7 days." The same article: "autopsy requires the close relatives' consent and signature; refusal of signature deems the close relatives unwilling," and "the party refusing or delaying past the deadline, affecting cause determination, bears responsibility." Article 24: sealing and unsealing "proceed with both parties present"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第二十四、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Note: "It always felt wrong" past the deadline flips responsibility onto the delaying side. Cremated bodies never autopsy — settle this before contacting the funeral home. Record sealing: Section 24.

### 5. Deregistration carries statutory deadlines: before burial in cities, within one month in rural areas
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=small metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Death certificate and household register in hand, one trip to the police station.
- In plain terms: Cities: before burial. Rural: within a month. Death certificate and household register to the station. Delay wedges inheritance transfers, benefits, and refunds against the registration status.
- Benefit: Article 8(1): "on a citizen's death, in cities before burial and in rural areas within one month, the household head, relatives, supporter, or neighbor reports the death registration and cancels the household registration; a death at a temporary residence has the temporary-residence authority notify the permanent-residence authority to cancel." Urban burials precede; rural runs a month. Temporary-residence deaths route by notification
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html>（广东省人民政府门户网站刊登）
- Note: An open registration wedges transfers, benefits, and refunds — every window reads registration status. Pensions still flowing are misappropriated, repayable eventually. At the station, ask how insurance benefits stop and funeral grants file: item 9.

### 6. Funeral services split basic and non-basic; the basic list carries lawfully set fees
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Minutes reading the list before setting out.
- In plain terms: Transport, storage, farewell, cremation, ash storage, and ecological burial all sit on the national basic list — government institutions' columbarium interment included. Prices follow regulation, not the funeral home's say-so. Nothing gets added beyond the list. Ask which items are basic and the local rates before anything else.
- Benefit: Transport, storage, farewell, cremation, ash storage, and ecological burial sit on the national basic list, government funeral institutions' niche interment included. Fees are set per law — not the funeral home's own pricing. Non-basic lists are set by prefecture-level-plus civil affairs
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第五条：「殡葬服务分为基础项目和非基础项目，收费政策由国务院发展改革部门、财政部门会同国务院民政等部门制定。」「国家制定殡葬服务基础项目清单，将遗体接运、遗体存放、遗体告别、遗体火化、骨灰寄存、生态安葬以及政府举办的殡葬服务机构提供的骨灰格位安葬等纳入清单范围」；第五十条「殡葬服务实行清单化管理并动态调整，禁止在清单之外设立项目、收取费用。」
- Note: Provinces may extend the national list. Ask which items are basic and the local rates first; list prices are set, outside-list items optional — separating the two gives the overcharges a leg to stand on. Wanting dignity for the departed is no error; this book urges no austerity — where the money goes stays yours.

### 7. Forced bundling, above-marked pricing, item-splitting fees: record and complain
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: insisting at that moment.
- In plain terms: The regulation names these price violations: no posted list, above-list charges, bundled funeral-goods sales, forced third-party services. Splitting one item into several counts as disguised price-raising. Demand the price list, keep receipts and chats; later complaints still work. Prices go to market regulation, funeral services to civil affairs.
- Benefit: The regulation lists five violation classes, four reproduced here: unposted prices or above-mark charges; buy-this-or-no-that bundling with unreasonable conditions; forced acceptance of third-party paid services; renamed extra charges as disguised rate-raising. Also banned: "splitting items and expanding scope to disguise fee increases" — splitting one service into several billable pieces or widening the billing scope
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十二条：「殡葬服务机构不得违反有关规定擅自设立收费项目，或者采取分解项目、扩大范围收费等形式变相提高收费标准。」第四十三条列举的价格违法行为含「（一）不按规定明码标价，或者在标价之外加价或者收取未标明的费用」「（三）采取捆绑、附加不合理条件或者不合理限制等方式，强制或者变相强制销售丧葬用品、提供殡葬相关服务并收取费用」「（四）强制或者变相强制丧属接受第三方的有偿服务，或者利用相关服务网点重复收取费用」；第五十一条要求民政部门与政务服务便民热线建立信息共享监督机制，「对丧属反映的殡葬服务问题，应当会同有关部门及时予以处理并将处理结果告知丧属」
- Note: Price violations go to market regulation, funeral services to civil affairs. Demand the list, keep receipts and chats — later complaints still work.

### 8. Funeral agents, goods brokers, ceremony planners: county civil-affairs filing required
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One question: filed or not.
- In plain terms: Agency, goods procurement, planning-and-hosting, and information-consulting intermediaries must file with county civil affairs. Unfiled outfits sit outside the funeral-service information system. Verification varies locally — calling the county office is safest. Deceptive charging and trading in the deceased's information get lawfully disposed of.
- Benefit: Unfiled intermediaries sit outside the civil-affairs funeral-service system; deceptive fee collection, induced extravagance, forced services, and trading the deceased's information draw lawful disposal, system annotation, and public disclosure — visible to all
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十一条：「除殡葬服务机构外，从事殡葬服务代理、用品代购、策划主持、信息咨询等殡葬相关服务活动的组织和个人，应当向县级人民政府民政部门备案。」「对存在以欺骗等手段违规收费、诱导大操大办、强迫接受服务、倒卖逝者信息等损害丧属权益行为的依法处置，并在殡葬服务信息系统中进行标注，向社会公开。」
- Note: "Trading the deceased's information" is the trade's commonest trap — telemarketing swarms right after procedures mean the data moved. Online memorial platforms fall under the same regime: Article 45 bars induced spending and unlawful handling of the deceased's information.

### 9. Scattered money gets collected one window at a time: housing fund balance, social-insurance benefits, work-injury benefits
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free, several windows. Death certificate and kinship proof in hand.
- In plain terms: The housing fund balance is inheritable and withdrawable — outside bank accounts, the easiest to forget. Pension personal-account balances inherit too. Illness deaths add funeral grants and 3–24 months' survivor pension; work deaths add the three work-injury sums.
- Benefit: Housing fund balances: heirs and legatees may withdraw; legatees being those named in wills. Work deaths add the three sums (Section 19). Pension personal-account balances inherit by statute. Employee-insurance illness deaths add funeral grants and survivor pensions — the survivor benefits — under a nationally uniform enterprise formula: funeral grant at 2× the province's prior-year urban monthly per-capita disposable income; survivor pension on that base by contribution years — under 5 years, 3 months; 5–10, 6; 10–15, 9; past 15, one month per year, capped at 24; retirees drop one month per year drawn, floored at 9
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 住房公积金管理条例（国务院令第 844 号修改，2026 年 9 月 20 日起施行）第二十四条末款. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12946/202608/content_7079363.html>：「职工死亡或者被宣告死亡的，职工的继承人、受遗赠人可以提取职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额；无继承人也无受遗赠人的，职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额纳入住房公积金的增值收益。」；工伤保险条例第三十九条的三笔待遇见第 19 节. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2018 修正). 社会保险法（第十四、十七条）：「个人死亡的，个人账户余额可以继承」「参加基本养老保险的个人，因病或者非因工死亡的，其遗属可以领取丧葬补助金和抚恤金」. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080816f135f46016f210989b9179a>（国家法律法规数据库）；人力资源社会保障部、财政部 (2021). 企业职工基本养老保险遗属待遇暂行办法（人社部发〔2021〕18 号，2021 年 9 月 1 日起施行，第四、五条），原件未公开挂网，条文按广州市花都区人民政府行政复议决定书（花都府行复〔2022〕226 号）的逐字引用核对：「丧葬补助金的标准，按照参保人员死亡时本省……上一年度城镇居民月人均可支配收入的2倍计算」「缴费年限不满5年的，发放月数为3个月……缴费年限30年以上的，按照30年计算，发放月数最高为24个月」. <https://www.huadu.gov.cn/xxgk/xzfy/content/post_8672730.html>；福建省人力资源和社会保障厅. 常见问题：「每领取1年基本养老金减少1个月，发放月数最低为9个月」「累计缴费年限不足5年的，其遗属待遇（含丧葬补助金和抚恤金）标准不得超过其个人缴费之和」. <http://rst.fujian.gov.cn/wz/cjwt/czzgly/202311/t20231113_6294929.htm>
- Note: The housing fund gets forgotten most — outside bank accounts, never auto-entering the estate. Pension balances and survivor benefits compute and pay separately; handle both at the enrollment window. Documents vary locally. Under 5 years' contributions, combined survivor benefits cap at the deceased's own contributions. The formula covers enterprise employees — public institutions and resident insurance ask at the window. Wills and inheritance forms: Section 17.

### 10. The deceased's accounts and personal information: close relatives may demand viewing, copying, correction, and deletion
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Uncooperative platforms mean complaints or suit.
- In plain terms: After death, close relatives — for their own lawful, legitimate interests — may demand platforms view, copy, correct, or delete the deceased's personal information. Refusals require stated reasons; courts hear the suits. One exception: the deceased's own arrangements — account inheritors or explicit wishes — govern instead.
- Benefit: The law states it: on a natural person's death, close relatives may, for their own lawful and legitimate interests, exercise the chapter's viewing, copying, correction, and deletion rights over the deceased's personal information
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 个人信息保护法. 中国人大网. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202108/t20210820_313088.html> 第四十九条：「自然人死亡的，其近亲属为了自身的合法、正当利益，可以对死者的相关个人信息行使本章规定的查阅、复制、更正、删除等权利；死者生前另有安排的除外。」；第五十条「个人信息处理者应当建立便捷的个人行使权利的申请受理和处理机制。拒绝个人行使权利的请求的，应当说明理由」「个人可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼」
- Note: The clause "save where the deceased arranged otherwise" — account inheritors and explicit wishes govern. So while lucid, writing down where accounts and passwords go is worth it (Section 17). Account cancellation and information deletion are two things (Section 14).
