# 10. Is dating and marriage worth it?

This section splits "is marriage worth it" into separate accounts — money, time, health, legal risk, and relationship quality — each computed alone. Each account uses only numbers with findable sources, restricted to official statistics or meta-analyses (recomputing pooled data from many studies). No moral judgments, no conclusions on your behalf. Health accounts run on death risk, time accounts on daily minutes, legal accounts on what statutes say; the three are never converted into each other. The account-splitting method, fill-in checklist, and common mistakes are in [docs/结婚划不划算.md](../docs/结婚划不划算.md).

Entries in this section are grouped by theme, with item numbers in parentheses.

**Meeting and dating**: meet more people (item 1), stop at a clear refusal (item 2), judge interest by behavior (item 3), alternate progressively deeper questions (item 5), long-distance is no worse (item 6).

**Relationship quality**: your own felt experience of the relationship (item 4), fight about the matter, not the person (item 17), go to couples therapy together (item 20).

**The worth-it accounts**: registration data first (item 7), discount health benefits from observational data (item 8), time as unpaid labor (item 9), compute exit costs (item 15), marrying for elders gets its own account (item 16).

**Registering and premarital checks**: registering needs only IDs (item 13), one premarital check (item 14).

**Money and debt**: money defaults first (item 10), parental home contributions written as loan or gift (item 11), a spouse's large borrowings (item 12).

**Same-sex couples and orientation**: same-sex couples arrange powers of attorney and wills (item 18), don't marry by concealing orientation (item 19).

### 1. Meet more people instead of betting everything on one: two people's chemistry can't be predicted before they meet
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Time goes to meeting several people, not re-litigating one. The hard part: not fixating.
- In plain terms: Two speed-dating studies had participants fill in 100-odd mate-preference questionnaires before meeting. The questionnaires could compute who generally likes people easily and who is generally liked — not whether these two click. Chemistry is only knowable in person. So meeting several beats staking everything on one.
- Benefit: In the two speed-dating studies, participants completed 100-odd mate-selection self-report scales before meeting. Random-forest models (a pattern-finding algorithm) could compute "how much a person likes others overall" — the questionnaires explained 4%–18% of variance (variance meaning person-to-person difference, of which the questionnaires explain only part) — and "how popular a person is overall," 7%–27%. But "do these two specific people click" couldn't be computed, no matter how pre-meeting traits and preferences combined. The same group's earlier speed-dating experiment had participants self-report ideal preferences — looks versus earning prospects — which failed to predict interest in any specific person on the night
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Joel S, Eastwick PW, Finkel EJ (2017). Is romantic desire predictable? Machine learning applied to initial romantic attraction. Psychological Science, 28(10), 1478-1489. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797617714580>；Eastwick PW, Finkel EJ (2008). Sex differences in mate preferences revisited: do people know what they initially desire in a romantic partner? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 94(2), 245-264. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.94.2.245>
- Note: The studies establish only "unpredictable before meeting," not "effort is useless." Chemistry is knowable only in person, so time spent genuinely meeting several beats all-in on one. The subjects were Euro-American students; China's dating environment differs — discount accordingly.

### 2. When they clearly refuse, stop — continued pursuit is an administrative offense, not "sincerity"
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=yes benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free, no time. The hard part: no more messages, no more waiting outside.
- In plain terms: After a clear refusal, still messaging and waiting makes it an administrative offense from January 2026: up to 5 days or 1,000 yuan; heavier, 5–10 days. Police can also order no-contact; breaching adds another 5–10 days.
- Benefit: The punishments law effective 2026-01-01 lists harassment and stalking separately: "repeatedly sending obscene, insulting, or threatening information, or using harassment, pestering, or stalking to disturb another's normal life" — up to 5 days or 1,000 yuan; heavier, 5–10 days plus up to 1,000. This clause adds a layer: with police-chiefs' approval, the actor may be ordered to avoid contact with the victim for a period; violating the no-contact order draws another 5–10 days
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025 修订). 治安管理处罚法（第五十条第一款第五项、第二款）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>（最高人民检察院转载）
- Note: "Persistence wins them over" has no research support, while detention and no-contact orders are in black and white. The record also touches civil-service exams, vetting, and some licenses. The line between pursuit and harassment isn't your intention — it's whether they clearly refused.

### 3. Judge interest by behavior, not "signals": who initiates, who plans, who follows up
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free, little time. The hard part: swapping habits — record facts instead of decoding expressions and tone.
- In plain terms: 52 stranger pairs chatted 10-odd minutes and judged whether the other flirted. "Not flirting" was judged accurately; "flirting" was missed. Same result with 261 people rating videos. So skip expression-reading: watch who initiates, who plans the next date, who follows up when you stop pushing.
- Benefit: Two studies measured flirtation-detection accuracy. In the first, 52 previously unacquainted mixed-sex pairs talked 10–12 minutes, each reporting own and perceived flirting — "interactions without flirting were judged more accurately; flirting interactions less so." The second used 26 one-minute videos rated by 261 observers, same conclusion. People reliably detect non-flirting but miss flirting
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Hall JA, Xing C, Brooks S (2015). Accurately detecting flirting: error management theory, the traditional sexual script, and flirting base rate. Communication Research, 42(7), 939-958. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0093650214534972>；Perilloux C, Kurzban R (2015). Do men overperceive women's sexual interest? Psychological Science, 26(1), 70-77. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614555727>（争议方）
- Note: Contested: other work holds that men don't overperceive women's interest — women underreport it on questionnaires. Who judges better remains open. Both camps agree: inferring minds from micro-expressions and tone is low-accuracy; visible behavior — initiating, planning next dates, following up unbidden — is far more reliable.

### 4. Whether the relationship is good is mainly about how you feel inside it, not the other party's specs
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free, little time. The hard part: shifting perspective — shelve the checklist, watch the relationship itself.
- In plain terms: What best tells you whether the relationship is good is your own experience, not their specs: do they feel committed and appreciative, do you fight often. Your own feelings outpredict your partner's reports 2–4×. But whether it improves or decays — no questionnaire computes that.
- Benefit: A machine-learning analysis across 29 labs and 43 longitudinal dyadic datasets (longitudinal meaning the same couples followed over time). The strongest predictors of relationship quality were all the person's own perceptions of the relationship: perceived commitment, appreciation, sexual satisfaction, perceived partner satisfaction, conflict. Among individual traits, the strongest were life satisfaction, negative affect, depression, avoidant and anxious attachment. Relationship perceptions explained up to 45% of variance at intake (variance meaning couple-to-couple quality difference) and up to 18% at study end. Self-reports outpredicted partner-reports 2–4×; beyond one's own relationship perceptions, adding individual differences and partner reports bought zero additional prediction
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Joel S, Eastwick PW, Allison CJ, et al. (2020). Machine learning uncovers the most robust self-report predictors of relationship quality across 43 longitudinal couples studies. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 117(32), 19061-19071. <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1917036117>
- Note: The same study found whether relationships improved or deteriorated was essentially unpredictable from any self-report set. So judging the current relationship: ask your own experience, not their checklist — but don't expect to forecast its future. Relationship quality and physical health: item 17.

### 5. To grow closer, alternate through a set of progressively deeper questions — skip the pickup scripts
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=small metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Takes 45 minutes.
- In plain terms: Two strangers alternating through 36 progressively deeper questions ended the 45 minutes clearly closer than pairs small-talking equally long. What works is alternating truth-telling, layer by layer — not scripts. It shows closeness then and there, not a long-term relationship.
- Benefit: Paired strangers alternated through three question sets, 36 total, from "who would you most want to dinner" to "when did you last cry in front of someone." Post-session closeness significantly exceeded small-talk controls of equal length — and exceeded the average rating students gave their closest real relationships
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Aron A, Melinat E, Aron EN, Vallone RD, Bator RJ (1997). The experimental generation of interpersonal closeness: a procedure and some preliminary findings. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, 23(4), 363-377. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167297234003>
- Note: One experiment, students, short-term closeness only — no long-term development measured. The closeness came from two people alternately going deeper with true things about themselves, not scripts or one-sided performance. In conversation: "I share something true, then ask one equally deep" — alternating.

### 6. Long-distance is no worse than same-city; the real cliff is the first three months after moving in
<!-- costs: money=high time=high will=yes benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Travel money and travel time; fewer meetings than same-city. The hard part: sustaining the separation.
- In plain terms: Long-distance runs more stable while apart — absence idealizes. The real cliff follows the move. In tracking data, about half of long-distance couples reached co-location, the other half ended during separation; of the reunited half, a third broke up within 3 months.
- Benefit: Two studies agree: long-distance relationships are more stable than same-city ones while apart, because idealization of the partner runs markedly higher (measured as idealization distortion, romantic love, relationship memories, perceived similarity) — the less frequent the meetings, the rosier the picture. But the stability holds only while apart; once same-city, long-distance couples break up more readily. The long-distance-to-cohabitation tracking study: roughly half reached moving in together, half ended during separation; of the reunited half, one-third broke up within 3 months. Longer gaps and stronger idealization during separation predicted post-reunion instability
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Stafford L, Merolla AJ, Castle JD (2006). When long-distance dating partners become geographically close. Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 23(6), 901-919. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0265407506070472>；Stafford L, Merolla AJ (2007). Idealization, reunions, and stability in long-distance dating relationships. Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 24(1), 37-54. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0265407507072578>；Dargie E et al. (2015). Journal of Sex & Marital Therapy, 41(2), 181-202. <https://doi.org/10.1080/0092623X.2013.864367>（备注里那项异地研究）
- Note: US college dating relationships, not marriages, and older data — don't transplant the absolute rates. Another study surveyed 717 long-distance and 425 same-city: quality differences were small, with the two individuals and the relationship itself mattering most. So don't cite "long-distance always fails" — prepare for the post-reunion months, when autonomy shrinks, knowledge deepens, and conflict and jealousy rise.

### 7. Read the registration data before listening to elders: what the marriage and divorce rates actually measure
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=small metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Minutes reading the marriage-registration section of the civil-affairs annual bulletin.
- In plain terms: 2024: 6.106 million marriage registrations, down a fifth year-on-year; 3.513 million divorces. The internet's "divorce rate over half" divides these two numbers — about 57.5%. But the divorcing and the marrying aren't the same people; it doesn't mean half of marriages end. The bulletin's marriage rate 4.3‰ and divorce rate 2.5‰ divide by the whole population.
- Benefit: 2024: 6.106 million marriage registrations, −20.5% year-on-year, marriage rate 4.3‰; 3.513 million divorces — 2.622 million registered at civil affairs, 891,000 by court judgment or mediation — divorce rate 2.5‰. The bulletin defines marriage (divorce) rate = that year's marriages (divorces) ÷ that year's average total population × 1000‰ — a "crude rate," denominator the whole population, not the married one
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 民政部 (2025). 2024 年民政事业发展统计公报（五（二）1 婚姻登记服务、注释 5），网页版与 PDF 版. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n1288/n1294/n1554/c1662004999980006190/content.html>, <https://www.mca.gov.cn/gdnps/n2445/n2451/n2458/n2681/c1662004999980006189/attr/400985.pdf>
- Note: The internet's "divorce rate over 50%" divides that year's divorces by that year's marriages, 351.3 / 610.6 ≈ 57.5% — the "divorce-to-marriage ratio." The two numbers aren't the same cohort, and the bulletin carries no such indicator, so it can't be read as "half of marriages end." Computing "the probability a given marriage eventually divorces" requires following one marriage cohort; no such official data was found.

### 8. Count marriage's health benefits — but discount them as observational
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=small metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free, no time. Remember one thing: these numbers say married people die less — co-occurrence, not marriage causing longevity.
- In plain terms: Married people run about 12% lower mortality than the unmarried; never-married run about 24% higher than married. Co-occurrence only, not causation: healthy, high-income, social people both marry more and live longer. The numbers are mostly Euro-American — magnitude reference only domestically.
- Benefit: An elderly meta-analysis pooled 53 comparisons, 250,000-odd people: married versus unmarried all-cause mortality RR 0.88, 95% CI 0.85–0.91 (RR risk ratio, CI confidence range) — about 12% lower. In the same analysis: widowed 1.11, divorced or separated 1.16, never-married 1.11 — about 11%, 16%, 11% higher. No sex difference. The most conservative estimate ran RR 0.94, just 6% lower. Another meta-analysis compared never-married with married across 95 papers, 500-million-odd people: HR 1.24 (1.19–1.30), about 24% higher, shrinking with age. A third, split by sex — 21 studies, 7.89 million-odd: unmarried status co-occurred with all-cause, cancer, and cardiovascular mortality in both sexes, with larger male gaps in cardiovascular and all-cause death; unmarried men versus unmarried women ran 31% more stroke death and 9% more all-cause death
- Evidence grade: A (disputed)
- Sources: Manzoli L, et al. (2007). Marital status and mortality in the elderly: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Soc Sci Med 64:77–94. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2006.08.031>；Roelfs DJ, et al. (2011). The rising relative risk of mortality for singles: meta-analysis and meta-regression. Am J Epidemiol 174(4):379–389. <https://doi.org/10.1093/aje/kwr111>；Wang Y, et al. (2020). Sex differences in the association between marital status and the risk of cardiovascular, cancer, and all-cause mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of 7,881,040 individuals. Glob Health Res Policy 5:4. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s41256-020-00133-8>
- Note: The dispute: married people are selected — healthy, wealthy, social people both marry more and live longer. All three meta-analyses use observational data, tracking only, no assignment; the authors themselves flag publication bias and method limits. Roelfs found the more rigorous the study, the smaller the gap. Mostly Euro-American samples — magnitude reference only.

### 9. The time account runs on "unpaid labor": settle the division before registering
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. An hour or two before marriage to negotiate unpaid work — chores, elder and child care, whose career yields — into an explicit division, then execute it. The hard part: laying it on the table.
- In plain terms: In the national time-use survey, unpaid work — chores and care — averaged 1 hour 52 minutes daily for men and 3 hours 29 for women. The survey doesn't split by marital status, so "how much housework marriage adds" has no official number; the gender gap serves only as an upper-bound reference. Your division can be negotiated into anything before the certificate.
- Benefit: The 2024 national time-use survey. Unpaid labor means chores and family care. Those doing it that day averaged 2 hours 45 minutes — men 1:52, women 3:29. Participation that day: 67.5% men, 83.9% women. Housework averaged 1:17 daily (1:59 among doers), down 28 minutes from 2018. Family care averaged 30 minutes (1:46 among doers). The 2018 figures: housework 45 minutes men, 2:06 women; child care 17 minutes men, 53 women
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家统计局 (2024). 第三次全国时间利用调查公报（第二号、第三号）. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202410/t20241031_1957216.html>, <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202410/t20241031_1957215.html>；国家统计局 (2019). 2018 年全国时间利用调查公报. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202302/t20230203_1900224.html>
- Note: Both surveys group by sex, urban-rural, and age — not marital status — so "how much housework marriage adds" has no official figure; the gender gap is the upper-bound proxy. The numbers are national averages; the two of you can negotiate anything else.

### 10. The money account starts with legal defaults, then decide on a written agreement
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Read the property articles of the Civil Code's marriage-family book. If needed, sign a written property agreement — the law requires writing only, not notarization. The hard part: talking money in advance.
- In plain terms: Without a written agreement, the law defaults post-marriage wages, bonuses, business and investment returns, and inherited or gifted property to joint ownership. Premarital property, and bequests or gifts expressly to one side, stay personal. A written agreement splitting everything binds both. The spouse contributing more through child care and elder care may claim compensation at divorce.
- Benefit: The defaults absent agreement: post-marriage wages, bonuses, labor remuneration, business and investment income, intellectual-property income, and inherited or gifted property are joint (Article 1062). Premarital property, personal-injury compensation, property bequeathed or gifted expressly to one side, and one side's personal-use items are personal (Article 1063). Couples may agree in writing on separate, joint, or mixed ownership — binding (Article 1065). The party bearing more duty through child-rearing, elder care, or assisting the other's work may claim compensation at divorce (Article 1088)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人民代表大会 (2020). 中华人民共和国民法典（2021 年 1 月 1 日起施行，第一千零六十二、一千零六十三、一千零六十五、一千零八十八条）. 国家法律法规数据库 <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?title=%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E5%85%B1%E5%92%8C%E5%9B%BD%E6%B0%91%E6%B3%95%E5%85%B8&id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>；条文全文见最高人民法院公报转载 <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7f184078694d811fb3314f6af9accf.html>
- Note: No official statistics on betrothal gifts, weddings, or marital homes — no numbers here. Article 1088's compensation is negotiated first, court-set on failure, no fixed formula. Written property agreements serve both sides: the higher earner and the heavier unpaid contributor alike.

### 11. Parental home contributions: write loan-versus-gift clearly at the moment of transfer
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. On the day, one page stating whether the money gifts or lends, and to whom, signed by parents and child; a loan has both spouses sign, with the same sentence in the transfer memo.
- In plain terms: Premarital parental contributions default to gifts to their own child. Post-marriage contributions without agreement run as joint property. From February 2025, post-marriage full contributions by one side's parents without an only-to-our-child clause let courts award the home to the contributing side's child at divorce, then decide compensation. To count as a loan, write it that day with both signatures.
- Benefit: Premarital parental contributions count as gifts to their own child, save express gifts to both. Post-marriage contributions follow agreement; absent or unclear agreement, joint-property principles apply. From 2025-02-01 there is more: where one side's parents fully funded during marriage and the gift contract doesn't state only-to-our-child, divorce courts may award the home to the contributor's child, then weigh compensation for the other side by cohabitation and children, fault, family contribution, and market price at divorce. Partial funding by one side or funding by both: award based on contribution sources and proportions, with reasonable compensation (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 最高人民法院 (2020). 关于适用《民法典》婚姻家庭编的解释（一）（法释〔2020〕22 号，第二十九条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282071.html>；最高人民法院 (2025). 关于适用《民法典》婚姻家庭编的解释（二）（法释〔2025〕1 号，第八条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/452771.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千零六十二条第一款第四项）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Note: Clarity serves both sides: contributing parents needn't count on producing a retroactive IOU at divorce; under Interpretation II, whose money it was is itself the basis — whose name is on the deed no longer decides. The receiving side also learns what the money counts as before divorce. A loan must be a real loan: agreed, repayment terms set, memo written, repayment records kept where needed — miss a piece and you'll explain it in court.

### 12. A spouse's large borrowings, unsigned and unratified by you, don't automatically become your debt
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Never sign "co-borrower" or "guarantor" on a spouse's papers, and never write "we'll repay this" in chat — it can read as ratification. The hard part: staying silent under collection pressure.
- In plain terms: Money a spouse borrows behind your back, clearly beyond household daily needs, unsigned and unratified, isn't automatically yours. The creditor must prove the money went to joint life or business. But home-purchase money that truly became the jointly occupied home often clears that bar.
- Benefit: Joint marital debts are: both signatures; after-the-fact ratification; or one party borrowing within household daily needs in their own name. One party's borrowing clearly beyond daily needs isn't joint — unless the creditor proves use for joint life, joint business, or mutual acknowledgment. Home-purchase money is clearly beyond daily needs, with the burden on the creditor; but money that became the jointly lived-in or jointly owned home often proves "joint-life use." Hence item 11's write-it-at-transfer step matters more (national, since 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2020 年第二次修正). 关于审理民间借贷案件适用法律若干问题的规定（第十五、十六条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/94b6623974526df7d2430a3c73f050.html>（最高人民法院公报）
- Note: When the other side's parents suddenly produce a loan agreement signed only by your spouse at divorce, two steps. First, whether the loan happened at all: your claim of no funds delivered, coherently reasoned, gets weighed against amounts, delivery method, both sides' means, transaction habits, asset movements, and witnesses; a transfer voucher alone still leaves the lender proving the loan. Second, if the loan stands, whether it's joint. IOUs and guarantees generally: Section 8, item 18.

### 13. Registering takes an ID card plus a signed declaration — no household register, no fee, certificate on the spot
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=small metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Both present in person; usually under an hour.
- In plain terms: From May 2025, registering needs two things: your ID card, and a signed declaration of having no spouse and no lineal or within-three-generation collateral relation with the other party. No household register, no trip to a registration-place hometown. Free of charge, certificate on the spot when qualified.
- Benefit: The new marriage-registration regulation effective 2025-05-10 changed the required documents to exactly two: "(1) the person's resident ID card; (2) a signed declaration that the person has no spouse and no lineal or within-three-generation collateral blood relation with the other party." The household register is no longer required. Registration authorities are "county-level people's governments' civil affairs departments or township people's governments determined by province-level governments for convenience." The old "at a party's habitual-registration residence" restriction is gone. The regulation also states registration "may not charge fees" and that qualified parties "shall be registered on the spot and issued marriage certificates"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 婚姻登记条例（国令第 804 号，第二、六、八、十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202504/content_7017752.htm>
- Note: Only four things block registration: under-age, not fully voluntary, an existing spouse, or prohibited blood relation. Only peripheral services — photos, copies — may charge. Registration itself is free: refuse and report any "production" or "rush" fees. Divorce's 30-day cooling-off: this section's exit-cost entry.

### 14. One premarital check before marriage; major illness must be truthfully disclosed before registration
<!-- costs: money=low time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free or reduced for age-eligible people in most regions; self-pay runs a few hundred yuan; half a day.
- In plain terms: Concealing major illness before registration lets the other side petition the court to annul the marriage — within 1 year of learning. The check itself hasn't been a registration prerequisite since 2003; attendance is voluntary. Fees follow local policy; half a day.
- Benefit: The legal consequence is explicit: the new registration regulation states "a party suffering major illness shall truthfully inform the other before marriage registration; failing which, the other party may petition the people's court to annul the marriage under Civil Code Article 1053." The state positions premarital checks as the first gate of birth-defect prevention, with current targets "premarital examination rate and pre-pregnancy eugenics check coverage of target populations sustained above 70% and 80%," requiring co-locating check institutions with registration offices and promoting "one-stop" registration-check-guidance services. The Civil Code: a party with major illness shall truthfully inform before registration; failing which, the other may petition annulment — within 1 year of knowing or should-have-known
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千零五十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；国务院 (2025). 婚姻登记条例（国令第 804 号，第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202504/content_7017752.htm>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2023). 出生缺陷防治能力提升计划（2023—2027 年）（国卫办妇幼发〔2023〕9 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202308/content_6900320.htm>；国家卫生健康委等五部门 (2020). 关于加强婚前保健工作的通知（国卫妇幼函〔2020〕205 号）：「积极倡导各地结合实际推行免费婚前检查」. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-05/22/content_5513902.htm>
- Note: Since 2003 the check hasn't been a registration prerequisite — voluntary. But concealment gives the other side annulment rights within the 1-year window. No national free-check mandate: the 2020 five-department notice only "actively advocates" locality-based free checks. Whether free and what's included is local — ask the maternal-child health institution or the one-stop registration window.

### 15. Compute the exit cost: consensual divorce has a 30-day cooling-off; litigated divorce has statutory grounds
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Before marriage, read once: if exit comes, how long and through what. The hard part: thinking about ending it before beginning it.
- In plain terms: Consensual divorce isn't sign-and-done. Within 30 days of application, either party may withdraw; after the window, both must collect the certificate in person within another 30 days — miss it and it's withdrawn. Disagreement means litigation: mediation first, judgment only on grounds like violence, bigamy, or two years' separation.
- Benefit: Consensual divorce requires a written agreement and in-person application (Article 1076). Within 30 days of the authority's receipt, either party may withdraw; after expiry, both must apply for the divorce certificate in person within 30 days — unclaimed means withdrawn (Article 1077). With disagreement, litigation: courts mediate first; bigamy or cohabitation, domestic violence or abuse and abandonment, incorrigible gambling or drugs, or separation for 2 years due to estrangement — failed mediation means divorce granted; separation of another year after a denial means granted on re-filing (Article 1079). In 2024, of 3.513 million divorces, 891,000 went through court judgment or mediation
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人民代表大会 (2020). 中华人民共和国民法典（第一千零七十六、一千零七十七、一千零七十九条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7f184078694d811fb3314f6af9accf.html>；民政部 (2020). 关于贯彻落实《中华人民共和国民法典》中有关婚姻登记规定的通知（民发〔2020〕116 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-12/04/content_5567010.htm>；民政部 (2025). 2024 年民政事业发展统计公报. 题录同第 7 条（结婚率、离婚率各是什么口径）
- Note: The cooling-off applies to consensual divorce only; litigation has none but runs longer. Filing and attorney fees vary — no numbers here. High exit costs don't argue against marriage, but you should know them before it.

### 16. Keep a separate account for "marrying for the elders" — don't merge it with your own
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Two columns on paper: which benefits of this marriage are yours (computable from the earlier items), and which are the elders' — reassurance, face, the end of nagging. The hard part: leaving their column alone instead of merging it into yours.
- In plain terms: The elders' benefit is real — but it doesn't convert into your health or relationship quality, while the time, money, and exit-cost accounts are all yours to bear. Two columns on paper, and the task-marriage's value becomes visible at a glance.
- Benefit: The elders' gains are real. But they don't transfer into your health (item 8) or relationship quality (item 4 — your own felt experience). The time account (item 9), money accounts (items 10–12), and exit costs (item 15) all land on the parties. Split the ledger, and whether "task marriage" pays becomes self-evident
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，方法类建议；数据部分见本节前面各条
- Note: The elders' column may legitimately weigh heavily — the weighing is yours. The fill-in checklist: [docs/结婚划不划算.md](../docs/结婚划不划算.md)

### 17. Treat relationship quality as a health account: fight about the matter, not the person — no insults, no sarcasm
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=small metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. In fights, address only the matter at hand — no insults, sarcasm, or old scores. The hard part: mouth control while angry.
- In plain terms: Good marriages run with better health and lower mortality — small associations, roughly the size of diet's. In one experiment, couples fighting with steady hostility healed experimental wounds at only 60% the speed of mild couples.
- Benefit: Pooling 126 studies, 72,000-odd people (meta-analysis): higher marriage quality tracked better health, correlations r between .07 and .21 (0 meaning none, 1 complete lockstep). Lower mortality risk: r = .11; smaller cardiovascular reactivity during conflict: r = −.13. The authors: these effects are small — comparable to dietary habits. In the crossover experiment, 42 healthy couples were hospitalized twice for 24 hours each — once for supportive conversation, once discussing a disagreement; after conflict, blister wounds healed slower. Couples hostile on both occasions healed at only 60% the rate of low-hostility couples, and their next-morning inflammatory markers (IL-6, tumor necrosis factor) rose higher
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Robles TF, Slatcher RB, Trombello JM, McGinn MM (2014). Marital quality and health: a meta-analytic review. Psychological Bulletin. <https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031859>；Kiecolt-Glaser JK et al. (2005). Hostile marital interactions, proinflammatory cytokine production, and wound healing. Archives of General Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1001/archpsyc.62.12.1377>
- Note: Why B: mostly tracking studies without assignment — unsortable whether bad marriage sickens or sickness sours marriage. Some studies held after adjusting for age and income; Robles flags publication bias in parts. Sex-comparing studies found essentially no differences. No pooled analysis comparing "bad marriage" against "unmarried" mortality was found — "bad marriage beats no marriage" stays inference. The wound experiment: 42 couples. "Matter-not-person, no insults or sarcasm" is the author's derivation from the hostile-behavior findings, never directly compared. Stuck in fight-cold-war cycles: item 20 (couples therapy). Violence or chronic verbal abuse isn't a communication problem — Section 8, item 43. Exit costs: item 15.


### 18. Same-sex couples: while both are lucid, arrange powers of attorney, guardianship agreements, and wills — legally you're not close relatives; without papers, no signing rights and no inheritance
<!-- costs: money=low time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Guardianship agreements and wills at a notary run from a few hundred yuan locally; a handwritten will is free. A half-day or two. The hard part: sitting down to talk illness and death.
- In plain terms: Statutory close relatives are spouses, parents, children, siblings — partners aren't among them. Without documents, a partner can't decide for an incapacitated one, and inherits nothing at death. Three papers cover the stages: power of attorney while competent, agreed guardianship after incapacity, a will afterward.
- Benefit: Civil Code Article 1045 lists close relatives as spouses, parents, children, siblings, grandparents, and grandchildren — partners absent. Article 1127's statutory heirs: first spouses, children, parents; then siblings, grandparents — partners absent again. Article 1219 covers surgery and special examination and treatment: where the patient can't be informed or shouldn't be, medical staff inform the patient's "close relatives" and take consent. Three paths patch it: Article 33 lets adults agree in writing in advance with "other individuals willing to serve as guardians" — on incapacity, that person becomes guardian, i.e., statutory agent. Article 165 requires written powers of attorney naming the agent, matters, powers, and term, signed or sealed — terminated by death. Article 1133 permits willing property by will to persons outside the statutory heirs. An article by the Yichang judicial bureau, carried on the Hubei justice-department site, confirms same-sex partners can notarize guardianship agreements naming each other (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第二十三、三十三、一百六十五、一百七十三、一千零四十五、一千一百二十四、一千一百二十七、一千一百三十三至一千一百四十、一千一百四十二、一千二百一十九条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>（国家法律法规数据库）；宜昌市司法局 (2021). 意定监护公证：通向彩虹的公证之路. 湖北省司法厅网站「实践荟萃」栏目. <https://sft.hubei.gov.cn/fbjd/sjhc/202101/t20210108_3208602.shtml>
- Note: A partner takes as "legatee," not heir: acceptance must be declared within 60 days of learning, silence forfeiting. The partner and interested parties can't witness the will. Multiple wills: the last governs — Section 17, item 2. Whether agreed guardians can sign surgical consent varies by hospital — ask yours in advance. Family contesting means litigation: 6+ months first instance, attorney fees yours — hence notarize where possible. Jointly bought property and savings get their own written agreements by true contribution. Arranging guardianship: Section 17, item 1. Beneficiaries: you and your partner.

### 19. Don't marry by concealing orientation, and don't treat a marriage of convenience as the easy way out: legally it's a real marriage, exit only by divorce
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=yes benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: years of withstanding family pressure.
- In plain terms: Orientation-concealing marriage is legally neither void nor voidable — exit means divorce. A marriage of convenience is likewise real: post-marriage earnings and debts count as spousal. The deceived party may seek greater property share and emotional damages at divorce — court support unsettled.
- Benefit: Article 1051: marriages are void only for bigamy, prohibited kinship, or under-age. Articles 1052–1053: voidable only for coercion or concealed major illness. Interpretation I, Article 17: other grounds for voidness are dismissed. Exit therefore means divorce. A registered convenience marriage is lawful: Article 1062 makes post-marriage wages and remuneration joint; Article 1064 makes co-signed and daily-need debts joint. Divorce damages under Article 1091 cover bigamy, cohabitation with another, domestic violence, abuse or abandonment, and "other grave fault"; Interpretation I, Article 2 confines "cohabitation with another" to "extra-marital opposite-sex" cohabitation — same-sex relations can only argue "other grave fault." Damages include emotional harm, claimed together with the divorce petition; denied divorce means denied damages. No published post-Code judgments were found. Under the old law, a 2013 Jiangsu High Court site article discussed a wife suing over her husband's concealed homosexuality, listing two views: one holding homosexuality outside the statutory faults, supporting neither greater share nor damages; the author endorsing the other — treating the deceived party as "without fault," favoring her in property division and granting emotional solatium (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千零五十一至一千零五十四、一千零六十二、一千零六十四、一千零七十九、一千零八十七、一千零九十一条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>（国家法律法规数据库）；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》婚姻家庭编的解释（一）（法释〔2020〕22 号，第二、十七、八十六至九十条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff80808177e75f880178006736f21aaf>；朱来宽、王洪娟 (2013). 因同性恋导致离婚能否主张损害赔偿？江苏法院网. <http://www.jsfy.gov.cn/article/26621.html>
- Note: The greater-share and damages points rest on one analytical article without the case's outcome — actual recovery depends on the court. The Code's "other grave fault" addition gives the deceived party a hook. Statutes are Grade A; the outcomes aren't settled. Where both convenience-marriage partners have same-sex partners, those partnerships lack marriage-law protection — property written separately: item 18. Convenience divorces run the same 30-day cooling-off or litigation: item 15. Waiving damages in a consensual divorce bars later claims. Beneficiary: first the spouse who would be deceived, then you — discovery brings divorce, property division, and child custody at once.

### 20. Stuck in repeat fights and cold wars: go to couples therapy together — waiting rarely fixes it
<!-- costs: money=high time=medium will=yes benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Paid. Per-session fees vary by place and hospital — no numbers here. Studies run weekly; one trial ran about 8 months end to end. The hard part: both attending and finishing.
- In plain terms: Stuck couples completing couples therapy end clearly more satisfied — about 70% improve. Waitlisted couples barely change. Effects fade: by year 5, about half still clearly improved and a quarter separated or divorced.
- Benefit: A 2020 meta-analysis pooled 58 studies, 2,092 couples. Completing couples therapy moved relationship satisfaction g = 1.12 (0.92–1.31) — a large effect by the authors' reading (g is effect size; see the README glossary). Waitlisted couples over the same period: g = 0.12 (−0.04 to 0.29) — nothing. Worse starting relationships improved more. A 2012 review: couples therapy improves roughly 70% of treated couples. A randomized trial took 134 long-term, severely distressed couples through about 8 months of two behavioral couple therapies: end-of-treatment satisfaction d = 0.90 and 0.71; by year 5, 50.0% and 45.9% retained "clinically significant improvement," while 25.7% and 27.9% had separated or divorced
- Evidence grade: A (disputed)
- Sources: Roddy MK, Walsh LM, Rothman K, Hatch SG, Doss BD (2020). Meta-analysis of couple therapy: effects across outcomes, designs, timeframes, and other moderators. Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology, 88(7), 583-596. <https://doi.org/10.1037/ccp0000514>；Lebow JL, Chambers AL, Christensen A, Johnson SM (2012). Research on the treatment of couple distress. Journal of Marital and Family Therapy, 38(1), 145-168. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1752-0606.2011.00249.x>；Christensen A, Atkins DC, Baucom B, Yi J (2010). Marital status and satisfaction five years following a randomized clinical trial comparing traditional versus integrative behavioral couple therapy. Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology, 78(2), 225-235. <https://doi.org/10.1037/a0018132>；Rathgeber M, Bürkner PC, Schiller EM, Holling H (2019). The efficacy of emotionally focused couples therapy and behavioral couples therapy: a meta-analysis. Journal of Marital and Family Therapy, 45(3), 447-463. <https://doi.org/10.1111/jmft.12336>（争议方）；Shadish WR, Baldwin SA (2003). Meta-analysis of MFT interventions. Journal of Marital and Family Therapy, 29(4), 547-570. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1752-0606.2003.tb01694.x>（争议方）；Karakurt G, Whiting K, van Esch C, Bolen SD, Calabrese JR (2016). Couples therapy for intimate partner violence: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Marital and Family Therapy, 42(4), 567-583. <https://doi.org/10.1111/jmft.12178>；全国人大常委会 (2018 修正). 精神卫生法（第二十三、五十一条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7448a066d>（国家法律法规数据库）
- Note: The dispute is durability: a randomized-only meta-analysis found end-of-treatment g = 0.60, 0.44 at six months; at 12 months only behavioral couple therapy held data, g = 0.06 — nearly gone. A review of 20 meta-analyses: clinically meaningful effects in 40%–50%. "70% improve" and "40–50% clearly improve" use different bars — don't mix. The 5-year trial had no no-treatment arm, so it can't say whether divorce rose or fell. The 2020 pool covered different-sex couples only; no Chinese randomized trial was found — discount accordingly. Where to go domestically: the Mental Health Law requires psychotherapy inside medical institutions, and counselors may not perform it — ask hospital psychiatry and psychology departments about couples therapy; outside institutions, check credentials and training first. With domestic violence, don't start jointly: the 2016 meta-analysis confines its conclusions to mild-to-moderate, mutually conflictual violence — control-driven or severe violence is excluded, and practitioners fear post-session retaliation. Being hit: Section 8, item 43 first. How to speak in fights: item 17; whether to leave: item 15. Beneficiaries: you and your spouse.
