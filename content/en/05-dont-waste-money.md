# 5. Don't waste money

This section counts only money: what gets saved, how annual returns differ, what a sum returns on long average. None of it converts into lifespan or time. Investment entries only show how to run the numbers, using verified historical data only — none of it is investment advice.

Entries in this section are grouped by theme, with item numbers in parentheses.

**Daily spending**: turn off auto-renewal (item 1), re-shop your phone and broadband plans (item 4), decline electronics extended warranties (item 11), choose drugs that won volume-based procurement (item 12), drink boiled tap water (item 14), pay per gym visit (item 21), cooling-off period for big purchases (item 23), don't stockpile for the strikethrough price (item 24).

**Impulse spending and kids' spending**: no lottery tickets (item 5), no tipping streamers (item 8), kids' recharges and tips can be reclaimed (item 9), anyone asking your child for a verification code is a scammer (item 10), count bracelets and designer toys at money spent (item 33), jewelry and jade only with a CMA report (item 34), randomized sales like blind boxes and card pulls (item 35).

**Online shopping and rights enforcement**: written contracts for prepayment (item 22), know the platform rules when shopping online (item 29), things bought in livestreams that go wrong (item 30), ten times the price for unsafe food (item 31), check inspections and certifications before big purchases (item 32), buy prepackaged if you want to read the ingredient list (item 36).

**Tax, housing fund, insurance (social)**: annual tax reconciliation (item 2), housing fund withdrawable for rent and renovation (item 3), family sharing on medical insurance (item 13), personal pension account (item 20), if self-paying employee pension insurance, pay the lowest tier (item 46).

**Saving, borrowing, investing**: walk away at "high returns" (item 6), convert loans to annualized rates (item 7), don't trade stocks frequently (item 15), never borrow to invest (item 16), broad index funds as the base (item 17), choose low-fee funds (item 18), don't bet everything on one place (item 19), emergency fund first (item 27), compare before prepaying the mortgage (item 28), sell losing stocks by rule (item 37), US stocks via QDII or Stock Connect (item 38), no crypto (item 45).

**Insurance**: prefer pure protection insurance (item 25), buy enough third-party liability (item 26), insure only losses you can't absorb (item 39), term life for the earner first (item 40), cancel within the free-look period (item 41), tell deposits from insurance at the bank counter (item 42), no "surrender agents" (item 43), name a specific beneficiary (item 44).

### 1. Turn off all auto-renewal; renew manually at expiry
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Go through the auto-debit lists in Alipay, WeChat, Apple, and Android — 10–20 minutes once. From then on, every renewal costs you one extra tap.
- In plain terms: What you save is the subscriptions you stopped using long ago that still charge monthly. Going through the lists takes under 20 minutes. By regulation, merchants must conspicuously remind you before charging and give you an always-available, easy-to-find, easy-to-tap cancellation option.
- Benefit: What you save equals the sum of subscriptions you no longer use but still pay for — different for everyone, no standard number. Regulators require merchants to remind you conspicuously before renewal and to offer "simple, anytime cancellation," free of charge (China, effective 2021)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 国家市场监督管理总局 (2021, 2025 修正). 网络交易监督管理办法（总局令第 37 号，第十八条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2025/art_4b47c79b8d994a42bba4835997688faa.html>
- Note: For services you genuinely use constantly, annual billing usually beats monthly — keeping auto-renewal on is actually rational there. What to kill are the ones you've forgotten that keep charging.

### 2. Do the annual individual income tax reconciliation between March and June, and fill in every special deduction you qualify for
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Done in the "Individual Income Tax" app in a dozen-odd minutes.
- In plain terms: These people usually get money back: anyone who worked only part of the year, changed jobs mid-year, or never filled in deductions for children's education, mortgage, rent, or supporting the elderly. A dozen-odd minutes on your phone. If your annual comprehensive income is under 120,000 yuan, or the tax you'd owe is under 400 yuan, you may skip it.
- Benefit: Wages, labor remuneration, authorship royalties, and licensing fees are settled in one combined account: overpayments refunded, shortfalls paid. Two situations exempt you: annual comprehensive income under 120,000 yuan, or additional tax under 400 yuan — per the State Taxation Administration's 2023 announcement, valid through 2027-12-31. Conversely, refunds usually go to: those with income only part of the year, job changers mid-year, and those with unfilled deductions for children's education, mortgage, rent, elder support, and the like
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家税务总局 (2025). 个人所得税综合所得汇算清缴管理办法（第六条）及其解读. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202502/content_7006465.htm>；财政部、税务总局 (2023). 关于延续实施个人所得税综合所得汇算清缴有关政策的公告. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202308/content_6900594.htm>
- Note: Those who owe must also file — skipping is illegal. There are seven special additional deductions: children's education, continuing education, serious-illness medical, home-loan interest, rent, elder support, and infant care under 3. Mortgage and rent are mutually exclusive — fill one, not both

### 3. The housing provident fund isn't only for buying: rent, renovation, and property fees are withdrawable; new regulations from September 20, 2026
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One trip to file the withdrawal.
- In plain terms: The housing fund isn't untouchable until you buy. The new regulations list nine withdrawal situations, including rent, renovating your own home, and property fees. Once you apply, the fund center must answer within 3 days.
- Benefit: The amended regulations list nine withdrawal situations, including: paying rent, renovating your own home, and paying property fees on your own home. The fund center must decide within 3 days of accepting your application

- Note: The money in your account is your own salary plus the employer's match. Left sitting, it earns only nominal interest. The regulations also state that on death or declared death, heirs and legatees may withdraw the balance. Required documents and procedures vary by local center — check its service guide before going.

### 4. Re-shop your mobile and broadband plans yearly; downgrade what you don't use; if the carrier won't switch you, complain
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One bill check, a dozen-odd minutes. The hard part is holding firm against the retention script.
- In plain terms: Your old plan is often a few dozen yuan more expensive than current ones. A few dozen a month is a few hundred a year thrown away. By regulation, carriers may not refuse your number portability without legitimate cause, may not drag their feet, and may not set up special tariffs just for porting customers. If they stall, complain.
- Benefit: MIIT rules state carriers may not "refuse, obstruct, or delay number-portability service without legitimate cause," may not "use interception, restriction, or other technical means to degrade service quality for ported users," and may not "set exclusive tariff or marketing schemes for porting users." Old plans often cost a few dozen yuan more than current ones — staying put wastes a few hundred yearly
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工业和信息化部 (2019). 携号转网服务管理规定（工信部信管〔2019〕242 号）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-11/13/content_5451483.htm>
- Note: First check six months of actual data and minutes in the carrier's app, then pick a plan for that usage. If the agent says "downgrades need a store visit" or "you're in a contract term," demand the written basis. None produced? Call 12300 and complain

### 5. Don't buy lottery tickets
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Not buying is the saving.
- In plain terms: By regulation, lotto and numeric games must return at least 50% of sales as prizes; the Double Color Ball returns 51%. So every 1 yuan you put in returns 0.51 on long average — a steady 49% loss. In 2024, China sold 623.486 billion yuan of lottery; 161.031 billion went straight to public-welfare funds, outside the prize pool.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Finance sets minimum prize ratios by game type: lotto and numeric 50%, scratch cards 65%, sports betting 73%. The Double Color Ball allocates 51% of sales to prizes — of every 100 yuan sold, only 51 go to prizes. For you: every 1 yuan invested returns 0.51 on long average, losing 49%. 2024 national lottery sales were 623.486 billion yuan, of which 161.031 billion went to public-welfare funds, never returning to buyers (China, 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 财政部 (2015). 关于规范和加强彩票资金构成比例管理的通知（财综〔2015〕94 号）. <http://m.mof.gov.cn/czxw/201511/t20151113_1560814.htm>；财政部 (2025). 财政部公告 2025 年第 8 号（2024 年彩票公益金筹集分配情况）. <http://zhs.mof.gov.cn/zhengcefabu/202508/t20250828_3970977.htm>；中国福利彩票双色球游戏规则（第十四条）. <https://mzj.nc.gov.cn/ncsmzj/flcp/202111/5dccc1a5da89434e94a62f8ea0e5887e.shtml>
- Note: The long-run loss is built into the system — luck has nothing to do with it. Buy if you like, but only as 2-yuan entertainment, never as investing.

### 6. Walk away the moment you hear "high returns," "capital guaranteed," "steady gains"
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Just pause when the words appear.
- In plain terms: The regulator's own words: question anything above 6% returns, treat 8%+ as dangerous, and above 10% prepare to lose all principal. Legitimate wealth products are no longer allowed to promise principal or returns. Money lost to illegal fundraising is, by rule, your own loss — nobody backstops you.
- Benefit: The then-banking-regulator chairman said it at the 2018 Lujiazui Forum: "question returns above 6%, above 8% is very dangerous, above 10% be prepared to lose all principal." The asset-management rules require institutions to "not promise principal protection or returns" and to "not advance funds in any form when redemption difficulties arise" — so no legitimate product promises safety anymore. Losses from illegal fundraising "are borne by the participants themselves"; when it blows up, no one compensates you (China, 2018, 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 郭树清 (2018). 防范化解金融风险 奋力跨越重大关口（第十届陆家嘴论坛讲话，国家金融监督管理总局官网）：「在打击非法集资过程中，努力通过多种方式让人民群众认识到，高收益意味着高风险，收益率超过6%的就要打问号，超过8%的就很危险，10%以上就要准备损失全部本金」. <https://www.nfra.gov.cn/cn/view/pages/ItemDetail.html?docId=182418>；天津市地方金融管理局转载. <https://jrgz.tj.gov.cn/ztlm/ztzl/jrjfwphjr/202006/t20200625_2744709.html>；中国人民银行等 (2018). 关于规范金融机构资产管理业务的指导意见（银发〔2018〕106 号，第二条、第六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2018/content_5323101.htm>；国务院 (2021). 防范和处置非法集资条例（国务院令第 737 号，第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5588815.htm>
- Note: The 6/8/10 lines are a regulator's rules of thumb, not statute. The quote was about illegal fundraising — high-return products and investment firms. Not investment advice.

### 7. Skip credit-card minimum payments; no installments or consumer loans for spending. If you must borrow, convert everything to annualized rates before comparing
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Keep enough cash to pay in full before the statement date. The hard part is resisting installments that spread out big purchases. When borrowing is unavoidable, spend a few minutes getting each loan's annualized rate.
- In plain terms: Minimum payment is a daily-interest loan running about 18% a year — and using it voids your grace period. Installments quoting "0.6% monthly fee" are about 13.8% annually, not 7.2% — because the principal shrinks monthly while the fee keeps charging on the original amount. To compare loan costs, look only at the annualized rate, never the daily rate or monthly fee.
- Benefit: Credit-card overdraft rates used to have caps: a "daily rate of 0.05%" ceiling, floor at 0.7× that. 0.05% daily annualizes to 18.25% simple — interest not compounding into principal. Since 2021, pricing is negotiated between issuer and cardholder. Once you use the minimum payment, the unpaid principal accrues daily interest in full, and the grace period is voided. The central bank requires issuers to display the annualized rate prominently and "not display only the daily rate or daily payment amount." Since 2021, all lenders must show the annualized rate prominently in ads and contracts; daily and monthly rates may appear alongside but not more conspicuously. The annualized rate must include interest and all directly related fees. For amortizing loans, the money you actually use is what remains after each repayment. The PBOC's worked example: borrow 100,000 yuan over 12 months, paying 0.5% of the original principal monthly as installment fees, plus a 1,000-yuan service charge upfront. Simple interest: about 12.80% annualized. Compounded (IRR method): about 13.58%. By the nominal fee math you'd think you paid 0.5% × 12 + 1% = 7% — actually nearly double, because the principal shrinks monthly while fees keep charging on 100,000. Same arithmetic on a 12-installment card plan at 0.6% per period, no other fees: nominally 7.2% a year, but about 13.8% annualized — roughly 1.9× nominal; across 6–36 installments it's 1.8–2×. Loans from individuals or ordinary companies are private lending: rates exceeding 4× the one-year LPR at signing get no court support for the excess. The one-year LPR on 2026-08-20 was 3.0%, so 4× = 12%. Interest deducted upfront counts the actually-received amount as principal. This cap doesn't bind licensed banks and consumer-finance companies, nor seven classes of local financial organizations (small-loan, pawn, leasing, etc.) — so don't apply 12% to them; read the annualized rate on the contract (China, 2020, 2021)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 中国人民银行 (2020). 关于推进信用卡透支利率市场化改革的通知（银发〔2020〕327 号）. <https://xining.pbc.gov.cn/zhengwugongkai/4081330/4406346/4693549/4159909/index.html>；中国人民银行 (2021). 中国人民银行公告〔2021〕第 3 号（第一、三、四条，附件《采用内部收益率法计算贷款年化利率示例》第三例）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-03/31/content_5597156.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 年第二次修正). 关于审理民间借贷案件适用法律若干问题的规定（第一、二十五、二十六条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/94b6623974526df7d2430a3c73f050.html>（最高人民法院公报）；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于新民间借贷司法解释适用范围问题的批复（法释〔2020〕27 号）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/285171.html>；全国银行间同业拆借中心受中国人民银行授权 (2026). 2026 年 8 月 20 日贷款市场报价利率（LPR）公告. <https://www.chinamoney.com.cn/chinese/rdgz/20260820/3399885.html>
- Note: 18.25% is 0.05% × 365 straight, no compounding — rolling monthly would be higher. Some banks discount; your statement's stated annualized rate governs. When truly short, borrow from cheaper legitimate channels rather than rolling minimum payments. Comparing loans: ask for the compounded (IRR) annualized rate; if they quote simple interest, regulation requires it be labeled "simple," and simple reads lower than compounded. The 13.8% and 1.8–2× figures are this book's own arithmetic per the PBOC method, shown in the verification record. For loan sharks, see Section 7, item 15 (no loan-shark borrowing).

### 8. No tipping streamers, no game top-ups, no impulse orders
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Three actions in minutes: disable password-free payment, set per-transaction and daily limits, remove bound payment methods. The hard part is not tapping in the moment.
- In plain terms: Chinese gamers spend about 480 yuan per person per year on games — spent, essentially unrecoverable. The law lets only minors' un-consented spending be refunded; adults have no such provision. The cheapest defense is set up in advance: no password-free payment, limits, unbound payment methods.
- Benefit: This is a large outlay. 2024 domestic gaming revenue: 325.783 billion yuan across 674 million users — about 480 yuan per head (dividing those two figures). Money spent is essentially unrecoverable. One exception: tips and top-ups by persons with limited civil capacity (here, minors) without guardian consent — "the people's court shall support" the guardian's demand for refund. Adult tips and top-ups have no such rule. Separately, minors may play online games only on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays, and statutory holidays, 20:00–21:00 (China, 2020, 2021)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 国家新闻出版署 (2024). 今年中国游戏市场收入超 3257 亿元创新高（中国音数协游戏工委《2024 年中国游戏产业报告》）. <https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/ywdt/202412/t20241216_877445.html>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于依法妥善审理涉新冠肺炎疫情民事案件若干问题的指导意见（二）（法发〔2020〕17 号，第 9 条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/230181.html>；国家新闻出版署 (2021). 关于进一步严格管理 切实防止未成年人沉迷网络游戏的通知（国新出发〔2021〕14 号）. <https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/tzgs/202108/t20210830_666285.html>
- Note: Grade B because no one has directly measured "money saved by not tipping" — only industry aggregates. Disabling password-free payment and setting limits are practice-based, Grade C alone. The Civil Code articles 19 and 145 are quoted verbatim in item 9 (kids' top-ups and tips). Not investment advice.

### 9. If your child recharged or tipped from your phone, large spending by a child over eight can be reclaimed without parental ratification
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. It takes time to gather evidence and negotiate with the platform — which usually refuses first, refunds later. Failing that, litigation runs months minimum, with your own attorney fees. So assembling the file and going through the platform and 12315 is the cheaper route. The hard part: the child won't talk; you must ask patiently.
- In plain terms: Money spent by a child over eight, when the amount is clearly disproportionate to their age and intelligence, and the parent neither consented nor ratifies — that transaction is void and refundable. Under eight, children can't commit binding spending at all. A parent who stays silent is legally deemed to refuse ratification.
- Benefit: Minors over eight have limited civil capacity: they may independently do only acts fitting their age and intelligence. Acts beyond that are valid only with the legal representative's consent or ratification — the parent's nod beforehand, or acceptance afterward. Under eight, a minor has no civil capacity; their affairs are transacted by the legal representative. Silence counts as refusal: a parent who never responds is deemed to reject the deal
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典. 最高人民检察院转载全文. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> 第十九条：「八周岁以上的未成年人为限制民事行为能力人，实施民事法律行为由其法定代理人代理或者经其法定代理人同意、追认；但是，可以独立实施纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其年龄、智力相适应的民事法律行为。」第二十条：「不满八周岁的未成年人为无民事行为能力人，由其法定代理人代理实施民事法律行为。」第一百四十五条：「限制民事行为能力人实施的纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其年龄、智力、精神健康状况相适应的民事法律行为有效；实施的其他民事法律行为经法定代理人同意或者追认后有效。」「法定代理人未作表示的，视为拒绝追认。」
- Note: The key is proof: you must show it was the child operating the phone, not you. Useful materials: charges clustered during school or parents' work hours; device usage records; the child's chat and game records; the child's own statement. "Fitting age and intelligence" has no fixed money line — a few dozen yuan for a skin is not tens of thousands in tips. Prevention is cheaper: cap password-free payments, enable youth mode, never share the payment password. For your own impulse spending, see this section's "no streamer tips, no game top-ups, no impulse orders."

### 10. Teach your child one sentence: anyone who tells you to use your parents' phone or read out a verification code is a scammer
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Walk the child through the six scam types below, and agree on one line: "tell me the day it happens — I won't scold you." Also cap password-free payments, enable youth mode, keep the payment password secret. The hard part is not erupting when it does happen.
- In plain terms: The six scam types differ, but the last step is always the same action: getting the child to transfer from a parent's phone or read out a verification code. So one sentence covers them all: whoever tells you to touch your parents' phone or read codes is a scammer. Add: "tell me first; I won't scold you." A child who dares speak the same day gives the money a chance of recovery.
- Benefit: Quanzhou's anti-fraud center, with the city education bureau, dissected six scams aimed at teenagers. They differ in setup but share the final step: making the child use a parent's phone to transfer. Type 1: cheap private trades of game accounts and gear. One boy privately sent 260 yuan; the other side claimed "minors transferring violates rules — the account will be frozen"; he tapped the "verification link" on his father's phone and 30,000-odd yuan left the account. Type 2: "removing anti-addiction locks." Threats that "without paying to cancel, your parents will go to jail" — a child transferred 7,000 yuan from the mother's phone. The notice states anti-addiction locks are official and "there is no private unlock channel." Type 3: tip refunds. Scammers pose as "official customer service," claim minors' tips are refundable, then demand "gifts to a designated livestream room via the parent's Alipay to process the refund" — 73,000 yuan tipped from the mother's account, then blocked. "Anyone adding you to 'refund via gifting' is 100% a scam." Type 4: impersonating classmates or teachers. Hijacked accounts send voice messages with real names to borrow money; at term start, scammers join class group chats as the teacher posting "school fee 500 yuan," same avatar and nickname, with shills replying "paid." Type 5: celebrity-chasing. The child is pulled into a "star fan group, 888-yuan red envelope," asked to send 48.88 yuan to "verify," then threatened: "a minor broke rules — the platform will freeze 80,000 and your parents will go to jail." Total taken: 16,000 yuan. "Real police and lawyers never contact minors online for investigation, let alone ask for parents' phones." Type 6: secondhand platforms with cheap listings to lure people off-platform for private transfers. The same notice lists three red lines for kids themselves: never lend, sell, or rent bank or phone cards — cards used to launder money can constitute the crime of assisting information-network criminal activity, leaving a record that affects graduate exams and civil service; "phone-relay" part-time jobs are illegal; overseas "high-pay recruitment" can mean being controlled on arrival (national, October 2025)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 泉州市反诈骗中心 (2025). 开学第一课｜反诈是青少年的必修课，这 6 大陷阱一定要避开！<http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202510/t20251028_7026836.htm>（福建省公安厅转载）
- Note: Grade B because the official material dissects scripts and cases, without incidence rates or prevention-effect statistics. Distinguish from item 9 (kids' top-ups): money the child spent on tips and recharges can be reclaimed under the Civil Code; money scammed away doesn't qualify — that's Section 8, item 2 (report and freeze). Recovery depends on whether the money is still in the account. The core threat — "you broke the rules, your parents will go to jail" — is itself the scam's signature; telling the child in advance beats interrogating after. Actually honor "tell me, no scolding": fear of scolding and concealment is the common thread that let these losses snowball. The adult versions, including AI face-swaps, are Section 8, items 3–4. For adults too: never do verification codes or face scans for anyone — Section 8, item 45.

### 11. Decline extended warranties on electronics
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The price is paying out of pocket for a post-warranty repair — a low-probability event.
- In plain terms: Extended warranties sell for 10%–50% of the original price, at margins some 18 times ordinary goods — that markup is your overpayment. Main components like washer and fridge motors carry a state-mandated 3-year warranty anyway. For a very cheap warranty, divide its price by the cost of one repair; the result is the break-even failure probability.
- Benefit: Warranties typically price at 10%–50% of the item's original price. ### 44. Name a specific beneficiary on the policy — and remember to claim: 5 years for life insurance, 2 for others
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Fill once at application; changes need written notice to the insurer.
- In plain terms: No beneficiary named, or the beneficiary dies first, makes the proceeds an estate — creditors of the deceased get paid before family. "Wife, so-and-so" followed by divorce counts as unnamed at claim time. Family must know the policy exists: life insurance claims within 5 years of learning of the death, other insurance within 2.
- Benefit: The Insurance Law: beneficiaries are designated by the insured or policyholder (with the insured's consent). Three situations make proceeds part of the estate, distributed by succession rules: no beneficiary named or designation unclear; the beneficiary predeceases with no other; the beneficiary legally loses or waives the right with no other. When beneficiary and insured die in the same event with no order determinable, the beneficiary is presumed to have died first. Estate shares first settle the deceased's taxes and debts. The Supreme Court's interpretation: designating "name plus relationship" that no longer holds at claim time counts as unnamed. Beneficiary changes not notified to the insurer don't bind it. Limitation periods: 5 years for life insurance, 2 for others, from knowing or should have known of the insured event. Coverage typically runs hundreds of thousands — hence "large" (national, 2015 amendment, since 2021)

### 46. Self-paying the employee pension without an employer: the lowest tier plus full years is the best value; it's not instant profit — even the lowest tier takes about 10 years of benefits to break even
<!-- costs: money=high time=low will=yes benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: All yours — 20% of the contribution base, the lowest tier running a few hundred to a thousand-plus yuan monthly. Fifteen-plus years required, rising gradually toward 20 from 2030. Unwithdrawable before retirement.
- In plain terms: For flexible workers paying their own employee pension, lowest tier plus enough years is the best value. The bulk of the benefit tracks local average wages at retirement. At then-current wages, 15 years at the lowest tier with benefits from 60 breaks even in about 10 years; extra tiers above the minimum take about 17. Worth paying — not instant profit.
- Benefit: The 2005 State Council decision sets the mechanics. The self-paying (officially "flexibly employed") pay 20% of the base, 8% credited to the personal account. Since 2019 the base is self-chosen, 60%–300% of the province's all-in urban average wage. The pension has two parts. The basic pension: local prior-year average wage plus your average contribution wage, halved, times 1% per year contributed. The personal-account pension: account balance divided by the divisor — 139 at retirement age 60, 170 at 55; later retirement, smaller divisor. What follows is the author's own arithmetic, not official numbers. Assume local monthly average wage of 8,000 yuan at retirement, both averages equal; amounts at then-current wages, personal-account interest assumed to exactly track wage growth. Lowest tier, 60%: base 4,800, monthly payment 960; 15 years totals about 173,000 yuan (exactly 172,800). From 60: about 1,457 monthly — basic 960 plus personal-account about 497. About 17,500 yearly (about 17,487), breaking even in about 9.9 years — age 70 or so. At the 100% tier: 15 years totals about 288,000 yuan, paying about 2,029 monthly. The extra 115,200 yuan (exactly) buys only about 572 more monthly — about 16.8 years to break even. Reason: half the basic pension follows the local average wage regardless of your contribution — light contributors are subsidized. Each extra year at the lowest tier keeps break-even at about 9.9 years — beating tier upgrades. At the lowest tier, the basic pension dominates the benefit. Break-even is independent of local wage level: high-wage regions raise contributions and benefits proportionally. Early-year contributions were nominally small but benefits pay at then-current wages, so nominal break-even beats 9.9 years. The law: 15 cumulative years at retirement to draw monthly; short, you may continue contributing. From 2030-01-01, the minimum rises 6 months yearly toward 20. On death, the personal-account balance is inheritable. The state adjusts pensions up with wage growth and prices; shortfalls are subsidized by government (national)
