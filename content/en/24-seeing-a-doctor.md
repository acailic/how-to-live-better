# 24. Seeing the doctor: less money, fewer detours

Metrics: money and time. This section covers the care path itself — which hospital first, how to move up, where the money saves. Plus emergency triage, and the assessments and certificates after serious injury. What benefits you can claim: Section 7. Chronic disease long-term: Section 16. On-scene emergencies: Section 13.

The primary basis is the State Council's April 2026 measures on tiered care — tiering by severity to decide which hospital level first — which ties "start at the base" directly to savings. Local rollout varies; the text states the national rules, and your pooling area's insurance and health authorities govern locally. The pooling area is your enrollment locality, usually a city.

### 1. Common illnesses start at the community clinic; referral up the ladder keeps the deductible counting once
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free, one community visit first. The hard part: resisting the direct specialist booking.
- In plain terms: Community first, then community-referred hospital admission counts your out-of-pocket deductible once. Direct tertiary admission later stepping down to community rehab may pay it twice. Downward transfers skip re-deductibles within the same disease cycle.
- Benefit: Insured patients referred level-by-level through base institutions get continuous inpatient-deductible counting at upper hospitals — the deductible being the upfront stretch before insurance begins; continuous meaning the two hospitals share one counting, no second outlay. Patients stepped down from upper hospitals carry no new deductible within the same disease cycle
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「统筹地区内经基层医疗卫生机构逐级转诊的参保患者，在上级医院的住院起付线可连续计算；由上级医院下转至基层医疗卫生机构的住院患者，同一疾病周期内不再另设住院起付线。」
- Note: Direct tertiary admission with later step-down may pay the deductible twice; referral counts once. Referral rules sit with provinces, cities, and medical alliances — ask the community first. Stable chronic disease belongs nowhere near big-hospital refills: those clinics shrink while communities dispense 12 weeks at a time — Section 16, item 5.

### 2. Within one pooling area, lower-tier hospitals reimburse more — about 10 percentage points per level
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part: not running to the big hospital.
- In plain terms: The same hospitalization bill reimburses more the lower the tier — localities must spread the tiers about ten points apart. A 10,000-yuan bill dropping from 80% to 70% coverage moves your share from 2,000 to 3,000 yuan. New insurance funds also tilt toward the base.
- Benefit: The document requires localities to spread reimbursement levels across hospital tiers "in principle, tier by tier, gaps of about 10 percentage points." By that gap, each tier up costs about 10% of the reimbursable bill extra out-of-pocket — 10,000 yuan at 80% versus 70% runs 2,000 versus 3,000; the extra 1,000 is a tenth of the total but half again on your share. Actual ratios, deductibles, and caps vary; the 10-point arithmetic is illustrative. Annual incremental funds tilt basic-ward
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「因地制宜适当拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的住院报销水平，原则上统筹地区内医疗卫生机构住院报销比例逐级拉开10个百分点左右的差距。」「具备条件的地方，可按分级诊疗导向酌情拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的门诊报销水平。」
- Note: The same bill at base versus tertiary can differ by thousands out-of-pocket. Base-manageable illnesses stay down — which isn't "everything at the community clinic."

### 3. Needing the big hospital: go through base referral or the hospital's referral center — never scalpers
<!-- costs: money=none time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free, one extra referral step.
- In plain terms: Big hospitals reserve slots and beds for base-referred patients and must run referral centers or designated departments by 2027. No slot doesn't mean no path. Scalper tickets cost more and may land you in the wrong department.
- Benefit: Lead hospitals of tight medical alliances "reserve a certain proportion of slots and beds for base institutions and promptly treat base-referred patients" — tight alliances being hospitals and community institutions bound into one cooperating group, so referrals draw on separate slots and beds away from the lobby scramble. Institutions must run referral centers or designated departments, full coverage by 2027
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「牵头医院要为基层医疗卫生机构预留一定比例的号源和床位，并及时接诊经基层转诊患者。」「医疗机构要强化转诊服务统一管理，设立转诊中心或指定固定部门承担患者转诊服务工作，到2027年实现全覆盖。」
- Note: Reserved slots exist precisely for referrals — no public slot means the referral route. Scalpers cost more and misplace you. No referral center found: call the hospital's service line.

### 4. Before cross-province care, ask locally first — necessity in principle requires associate-chief-physician-or-above assessment
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free, one question before departure.
- In plain terms: Whether to seek care elsewhere is assessed, in principle, by physicians at associate-chief rank or above in secondary and tertiary hospitals. Temporary out-of-town care reimburses differently than same-tier home care, per local rules — asking first beats discovering rejection after arrival.
- Benefit: Cross-pooling-area and cross-province necessity is assessed, in principle, by physicians of associate-chief rank or above at second- and third-tier hospitals. Temporary cross-province care reimbursement "maintains a reasonable difference" against same-tier home institutions — the same illness away returns less
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「原则上由二、三级医院副主任医师及以上职称人员评估患者跨统筹地区、跨省异地就医的必要性。」「进一步强化异地就医直接结算管理服务，跨省临时外出就医人员报销水平与参保地同级别医疗机构报销水平保持合理差异。」
- Note: "Temporary out-of-town care" and "long-term out-of-town residence" are different filings at different rates — filings and direct-settlement diseases: Section 16. Never travel first and think reimbursement after.

### 5. After every visit: keep your own copy of records, reports, and imaging
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free — hospitals may charge published copying costs. The hard part: remembering every time.
- In plain terms: Reading and copying your records is your statutory right — outpatient records, admission notes, lab slips, imaging, surgical and anesthesia records, pathology, and bills. A dedicated window handles it at cost. Changing hospitals, second opinions, commercial insurance, and disputes all run on this stack.
- Benefit: The regulations list consulting and copying as patient rights, covering outpatient records, admission notes, temperature sheets, orders, lab reports, imaging, consent forms, surgical and anesthesia records, pathology, nursing records, and costs — the full record. Hospital changes, second opinions, insurance claims, and disputes all rest on it
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号）第十六条：「患者有权查阅、复制其门诊病历、住院志、体温单、医嘱单、化验单（检验报告）、医学影像检查资料、特殊检查同意书、手术同意书、手术及麻醉记录、病理资料、护理记录、医疗费用以及国务院卫生主管部门规定的其他属于病历的全部资料。」「患者要求复制病历资料的，医疗机构应当提供复制服务，并在复制的病历资料上加盖证明印记……可以收取工本费，收费标准应当公开。」「患者死亡的，其近亲属可以依照本条例的规定，查阅、复制病历资料。」第十五条「任何单位和个人不得篡改、伪造、隐匿、毁灭或者抢夺病历资料。」<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>；国家卫生计生委、国家中医药管理局 (2013). 医疗机构病历管理规定（2013 年版）：医疗机构「应当指定部门或者专（兼）职人员负责受理复制病历资料的申请」，复制在申请人在场的情况下进行并加盖证明印记. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2600084.htm>
- Note: A dedicated window takes requests — no begging physicians. Read the copy list straight from Article 16: outpatient records, admission notes, temperature sheets, orders, labs, imaging; then surgical and anesthesia records, pathology, nursing records, and bills. File chronologically; the crown pieces are discharge summaries, operative reports, pathology, and key imaging. Chronic disease logs: Section 16.

### 6. Questioning the care: demand record sealing on the spot — both parties present, listed, each holding a copy
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free, pending both parties completing the formality. The hard part: saying it to the physician's face.
- In plain terms: Doubting the treatment lets you seal the records: both parties present, inventory listed, signed and stamped, one copy each. Records incomplete seal the completed part. Suspected infusion, transfusion, injection, or medication harm: the bag, vial, and tubing get sealed too.
- Benefit: The regulations: "sealing and unsealing of records in medical disputes shall proceed with both parties present," originals or copies held by the institution; incomplete records seal the completed portion first. Institutions list the sealed items, both parties sign or stamp, each holding a copy. For suspected infusion, transfusion, injection, or medication harm, the parties jointly seal and unseal the physical items; testing goes by joint commission to qualified agencies, or by the county health authority's designation where joint commission fails; suspected transfusion harm brings the blood station's personnel. Hard constraint: "no unit or individual may tamper with, forge, conceal, destroy, or seize medical records." Disputes resolved or 3 years unclaimed let the institution unseal (national; since 2018-10-01)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第十五、二十四、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Note: Sealing and copying are different acts — copying is routine (item 5); sealing fixes evidence against later revision. Suspected infusion or medication harm seals the bag, vial, and tubing with the records — never let them be carried off. Institutions must post dispute channels and run unified complaint departments — take it there, not to the physician on shift.

### 7. Acute and serious: straight to emergency triage, never the registration queue
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free, a few extra steps to the triage desk. State the deadliest symptom in one sentence.
- In plain terms: Emergency ranks by severity in four grades, graded at the entrance triage desk, not registration. Untriaged means unranked in the system — however grave, you shuffle with the queue. Lead with the most dangerous: major bleeding, breathlessness, unconsciousness, impalement, crushing chest pain.
- Benefit: The 2024 emergency quality indicators grade triage into four levels: "I critical — immediate treatment"; "II emergent — assessment and treatment concurrently"; "III urgent — treatment within a short time"; "IV non-urgent or sub-acute — routine order." Beijing's standard sets the responses: level 1 "immediate assessment and treatment, resuscitation or rescue room"; level 2 "immediate vital-sign monitoring, treatment within 10 minutes, rescue room"; level 3 "priority over level 4, general area, reassessment past 30 minutes' wait"; level 4 "routine order, reassessment past 4 hours." Triage "adheres to critical-first," with the triage area conspicuously at the emergency entrance (four-level grading national; response times Beijing's)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 关于印发急诊医学等 6 个专业医疗质量控制指标（2024 年版）的通知（国卫办医政函〔2024〕150 号）附件 1，指标四「急诊分级分诊执行率」. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202405/content_6951260.htm>；北京市卫生健康委员会 (2019). 北京市加强急诊预检分诊分级工作方案及附件《北京市医院急诊预检分诊分级标准（试行）》：「坚持急危重症优先就诊原则。按照患者病情的严重程度，遵循从重到轻、从病情迅速变化到相对稳定的原则，合理安排患者就诊顺序。」「在急诊科入口的显著位置，设置急诊预检分诊区域」. <http://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/ylws/201912/t20191216_1242338.html>
- Note: The four grades are national; per-grade timings are provincial — Beijing's is the verbatim-checkable version. Grading reads pulse, pressure, oxygen, and symptoms, not your felt pain. Level-1 hard markers: heart rate above 180 or below 40; systolic pressure under 70 (the higher number); oxygen under 80%; shock, confirmed infarction, acute consciousness disorders. Feeling under-graded: state the deadliest symptom again; deterioration in the queue means immediate re-triage. Truly critical: 120 beats self-transport — pre-hospital physicians grade on the same scale and pre-notify the hospital (Section 13).

### 8. No money, no ID, no name — emergency treats you first anyway
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: The disease-emergency-assistance fund pays first aid for two classes: the unidentifiable and the unpaying. Hospitals may not refuse or delay rescue, and ambulance services may not decline over money. So no enduring at home broke, and no skipping the ambulance for fear of bills. The fund covers first aid only — later hospitalization runs on insurance and medical assistance.
- Benefit: The system covers "patients suffering acute, severe, and critical illness or injury in China needing emergency care whose identity is unclear or who cannot pay." The fund pays "emergency-care costs of patients whose identity cannot be determined" and "outstanding emergency-care costs of identified patients unable to pay." "Medical institutions and their staff must promptly and effectively rescue acute, severe, and critical patients, and may not refuse, shirk, or delay treatment for any reason." Pre-hospital: the national emergency number 120; centers "may not refuse, shirk, or delay pre-hospital services for dispatch reasons" nor "for cost reasons"; transport follows "nearest, most urgent, professionally sufficient, and patient preference considered" (national; since 2013)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2013). 关于建立疾病应急救助制度的指导意见（国办发〔2013〕15 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>；国家卫生和计划生育委员会 (2014). 院前医疗急救管理办法（委令第 3 号，第十三、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Note: The fund pays only the first-aid stretch, after treatment, applied for by the institution — you never collect at a window. The insured still run insurance first; provincial procedures and caps vary. Transport ranks "nearest, most urgent" ahead of preference — ambulances needn't take you to your chosen hospital; the nearest capable one wins when critical. Remaining burdens: medical assistance — Section 7, item 10.

### 9. Disability assessment waits until treatment concludes — too early under-grades
<!-- costs: money=low time=medium will=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Assessment fees run hundreds to 2,000 yuan, advanced by the applicant; waiting for treatment conclusion or stable effect — typically 3–6 months post-injury, sometimes longer. The hard part: waiting while still healing.
- In plain terms: Compensation rides on the grade; the grade rides on residual impairment after treatment. The standard mandates assessment "after treatment concludes or clinical effect stabilizes." Assessing with bones unhealed or hardware in place grades that interim state — depressing your own grade. Ordinary fractures healing without functional loss grade nothing.
- Benefit: The Human Injury Disability Grading sets timing "after treatment of the primary injury and its directly associated complications concludes or clinical effect stabilizes," grading disability in 10 levels — level 1 (100% disability rate) to level 10 (10%), each level 10 points apart — judged by structural damage, functional impairment, and dependence on care and nursing, "with appropriate consideration of social-interaction and psychological factors due to disability." Level-10 fracture clauses include "one vertebral compression fracture (compression reaching 1/3) or comminuted fracture," "two-plus pelvic fractures or comminuted fracture with malunion," and "any major limb joint (ankle excepted) losing 25%-plus function." Same-site same-nature disabilities never double-count; multiple disabilities list grades separately (national; since 2017-01-01)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部 (2016). 关于发布《人体损伤致残程度分级》的公告（2016 年 4 月 18 日发布，2017 年 1 月 1 日施行），标准第 4.2、4.4、4.5、5.10.6、6.2 条. <https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/zwxxgk/fdzdgknr/fdzdgknrtzwj/201908/P020210316701681025917.pdf>
- Note: Three disability systems never interchange: assault, traffic, and personal injury run judicial appraisal under the Grading (computing disability compensation — Section 9); work injury runs labor-capacity appraisal under its own ten grades (Section 19); disability policy benefits require the separate disability certificate (item 10). Assemble records, operative notes, and follow-up imaging beforehand (item 5) — missing imaging is the commonest grade-depressor. Old conditions mixing with new injury: the opinion must state the injury's share of the disability — a line that directly moves the money.

### 10. Lasting impairment after treatment: apply for the disability certificate at the county disabled persons' federation of your registration
<!-- costs: money=low time=medium will=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: The certificate itself is free; assessment and photo fees are yours in principle, waivable for hardship. Bring ID, household register, and three 2-inch white-background photos to the county federation; passing assessment publishes 5 working days in the village or community, with the federation reviewing within 10 working days. The hard part: trips and waiting.
- In plain terms: The certificate gates disability benefits — it isn't an injury proof. Seven categories — visual, hearing, speech, physical, intellectual, mental, multiple — each graded 1–4. Process: application, acceptance, designated-agency assessment, publication, review, issuance; valid ten years, renewed free. Failure is failure — connections don't grade.
- Benefit: The certificate "is the legal credential certifying disability category and grade, and the important basis for disabled persons to enjoy state and local preferential policies," graded under the national standard GB/T 26341-2010. Issuance runs voluntary-application, domicile-managed; county federations accept and issue on provincial designated hospitals' assessment conclusions; passing conclusions publish 5 working days with review within 10; "no certificate cost," assessment and photo costs "in principle self-borne; conditional localities may subsidize; special hardship gets coordinated reduction." Valid ten years, free renewal; category or grade changes support re-assessment; disagreements go to the municipal federation within 10 working days, then the provincial expert committee concludes (national; since 2018-01-01)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中国残疾人联合会、国家卫生和计划生育委员会 (2017). 中华人民共和国残疾人证管理办法（2017 年 5 月 27 日发布，2018 年 1 月 1 日施行；第二、三、五、七、九、十七、十九、二十二、二十五条）. <https://www.bdpf.org.cn/cms68/web1459/subject/n1/n1459/n1508/n1509/n1512/c68465/content.html>
- Note: Do the math first: the certificate pays nothing directly — it connects the two disability subsidies (Section 7, item 8: living subsidy for dibao-household members, nursing subsidy for grade 1–2 with long-term care needs) and local rehabilitation, assistive-device, tax, employment, and transit benefits, all provincially varied — check the local federation's site before running the trips. It substitutes neither for judicial disability grading nor work-injury appraisal (item 9). Personal use only; lapsed-status certificates cancel with a one-year re-application bar; multiple disabilities grade by the heaviest category.

### 11. Thanking the doctor who saved you: thank-you letters, banners, and satisfaction surveys — never red envelopes; the code bans valuables, not gratitude
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One letter or one survey, a dozen-odd minutes.
- In plain terms: Money upfront buys no priority — slots, beds, scarce drugs, and surgical scheduling are all barred from conversion. Thanking afterward is fine, but cigarettes, liquor, and envelopes put the physician in violation. Three thanks actually land: a serious satisfaction survey, a named letter or banner, and keeping follow-up appointments.
- Benefit: The Nine Codes of Clean Practice (2021), item 7: "maintain treatment order, do not undermine care equity... strictly prohibited: converting slots, beds, scarce drugs and consumables, or examination and surgical arrangements into benefits." Item 8: "build harmonious relations, accept no 'red envelopes'... strictly prohibited: soliciting or accepting gifts, cash, shopping cards, securities, equity, or other financial products from patients and their families," and "strictly prohibited: attending banquets, travel, fitness, or entertainment arranged or paid by the patient side." Institutions must enforce, supervise, and "resolutely punish violations." Satisfaction surveys feed tertiary public hospitals' performance evaluation (the 2019 State Council opinion): the indicator system spans "medical quality, operating efficiency, sustained development, and satisfaction evaluation," hospital satisfaction comprising patient and staff satisfaction, measured through outpatient, inpatient, and staff surveys, on a nationally unified platform — by March 2019 all tertiary public hospitals entered the national survey platform with results in performance evaluation. Hospitals must also manage complaints (the 2019 Complaint Measures): second-tier-plus institutions run patient-relations offices "uniformly receiving complaints, investigating and verifying, proposing handling, and promptly replying," with staff encouraged to collect opinions (national)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委、国家医保局、国家中医药局 (2021). 关于印发医疗机构工作人员廉洁从业九项准则的通知（国卫医发〔2021〕41 号）. 国务院公报 <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5678098.htm>；国务院办公厅 (2019). 关于加强三级公立医院绩效考核工作的意见（国办发〔2019〕4 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-01/30/content_5362266.htm>；国家卫生健康委 (2019). 医疗机构投诉管理办法（委令第 3 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-10/08/content_5436976.htm>
- Note: The beneficiaries: you and the person who saved you. Wanting to thank after an all-night rescue is human — only the form changes. Envelopes and liquor transfer risk to the recipient; letters, banners, and surveys are the institutionally recognized forms. A returned gift isn't disdain — it's compliance. Separate the two: physician red envelopes run under industry discipline; gifts to case handlers are criminal bribery — Section 8, item 40. Letters and banners are widely accepted, though "entering personal and department records" is practitioner lore, not written rule; the documented piece is survey results in tertiary performance evaluation. Doubting the care itself: seal records (item 6) and keep copies (item 5).

### 12. Before dental implants: ask what the bundled price includes, prefer institutions pledging price governance, distrust low-price ads
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One check of the institution list on the NHSA's public account plus an in-person price question, a dozen-odd minutes.
- In plain terms: After the 2023 price campaign, a routine implant's total cost fell from an average 15,000 to 6,000–7,000 yuan. Tertiary public hospitals cap single-implant service fees at 4,500 yuan in principle, fixtures and crowns separate. Implants, dentures, and orthodontics stay unreimbursed; fillings and extractions are locally covered.
- Benefit: The NHSA's January 2024 notice: a routine single-implant total comprises three parts. Service fees bundle "consultation, lab and imaging, implant placement, crown placement, and scan-design modeling — excluding extraction, periodontal scaling, root-canal treatment, bone grafting, and soft-tissue grafts," with tertiary public hospitals capped "in principle at 4,500 yuan" (developed regions up to 20% looser per the 2022 campaign notice). Fixtures: January 2023 national procurement, "winning prices 548–1,855 yuan, averaging 900-odd" — Sichuan's lead announcement: premium imports from a 5,000-yuan median to about 1,850, top-volume brands from about 1,500 to about 770. Crowns: "100–656 yuan, averaging 300-odd." The NHSA's H2-2023 briefing: measures landed by early May, "total implant costs falling from an average of 15,000 to about 6,000–7,000 yuan."
