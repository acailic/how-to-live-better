# 32. Study abroad: status, work, insurance, and home-country recognition

Two accounts — money and personal freedom. No debate over going, no school rankings. Four things cost you if unknown: when visa status breaks, the work-hour caps, which insurance and fees are mandatory, and whether the diploma will be recognized at home.

Covers the US, Canada, the UK, and Australia, figures country by country, sourced from immigration authorities and the Ministry of Education. **These policies change faster than Chinese regulations do. All figures below are current to September 2026 — re-check every source link before departure and before every renewal.**

Embassies, 12308, and overseas medical insurance: Section 21. Study-versus-work: Section 23. Stolen accounts: Section 14; telecom fraud: Section 8. None repeated.

### 1. Before paying tuition: check the school against CSCSE's accredited-institution list
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. One lookup at CSCSE's "accredited institutions" page, minutes.
- In plain terms: For the diploma to count at home — civil service, state enterprises, hukou, professional titles — it must clear accreditation by the Ministry of Education's Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (CSCSE). Schools outside the accreditable list mean tuition and years wasted. The lookup is free; do it before paying.
- Benefit: CSCSE maintains an accredited-institution query — searchable by country or name — covering whether the school's degrees fall within accreditation. The Ministry's cross-border education supervision site additionally publishes Sino-foreign cooperative-program supervision
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 认证院校查询. <http://yxcx.cscse.edu.cn/>；教育部教育涉外监管信息网. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>
- Note: An agent's "Ministry-recognized" means nothing — your own lookup governs. Lists shift: in at enrollment doesn't guarantee in at graduation, so re-check yearly. Cooperative programs carry their own registration regime; post-2008 entrants verify their registration number by name and ID on the supervision site.

### 2. Watch the US F-1 rulemaking lawsuit: "four-year cap, 30 days to leave" was set for 2026-09-15 and suspended the day before — for now it's still "duration of status"
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Copy the I-20 program-end date into a calendar with a 90-day alarm — the I-20 being the school-issued eligibility document. Each semester, check the international-student office's notices for case developments.
- In plain terms: The US tried to cap F-1 at four years with 30 days to depart after finishing; a federal court suspended it the day before effect. For now the old rule holds: stay for the duration of study, 60 days to prepare departure after completion. Suspension isn't repeal — the government can appeal, and the rule can return any time.
- Benefit: DHS published the rule on 2026-07-17, effective 2026-09-15: F-1 admissions shift from duration-of-status to fixed terms capped at four years, extensions applied for separately; 30 days to depart after study or practical training. On 2026-09-14, Judge Saylor of the federal district court in Massachusetts postponed the rule's effect under APA §705 — nationwide, the whole rule. The court didn't vacate it (vacatur denied without prejudice); the case continues. Under the court's order, F, J, and I entrants still enter duration-of-status, the 60-day grace period holds, and no separate extension applications are required
- Evidence grade: A

U.S. Department of Homeland Security, No. 1:26-cv-13799-FDS (D. Mass. Sept. 14, 2026)，法院案卷. <https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/74661796/presidents-alliance-on-higher-education-and-immigration-v-united-states/>；Yale Office of International Students & Scholars (2026-09-14). Important Update: Court Action on the D/S Rule. <https://oiss.yale.edu/news/important-update-court-action-on-the-ds-rule>；AILA (2026). One Day Before Taking Effect, Federal Court Postpones the F, J, and I Fixed Admission Period Rule. <https://www.aila.org/blog/think-immigration-one-day-before-taking-effect-federal-court-postpones-the-f-j-and-i-fixed-admission-period-rule>；8 CFR 214.2(f). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>
- Note: Current to 2026-09-25. The government may appeal to the First Circuit, or DHS may reissue a revised rule — multi-year students shouldn't assume permanence. The court set an October 2, 2026 status conference; re-read school notices after that date. If the rule later takes effect, extensions need pre-expiry applications — nothing auto-extends. Under the current rule, early withdrawal changes the departure window; ask the international office. J visas sat in the same rule with different details — check your own category.

### 3. Work-hour caps are written red lines in all four countries — one hour over is a visa-condition violation
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Log your weekly hours. The hard part: employers saying "nobody checks extra hours" — never believe it.
- In plain terms: All four cap student work: exceeding risks visa cancellation, removal, and questions on every future application. US 20 hours weekly; UK 20 for degree-level and 10 below; Canada 24 weekly; Australia 48 per fortnight. Two jobs sum.
- Benefit: US F-1: on-campus work under 20 hours weekly during term; approved off-campus likewise 20; full-time on breaks — 8 CFR 214.2(f)(9). UK degree-level: 20 term-time hours; below-degree 10; part-time courses barred outright, self-employment and professional-sport or entertainment work barred — Appendix Student ST26.1, ST26.5. Canada eligible full-time students: 24 weekly off campus — IRPR 186(v). Australia: 48 hours per fortnight during session; research masters, doctoral students, and their dependants exempt
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 8 CFR 214.2(f)(9). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>；UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student（ST26.1、ST26.5）. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>；IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>；Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Note: US off-campus work needs prior authorization — no working while the I-765 pends. Canadian permits printed "20 hours" under the old rule still allow 24 while eligible — current regulation governs. The UK self-employment ban reaches far: freelance gigs, online shops, and self-run businesses all count.

### 4. Status's root is "full-time enrollment": leaves, withdrawal, and school-transfer gaps all kill work permission
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Before stopping classes, taking leave, or transferring, ask the international office where your status stands.
- In plain terms: Work permission tracks active full-time study. Leave, withdrawal, expulsion, or a transfer gap ends it on the spot — continuing to work is unauthorized employment. Many assume an unexpired visa means safe status; the status broke earlier.
- Benefit: Canada: no off-campus work during authorized leave, none during transfer non-study periods, resumption only with resumed studies. US: on-campus work requires Active SEVIS status — SEVIS being the student-tracking system, Active meaning validly enrolled — plus maintained full-time course loads. UK: permission follows course type; part-time courses can't work
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>；U.S. Department of Homeland Security, Study in the States. Working in the United States. <https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/students/work/working-in-the-united-states>；UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student（ST26.1）. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>
- Note: Before dropping below full-time, ask where the line sits — course-load breaks hit before work violations do. Expulsion or program termination typically cuts status the same day; never wait for the formal letter.

### 5. US: report a new address within 10 days of moving
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. One online change-of-address, ten minutes.
- In plain terms: Moving in the US means notifying immigration within 10 days by law. Not reporting isn't small: it's independently unlawful, and USCIS mail goes missing.
- Benefit: 8 CFR 265.1: registered aliens report address changes within 10 days, to USCIS by its prescribed means, covering both the change and the new address
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 8 CFR 265.1. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-265/section-265.1>；USCIS. AR-11, Alien's Change of Address Card. <https://www.uscis.gov/ar-11>
- Note: USCIS reporting doesn't update the school's SEVIS record — that's separate. The other three countries likewise require notification; deadlines and means vary — follow your host country's immigration page.

### 6. Before and during study abroad: read the Ministry of Education's study-abroad warnings
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Once before departure, once per semester.
- In plain terms: The ministry issues warnings for countries or regions where security or policy recently disadvantages Chinese students — official, published risk notices, far more reliable than agency talk. Reading costs nothing.
- Benefit: The supervision site published four warnings in 2025: No. 1 on China-negative provisions in certain US state higher-education acts (2025-04-09); Nos. 2 and 3 on frequent cases against Chinese citizens and poor study conditions in the Philippines (2025-07-18, 2025-08-30); No. 4 on Japan's security and study environment, advising caution (2025-11-16)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 教育部教育涉外监管信息网. 预警信息. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>；教育部发布 2025 年第 4 号留学预警. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2060.shtml>；第 1 号. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2001.shtml>
- Note: Warnings aren't bans — official grounds for weighing local risk. The list shifts with events; this book won't chase updates — the site's current page governs. Personal safety and consular protection: Section 21.

### 7. Australia: OSHC must cover the whole stay without gaps; arriving without proof can mean refused entry
<!-- costs: money=high time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Thousands to over ten thousand yuan, bought by visa length — years of study, years of cover.
- In plain terms: Australia writes student health insurance into visa conditions — not optional. Cover must span the entire stay; renewals can't gap; arriving without proof risks refusal at the border. Accompanying family each carry their own policy.
- Benefit: Home Affairs: student-visa applicants and family members hold Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the entire stay, from approved Australian insurers, save exemptions — no gaps between old and new policies at renewal; arriving without proof of cover risks refused entry; arriving before the course starts, the policy start date must read the actual arrival day
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Note: School-arranged policies: get the insurer, dates, and policy number — the visa application demands them. Under-buying breaks renewals; over-buy past graduation rather than risk a gap.

### 8. UK: beyond the visa fee, a yearly healthcare surcharge — students £776 annually, paid in full at application
<!-- costs: money=high time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: The visa fee runs £558 plus the surcharge at £776 yearly — both in full at application.
- In plain terms: The UK bills beyond the visa: a healthcare surcharge prepaid by visa years unlocks the National Health Service. A two-year visa pays £1,552 at once; with the visa fee, budget both before departure.
- Benefit: The student visa fee is £558 — applying from abroad, extending, or switching in-country all alike. The healthcare surcharge, prepaid at application by visa years, runs £776 yearly for students and dependants (£1,552 for a 2-year visa) and £1,035 for other categories; visas over 6 months but under a year charge a full year
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: UK Government. Student visa. <https://www.gov.uk/student-visa>；UK Government. Pay for UK healthcare as part of your immigration application. <https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application>
- Note: Amounts and exchange rates move — compute from the current pages. Degree-level students over 18 typically stay up to 5 years, below-degree 2 — estimate surcharges on those years.

### 9. Before returning: allow accreditation time — CSCSE credential verification takes 10–20 working days
<!-- costs: money=low time=medium will=no benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Online application and payment; materials plus review, about a month in working days.
- In plain terms: Foreign credentials need degree verification before home use — review alone takes 10–20 working days, longer in hiring season or on returned materials. Autumn recruitment, civil-service registration, and hukou all carry deadlines; never start when the credential's already needed.
- Benefit: Verification runs on CSCSE's online service hall: register with real-name authentication, submit application and materials, pay, await assessment. The published processing time runs 10–20 working days — excluding weekends and holidays
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 网上服务大厅·学历学位认证. <http://zwfw.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Note: Materials: the diploma, passport or travel permit, residence card or visa annotations, photo, and authorization statement. Entry-exit records are pulled system-side, so keep visa pages from your time abroad. CSCSE also announces invalidated certificates and suspended-school processing — check whether your school appears before applying.

### 10. The "enhanced review" school list grows yearly — buying a diploma no longer works
<!-- costs: money=none time=low will=no benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Before enrolling, search the school name in CSCSE's notices.
- In plain terms: CSCSE names foreign institutions for enhanced credential review and suspends some schools' processing outright. The named are typically the "no language test, no travel, master's in months" promotions. One search beats appeals later.
- Benefit: CSCSE keeps issuing enhanced-review announcements (the ninth, 2025-10-28, is the latest), alongside suspension notices and fraud alerts on credential-verification scams
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 关于对部分国外院校学历学位认证加强认证审查的公告（九）（2025-10-28）. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/cscse/sy/tzgg/2025102809225023345/index.html>；中国（教育部）留学服务中心. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Note: "Enhanced review" isn't certain denial, but demands more materials and time. Agents promising "guaranteed accreditation" or "internal expedited channels" are scams — CSCSE has issued its own fraud warning. Ghostwritten theses and proxied coursework, discovered, void credentials and can stain visa records.
